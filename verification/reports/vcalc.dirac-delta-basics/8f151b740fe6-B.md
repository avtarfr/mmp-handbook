## Findings
1. [minor] statement "The Gaussian family sifts" (line 63): $f$ is required to be "bounded and continuous at $x = 0$", with no integrability assumption, yet the conclusion is about $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f\delta_\varepsilon\,dx$. For a bounded but non-measurable $f$ that happens to be continuous at $0$, that integral does not exist and the statement has no content; Step 2 of the proof asserts absolute convergence "because $f$ is bounded and $\delta_\varepsilon$ is integrable", which needs measurability too. The conclusion is false for no $f$ for which the integrals are defined, so this is a gap in the hypotheses rather than a wrong claim. Fix: "let $f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}$ be measurable, bounded, and continuous at $x=0$" (or "locally integrable and bounded").
2. [minor] definition block (line 37): a delta family is defined as a family of **integrable** functions with $\int\delta_\varepsilon = 1$, but the third family listed three lines later, $D_T(x) = \sin Tx/\pi x$, is not Lebesgue integrable — its integral is $1$ only as an improper/principal-value integral, which the remark at line 190 acknowledges ("not integrable in absolute value on $\mathbb{R}$"). As written the definition excludes one of its own examples. Fix: "a family of locally integrable functions whose integral over $\mathbb{R}$ converges to $1$ (possibly only as an improper integral, as for the third family below)".
3. [minor] definition block (line 59): "The object $\delta^{(3)}$ is localized at a point and carries unit integrated weight whatever coordinate system is in use" tracks Arfken p. 79 closely for an `adapted` block — he writes "it describes a function localized at the origin and with unit integrated weight, irrespective of the coordinate system in use", and "unit integrated weight" and "coordinate system in use" both come over unchanged. It is one clause rather than a passage, and the rest of the block is plainly our own, so I do not call it a close paraphrase; but it is the one sentence in the unit I would rewrite. Fix: recast in our own words, e.g. "$\delta^{(3)}$ sits at one point and integrates to $1$, and both facts are independent of the coordinates used to write the volume element."
4. [minor] example "The Gaussian family sifting a cosine, exactly", step 3 (line 206): "the approximation converges, but only linearly in the width parameter". The error is $\varepsilon/4$, which is linear in $\varepsilon$ but quadratic in the Gaussian's actual width ($\propto\sqrt\varepsilon$), so the sentence is ambiguous about which quantity is meant. Fix: "the error is $\varepsilon/4$ to leading order — first order in $\varepsilon$, and so second order in the width $\sqrt\varepsilon$".
5. [minor] proof "The Laplacian of $1/r$" (line 146): labelled `original`. The route — excise a ball of radius $\varepsilon$ around the singularity and apply Green's second identity on the shell — is the route Strauss takes on p. 186 to prove his representation formula, and Strauss is not among this unit's sources. I do not think this is a mislabelling: the device is the standard one, our statement is a different one (the distributional identity tested against a compactly supported $f$, not the representation formula for a harmonic $u$), and the estimates in Steps 4 and 5 are ours. But a reader would be better served by a pointer. Fix: add a sentence to the proof or the idea block noting that the same excision is used in Strauss §7.2, p. 186, and add that page to the sources — or relabel the block `adapted` with that citation, whichever the drafter prefers.
6. [source] statement "The Gaussian family sifts" (line 71): Hassani's Eq. (7.20) states the sifting property for "a function $f$ defined on the interval $(a,b)$" with no regularity hypothesis, which is false as it stands (for the unit step both the Gaussian and the symmetric rectangular family give $\tfrac12$, as the unit's own example 1 step 4 observes). The unit cites Eq. (7.20) only for the sifting property and the $x'\notin(a,b)$ case, and supplies the correct hypothesis from Stakgold and Holst. Recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs add vcalc.dirac-delta-basics hassani-2013 "§7.3, Eq. (7.20), p. 229"`. No action needed in the unit.
