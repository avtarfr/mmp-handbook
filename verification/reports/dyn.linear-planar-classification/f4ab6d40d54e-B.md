## Findings
1. [blocking] statement, case 2 (line 80): the clause "every orbit off the fast eigenline arrives tangent to the slow eigendirection, **while its direction tends to the fast eigendirection as $t \to -\infty$**" is false as written. The two halves need different exclusions: tangency to the slow direction as $t\to+\infty$ needs $c_s \neq 0$ (orbit off the fast eigenline), but convergence of the direction to the fast eigendirection as $t\to-\infty$ needs $c_f \neq 0$ (orbit off the *slow* eigenline). Counterexample: $A = \operatorname{diag}(-1,-2)$ ($T=-3$, $D=2$, $\Delta=1>0$, a stable node; slow eigendirection $e_1$, fast $e_2$). The orbit $x(t) = (e^{-t},0)$ lies on the slow eigenline, hence is "off the fast eigenline", yet its direction is $e_1$ for every $t$, so it tends to the *slow* eigendirection as $t \to -\infty$, not the fast one. Proof Step 3 (line 125) has the same gap: "Dividing instead by $c_f e^{\lambda_f t}$ and letting $t \to -\infty$ gives convergence to $v_f$" silently requires $c_f \neq 0$, which is never stated. Fix: split the clause — "every orbit off the fast eigenline arrives tangent to the slow eigendirection; every orbit off the slow eigenline has its direction tending to the fast eigendirection as $t \to -\infty$" — and add "suppose $c_f \neq 0$" at the corresponding point of Step 3.
2. [source] statement/proof, case 2 (lines 80, 125): the imprecision above originates in the source. :cite Strogatz p. 133 ("Trajectories typically approach the origin tangent to the slow eigendirection... In backwards time the trajectories become parallel to the fast eigendirection") and p. 136 ("all trajectories are parallel to the slow eigendirection as $t\to\infty$, and to the fast eigendirection as $t\to-\infty$") state the backward-time half for *all* trajectories, which is false for the orbits lying on the slow eigenline. Recorded as a discrepancy against strogatz-1994; it does not by itself fail the unit — finding 1 does, because our text restated the claim with its own (wrong) quantifier rather than reporting the source's.
3. [minor] example, Step 6 (line 227): $e^{-\pi} = 0.04321391826\ldots$, so the digit string "$0.0432139183\ldots$" is the correctly *rounded* ten-decimal value followed by an ellipsis, which conventionally signals truncation. The same mixing occurs throughout the part. Fix: either truncate (`0.0432139182\ldots`) or drop the ellipsis after a rounded value.
