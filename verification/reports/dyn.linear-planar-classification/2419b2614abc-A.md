## Findings
1. [source] remark (line 181) / register entry: Strogatz states the backward-time half of the node
   tangency for all trajectories (p. 133 "In backwards time the trajectories become parallel to the
   fast eigendirection"; p. 136, unhedged), which fails on the slow eigenline. The unit does not
   reproduce the defect: case 2 of the statement names the excluded eigenline in each half, the
   remark says so explicitly, and the counterexample $A=\operatorname{diag}(-1,-2)$ given there is
   genuine. Already recorded in verification/discrepancies.md (strogatz-1994, §5.2, pp. 133 and 136).
   No action; the unit passes.
2. [minor] method (line 191): step 4 says the orbits are tangent to the slow eigendirection without
   the exclusion that the statement and proof are careful about. Fix: "every orbit except the two on
   the fast eigenline is tangent to the slow eigendirection at the origin".
3. [minor] proof (line 172): Step 5 establishes $x(t)\to 0$ for $\lambda<0$ and then asserts
   asymptotic stability, whereas Steps 3 and 4 exhibit the uniform bound that gives Lyapunov
   stability. Fix: add that $\sup_{t\ge 0}(1+t)e^{\lambda t}<\infty$, so $|x(t)|\le C|x(0)|$.
4. [minor] remark (line 179): "On the parabola $\Delta = 0$ the Jordan form is needed instead
   (Step 5)" overstates slightly — the star-node subcase of Step 5 uses no Jordan form. Fix: "in the
   degenerate subcase the Jordan form is needed instead".
5. [minor] method (line 194): step 7 is valid because $\Delta<0$ forces $c\neq 0$ in
   $A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$ (if $c=0$ the eigenvalues are $a$ and $d$, real), so the
   test never returns zero. Fix: say so in half a sentence, since the recipe otherwise looks as if it
   could fail.
6. [minor] statement (line 72): $\lambda_\pm = \tfrac12(T\pm\sqrt{\Delta})$ is written before the
   sign of $\Delta$ is split, so for $\Delta<0$ it silently needs a branch of the complex square
   root; case 3 then re-derives the eigenvalues correctly. Fix: a parenthesis "(a complex square root
   when $\Delta<0$; see case 3)".
