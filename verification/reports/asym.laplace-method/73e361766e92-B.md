## Findings
1. [minor] application (line 231): "the exponent $K$ is only continuous, not smooth, at the points
   where the minimiser jumps ... the non-degeneracy hypothesis (L1) would be false". Two things are
   run together. The minimiser $y(x,t)$ of $y\mapsto K(x,y,t)$ jumps because two separated minima
   exchange rank at a shock, not because $K$ fails to be smooth there: for smooth initial data $g$
   the antiderivative $h$ is smooth, $K(x,\cdot,t)$ is smooth in $y$, and the minimiser still jumps.
   And (L1) is not automatically false: $\partial_y^2K = 1/t + h''(y)$ is perfectly capable of being
   positive at the minimiser. Fix: give the two real reasons separately -- that Evans's lemma asks
   only for continuity, which matters because $h$ is merely Lipschitz when $g$ is bounded measurable,
   and that at the exceptional $x$ the minimiser is not unique, so there is no single Gaussian to
   expand about.
2. [minor] application (lines 233-237): the theorem proved above is stated for an exponent $f$ that
   does not move with the large parameter, but the Bayesian paragraph applies it with $x=n$ and
   $f=\ell_n$, an $n$-dependent average log-likelihood (as does Grimmett and Stirzaker's $f_n$,
   quoted in the sources). Nothing here is false in the cases one cares about, but the step needs a
   word. Fix: add half a sentence saying that an $n$-dependent exponent needs a version of the
   hypotheses uniform in $n$ (or that $\ell_n \to \ell$ with $\hat\theta \to \theta^*$), rather than
   the theorem as literally stated.
3. [minor] statement (line 67): "The pointer there should be to his corrected form (12.36), p. 387"
   reads as an editorial note to a drafter rather than as prose for a reader. The content is right
   and is confirmed below; only the sentence form is off. Fix: state it directly, e.g. "The
   consistent form of the series is Hassani's (12.36), p. 387, not (12.33)".
4. [minor] automated check: two `warn ... never mention laplac` lines, for hassani-2013 pp. 383-387
   and rhb-2006 pp. 637-638. Both are false positives and the unit already anticipates them: Hassani
   files this under "method of steepest descent" (the definition block says so explicitly and
   contrasts the naming with Grimmett and Stirzaker), and Riley, Hobson and Bence carry the recipe
   out on the Gamma integral without naming the method at all, which is exactly what the `role`
   field claims. No change needed; recorded so the warnings are not re-raised.
