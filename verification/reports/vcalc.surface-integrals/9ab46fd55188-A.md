## Findings
1. [minor] statement "Orientation conventions" (line 60): the clause "A closed surface need not be simply connected — the surface of a torus is closed" offers as illustration a property (closedness) other than the one being illustrated (failure of simple connectedness). RHB p. 390 says the torus surface is *not simply connected*. Fix: "— the surface of a torus is not".
2. [minor] proof, Step 4 (line 130): "On a connected parameter domain $\det Dh$ has constant sign" uses connectedness of the *interior* of $\bar D$, whereas the definition (line 29) assumes only that $D$ is connected. A closure of a bounded open set can be connected with disconnected interior (two closed discs tangent at a point), and then $\operatorname{sgn}(\det Dh)$ need not be constant. Fix: either assume the interior of the parameter domain is connected in the definition, or say in Step 4 "on each connected component of the interior", noting that this is exactly the freedom a non-orientable gluing exploits.
3. [minor] frontmatter `prereqs` (line 7): three claims lean on the inverse/implicit function theorem — "Every regular patch is locally of this form" (line 83), the level-set criterion of form 2 (line 84, Strauss Thm 5), and the change-of-parameters proposition quoted at line 67, which do Carmo derives from it. The handbook has `found.implicit-inverse-function`. Fix: add it to `prereqs`. (Not blocking: the theorem is standard undergraduate calculus, and the one place the proof needs it, the diffeomorphism property of $h$, is taken from a cited source rather than argued here.)
4. [minor] definition (line 49): the equivalence of "continuous unit normal field" with do Carmo's positive-Jacobian Definition 1 is asserted as "equivalently". do Carmo proves it in §2-6 just after Def. 1 (p. 107), which is outside the cited page range "72, 98-102, 106". Fix: extend the locator to p. 107, or mark the equivalence as a standard fact.
