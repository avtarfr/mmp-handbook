## Findings
1. [minor] statement "The surface element does not depend on the parametrization" (line 69): the text says that $h$ being a $C^1$ diffeomorphism "is not an extra assumption but a theorem: for two regular patches covering the same surface it is automatic, being do Carmo's change-of-parameters proposition". do Carmo's Prop. 1 (p. 72) is stated for parametrizations of a *regular surface*, i.e. maps that are homeomorphisms onto open subsets of $S$; our "regular patch" asks only for injectivity on $\operatorname{int} D$ together with $\mathbf{r}_u\times\mathbf{r}_v\neq\mathbf{0}$, which makes $\mathbf{r}|_{\operatorname{int}D}$ an injective immersion but not necessarily an embedding, so the proposition does not literally apply to every pair of patches our definition admits. The unit already flags the smooth-to-$C^1$ extension honestly; this second gap is not flagged. Fix: add to the definition that $\mathbf{r}$ is a homeomorphism of $\operatorname{int} D$ onto its image (automatic in every case the unit uses), or say that the automaticity is do Carmo's for patches of that kind.
2. [minor] definition "Regular patch, surface element, orientation, flux" (line 42): "An **orientation** ... equivalently (do Carmo's Def. 1) it can be covered by patches whose changes of parameters all have positive Jacobian determinant." do Carmo's Def. 1 (p. 106) *is* the positive-Jacobian atlas condition; its equivalence with the existence of a continuous unit normal field is a separate proposition. Fix: "do Carmo takes the atlas condition as his definition (Def. 1, p. 106); the two conditions are equivalent."
3. [minor] proof, Step 4 (line 110): "On a connected parameter domain $\det Dh$ has constant sign". The constancy argument needs the *interior* of $\bar D$ to be connected, and a connected $D$ that is the closure of a bounded open set can have disconnected interior (the closure of two externally tangent open discs). Fix: say "connected interior", or add that to the definition's standing assumptions.
4. [minor] frontmatter `sources`, strauss-2008 role line (line 20): it describes Strauss as defining "a surface as a $C^1$ map from a plane parameter domain". On p. 422 Strauss defines a surface as a *continuous* map from a closed plane set and then says it is of class $C^1$ when its three coordinate functions are. The body of the unit does not repeat the imprecision. Fix: "a continuous map from a plane parameter domain, of class $C^1$ when its coordinate functions are".
