   locator but not in this inline cite.
   Fix: split the cite, or widen it to "§§25.8.1-25.8.2, pp. 907-909".

3. [minor] method, step 6 (line 185), and proof step 4 (line 158): "the relative error should fall
   like $\lambda^{-1}$ (or like $\lambda^{-1/2}$ if $g'(z_0)\neq0$ contributes at half-integer
   order)". With $f$ and $g$ analytic at a non-degenerate saddle — the hypotheses of this unit —
   there is no half-integer order: in the variable $\tau$ of step 2 the odd terms are odd and
   integrate to zero, which is exactly why Hassani's Eq. (12.33), p. 385, runs over $k$ in powers
   of $\alpha^{-k-1/2}$ with no intermediate terms. $g'(z_0)\neq0$ contributes at order
   $\lambda^{-1}$, paired with the cubic term of $f$. The same slack appears in step 4, where the
   relative error of replacing $g$ by $g(z_0)$ is given as $O(\lambda^{-1/2})$; that is a true but
   needlessly weak bound, and it is what makes the parenthetical in step 6 look justified.
   Fix: delete the parenthetical, and in step 4 say that the naive bound $O(\lambda^{-1/2})$
   improves to $O(\lambda^{-1})$ because the linear term is odd in $\tau$.
4. [minor] example, "The number" (line 263) and remark: the claim that the example's accuracy is
   "comfortably inside the $O(\lambda^{-1})$ relative accuracy the method claims" holds at the two
   $\lambda$ shown but is not a stable statement of the error law for an oscillatory leading term.
   Measuring $(\text{pred}-\text{exact})/\text{exact}$ I get $+5.87\times10^{-3}$ at $\lambda=20$,
   $+1.09\times10^{-3}$ at $40$, $-9.3\times10^{-4}$ at $60$, $-3.7\times10^{-3}$ at $80$,
   $+3.7\times10^{-3}$ at $120$, $-4.3\times10^{-3}$ at $240$: the product with $\lambda$ is not
   bounded, because near a zero of the cosine the $O(\lambda^{-1})$ phase shift produces an
   unbounded *relative* error. The unit's own closing sentence says as much qualitatively; the
   sentence before it does not.
   Fix: say the error is $O(\lambda^{-1})$ relative to each saddle's contribution, not to the sum.

5. [minor] application (line ~290): Hassani's Example 12.5.2 is cited as "pp. 385-386"; it begins
   on p. 386 and the saddle-point work runs to p. 387. Likewise the statement block cites RHB

   Fix: "pp. 386-387" and "pp. 909-910" respectively (the note block already has the latter right).
