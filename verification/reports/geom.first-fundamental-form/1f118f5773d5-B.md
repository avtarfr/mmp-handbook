[minor] frontmatter, `lee-2013` entry, and the `src` of the definition block: `pages: "327-329"` is declared to cover "the induced metric on a submanifold of $\mathbb{R}^n$ by pullback, and the length of a curve as the integral of the norm of its velocity". Those pages do not. Lee pp. 327–328 define a Riemannian metric as a smooth positive-definite symmetric 2-tensor field and give $g = g_{ij}\,dx^i dx^j$ (p. 328) — both correctly used by the unit — but the pullback/induced metric on a submanifold is in the section "Riemannian Submanifolds", p. 333, and $L_g(\gamma)$ for a piecewise smooth curve segment is p. 337. The definition block's sentence "Lee calls it the Riemannian metric induced on $S$ by the inclusion into $\mathbb{R}^3$" is accurate as to Lee but points at the wrong pages. Fix: `pages: "327-328, 333, 337"` (or just 327–328, 333, since the unit does not actually lean on Lee for curve length), and cite p. 333 in the definition block's `src`.
2. [minor] definition block, "Regions" paragraph: the block's `src` lists docarmo-2016 §2-2 pp. 54–55, §2-4 pp. 86–87 and p. 89, and §2-5 Def. 1 pp. 94–95. None of those pages defines a domain or a region; the definition is on p. 99 (declared in the frontmatter, not in the block). The paragraph is also the closest thing in the unit to a sentence-by-sentence rendering of its source: do Carmo p. 99 reads "an open and connected subset ... image in S of a circle by a differentiable homeomorphism which is regular ... except at a finite number of points", the unit "a connected open subset whose boundary is the image of a circle under a differentiable homeomorphism that is regular except at finitely many points". For a technical definition this is defensible, and the block is honestly labelled `adapted`, but the locator should at least be right. Fix: add `docarmo-2016:§2-5, p. 99` to the block's `src`; optionally recast the sentence (e.g. lead with what the condition is for — a boundary that is a single closed curve, smooth except at corners).
