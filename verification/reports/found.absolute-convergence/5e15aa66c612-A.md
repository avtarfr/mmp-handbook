Riemann: the $a_n^\pm$ lemma, that each list is infinite and sums to $+\infty$ while its entries tend to $0$, that each prescription consumes both lists and so is a bijection, $\lvert v_k - L\rvert \le \lvert t_k\rvert$ for $k \ge 2$ with the interior partial sums trapped between $v_{k-1}$ and $v_k$, the $k - \sup_{j \ge j_k} q_j$ bound in part 2, and the two-sided bound $[-Q, 1+P]$ with no limit in $\overline{\mathbb R}$ in part 3.
