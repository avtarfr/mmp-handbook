## Findings
1. [minor] remark (line 144): "for a compactly supported $f$ a jump is precisely what forces $\hat f$ to decay no faster than $1/\lvert\xi\rvert$" is stated as an equivalence, and only one direction is true. A jump (with $f$ of bounded variation) does force $\limsup \lvert\xi\hat f(\xi)\rvert>0$, because $2\pi i\xi\hat f(\xi)$ equals a Riemann-Lebesgue term plus a non-vanishing almost-periodic sum of jump exponentials; but a continuous compactly supported $f$ can also have $\hat f$ decaying arbitrarily slowly, so continuity does not buy $o(1/\lvert\xi\rvert)$. Fix: write "a jump forces $\hat f$ to decay no faster than $1/\lvert\xi\rvert$" and drop "precisely", or restrict the remark to piecewise-$C^1$ $f$.
2. [minor] remark (line 144) and method (line 153): the averaging rule is asserted in general ("this is the standard caution for a function with a **jump**"; "at a jump $f$ must be given the average of its one-sided limits") on the strength of one verified example. For $f=\mathbf 1_{[0,1]}$ the check is exact, but in general the right-hand series need not converge absolutely at a jump, and the averaged identity needs an extra hypothesis (bounded variation, with the $\xi$-series summed symmetrically). Nothing in this unit proves it. Fix: say that the averaged form holds for $f$ of bounded variation with the right side summed symmetrically, and mark it as outside the theorem proved here.
3. [minor] statement "The formula in $\mathbb{R}^n$" (line 93): "the proof given below is written for $n=1$, and Folland's runs the same way on $\mathbb{T}^n$". The coefficient computation is indeed the same (Folland, p. 254, over a fundamental cube), but his closing step is not the one used here: he concludes from $L^2$ convergence of the Fourier series of $Pf\in C(\mathbb{T}^n)$ (his Thm 8.35) together with uniform convergence, not from the pointwise uniqueness theorem of Stein and Shakarchi Thm 2.1. Fix: say that the Fourier-coefficient computation is identical and that Folland closes the argument differently.
4. [minor] application (line 241): the integral representation $\pi^{-s/2}\Gamma(s/2)\zeta(s)=\tfrac12\int_0^\infty t^{s/2-1}(\vartheta(t)-1)dt$ is quoted without its range. The source states it for $s>1$ (p. 156), and the integral in fact diverges at $t=0$ for $s\le 1$ since $\vartheta(t)-1\sim t^{-1/2}$. Fix: add "for $s>1$".
5. [minor] front matter (line 12): the `pages` field lists "39, 131, 153-156", but the definition block cites Stein and Shakarchi p. 134 for the $2\pi$-in-the-exponent convention (correctly: it is §1.2 there). Fix: add 134 to `pages` and to the `loc` string.
