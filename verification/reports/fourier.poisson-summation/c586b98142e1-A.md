## Findings
1. [blocking] definition "Periodization and sampling" (the sentence beginning "Here, as in :ref[the Fourier transform]"): the unit asserts that its convention $\hat f(\xi)=\int f(x)e^{-2\pi i x\xi}dx$ is the one used in the listed prerequisite `fourier.fourier-transform`. It is not. That unit defines $\hat f(k)=\int f(x)e^{-ikx}dx$, says explicitly "We follow Strauss", and lists the $2\pi$-in-the-exponent form as a *different* convention in its table of six, with dictionary entry $\hat f(2\pi\xi)$. So (a) the attribution is false, and (b) the symbol $\hat f$ silently changes meaning between a listed prerequisite and this unit, which is exactly the clash the note "Nothing in this unit mixes conventions" is meant to rule out. The consequence is concrete: the second example uses $\widehat{1/(x^2+a^2)}(\xi)=(\pi/a)e^{-2\pi a\lvert\xi\rvert}$, while the prerequisite's table of standard pairs gives $(\pi/a)e^{-a\lvert k\rvert}$ for the same $f$ under the same symbol; likewise the Gaussian rule $\widehat{e^{-\pi x^2}}=e^{-\pi\xi^2}$ and the dilation rule $\hat f(\xi)=s^{-1/2}\hat g(\xi/\sqrt s)$ used in the theta example are quoted in a convention the cited prerequisite does not supply. Fix: delete "as in :ref[the Fourier transform]{to=fourier.fourier-transform}" and say instead that this unit departs from the handbook's $e^{-ikx}$ convention because the $2\pi$-in-the-exponent form is the one in which the formula has no constants; then add our own line to the conventions note, $\hat f_{\text{here}}(\xi)=\hat f_{\text{house}}(2\pi\xi)$, so that the transform pairs and operational rules imported from the prerequisite can be converted. (The internal mathematics is unaffected: every formula in the unit is correct in its declared convention.)

2. [minor] example "A slowly convergent sum done in closed form" (the "Numbers" paragraph): "five hundred terms give three correct digits" overstates it. The symmetric partial sum at $N=500$ is $6.8467583$ against the exact $6.8507542998$, an absolute error of $4.0\times10^{-3}$ and a relative error of $5.8\times10^{-4}$, so the printed digits agree only to "6.8". Fix: "give the answer to about one part in $2000$", or quote the error, and keep the contrast with the eight digits from six terms of the transformed series.
