The second component needs step 3 (the orthochronous set is a subgroup) together with the observation that $(T\Lambda)^{0}{}_{0}=-\Lambda^{0}{}_{0}$, so the orthochronous subgroup has index two, hence is normal and its sign map is a homomorphism. The conclusion is correct; the inference is not spelled out. Fix: add half a sentence joining step 3 to step 4 ("step 3 makes the orthochronous transformations a subgroup, and $T$ maps the antichronous ones onto them, so the subgroup has index two and $\operatorname{sgn}\Lambda^{0}{}_{0}$ is multiplicative too").
