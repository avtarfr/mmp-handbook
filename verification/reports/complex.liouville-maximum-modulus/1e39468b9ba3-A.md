## Findings
1. [minor] frontmatter (line 7): `prereqs` names `complex.cauchy-integral-formula`,
   `found.compactness` and `found.connectedness-homotopy`, but the proofs use the $ML$ estimate
   directly (part 1) and the Cauchy-Riemann equations directly (part 2, and Step 3 of part 4), and
   the text `:ref`s `complex.contour-integrals`. Both are reachable transitively through
   `complex.cauchy-integral-formula`, and 79 of the 83 written units reference units outside their
   `prereqs`, so this is the handbook's practice rather than a defect of this unit; naming the two
   would still match the style guide's "name every prerequisite you rely on".
2. [minor] proof, part 4, Step 3 (line 105): $u^2 + v^2 = c$ is differentiated "on $D$", with
   $D = \{|z-z_0| \le r\}$ closed. The derivation is valid on the interior and extends to $D$ by
   continuity. Fix: say so in the one clause.
3. [minor] proof, part 5 (line 128): "let $\Omega_0$ be the connected component of
   $\operatorname{int}E$ containing $z_0$, a region" uses that components of an open subset of
   $\mathbb{C}$ are open (local connectedness). Everything else in this part is argued in full, so
   the omission stands out. Fix: one clause.
4. [source] statement, part 4 (line 45): Hassani Prop. 10.5.4, p. 317 states that $|f|$ cannot have
   a local maximum in the region of analyticity, with no exception for constants — which, read
   literally, is false, since a constant has a non-strict local maximum everywhere. The unit says
   exactly this, adopts the conditional form (b) instead, and proves that. Handled as
   `docs/contributing/source-authority.md` requires; already recorded in
   `verification/discrepancies.md` (two entries). Not blocking.
