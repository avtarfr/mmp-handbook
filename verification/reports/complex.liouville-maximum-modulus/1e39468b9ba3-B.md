## Findings
1. [source] statement, discussion after part 4 (line 45), on hassani-2013 Prop. 10.5.4, p. 317. The unit reports Hassani's proposition faithfully: he states, with no hypothesis excluding constants, that "the absolute value of an analytic function $f(z)$ cannot have a local maximum within the region of analyticity of the function", and his proof ends with "there is always another point which produces a larger absolute value", although the displayed chain only gives $\lvert f(z_0)\rvert\le\lvert f(z_{\max})\rvert$. The proposition is therefore false as written (any constant) and its proof does not give the strict inequality it claims. Per `docs/contributing/source-authority.md` this is not a failure of the unit: the unit says which version it follows (its conditional form (b)), cites both sources, and explains the difference. Recorded with `node tools/discrepancy.mjs add complex.liouville-maximum-modulus hassani-2013 "Prop. 10.5.4, p. 317" ...`.
