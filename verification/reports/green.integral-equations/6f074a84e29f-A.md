## Findings
1. [blocking] method "Turning a second-order linear ODE into an integral equation", step 2 (line 145): the three properties asserted of the resulting Fredholm kernel — "continuous, symmetric, and has a jump of $-1$ in $\partial K/\partial t$" — are false for the general equation $y''+A y'+B y=g$ with which the method opens, and they are stronger than what the source says. Arfken, Weber and Harris state those three properties (p. 1052, their numbered properties 1–3) only of the kernel of Example 21.1.2, i.e. for $A\equiv0$ and $B=\omega^{2}$ a constant that has been divided out and carried as the parameter; their general summary on p. 1052 claims only "a Fredholm-type integral equation with a kernel that will be the Green's function". Counterexample to the unit's version: take $a=0$, $b=1$, $A\equiv1$, $B\equiv0$, $g\equiv0$, $y(0)=y(1)=0$. Step 1 gives $K_{V}(x,t)=(t-x)[B-A']-A=-1$; carrying $y'(0)=p$ and imposing $y(1)=0$ gives $p=\int_{0}^{1}y$ and hence $y(x)=\int_{0}^{1}K(x,t)y(t)\,\mathrm{d}t$ with $K(x,t)=x-1$ for $t<x$ and $K(x,t)=x$ for $t>x$. That kernel is not symmetric ($K(0.2,0.8)=0.2$, $K(0.8,0.2)=-0.2$), is discontinuous across $t=x$ (it jumps by $+1$, the value $-K_{V}(x,x)=A(x)$), and has $\partial K/\partial t\equiv0$ on both sides, so there is no jump of $-1$. Even when $A\equiv0$ the kernel produced by this procedure is $B(t)$ times the Green's function, so it is symmetric only for constant $B$, and the jump in $\partial K/\partial t$ is $-B(x)$, not $-1$. Fix: attach the three properties to the case the source treats — $A\equiv0$ with the constant coefficient of $y$ extracted as $\lambda$, which is exactly the situation of the unit's own example — and state for the general case only that the kernel is the Green's function of the operator with those boundary conditions.
2. [minor] example, "A boundary-value problem, for contrast" (lines 231–235): the data are $y(0)=0$, $y'(1)=0$, which are not the two-point data $y(a)=y(b)=0$ that step 2 covers, so "Step 2 therefore gives" is not a literal application of the method. Fix: add `funcan.compact-operators`.
