## Findings
1. [minor] :::statement[Equivalent formulations of continuity] (line 57): item 6 introduces $\mathcal{E}$ inside the list ("some fixed family $\mathcal{E}$ that generates the topology of $Y$"), which reads as an existential quantifier, while the proof of $1 \Rightarrow 6$ (line 83) treats $\mathcal{E}$ as given in advance. Both readings are true and the proof of $6 \Rightarrow 1$ covers either, so nothing is wrong, but the quantifier is ambiguous. Fix: name $\mathcal{E}$ in the preamble ("let $\mathcal{E}$ be any family generating the topology of $Y$") and drop "some fixed" from item 6.
2. [minor] :::proof[When a continuous bijection is a homeomorphism] (line 184): "each point has a neighbourhood on which the restriction is a homeomorphism and therefore continuous" passes silently from continuity of $f|_U : U \to f(U)$ to continuity of $f|_U : U \to Y$. That step is item 4 of "Building continuous maps" (or composition with the inclusion), and the unit already has it. Fix: cite item 4 or item 2 at that point.
3. [minor] :::example[Producing a delta, and watching it shrink] (line 227): "Since $f'(2) = 10 > 0$, $f$ increases near $2$, so the largest admissible $\delta$ is the smaller of the two distances..." The conclusion is correct, but local monotonicity alone does not exclude a *nearer* point outside the interval at which $|f(x) - 4| \ge 1/10$; what makes it work is that $x^3 - 2x - 4.1$ and $x^3 - 2x - 3.9$ each have exactly one real root (their local maximum, at $x = -\sqrt{2/3}$, is $-3.01\ldots$ and $-2.81\ldots$, both negative). Fix: add that the two cubics have a single real root each, or phrase the claim as "$f$ is strictly increasing on $[1.98, 2.01]$ and $|f - 4| < 1/10$ exactly on the open interval between the two roots".
4. [minor] :::definition[Open and closed maps, homeomorphism, embedding] (line 157): the local-homeomorphism clause omits the continuity of $f$ that Lee (p. 597) builds into his definition. The two definitions pick out the same class of maps - the unit derives continuity from the local condition at line 184 - so this is not an infidelity, but it is a silent deviation from the cited source. Fix: a half-sentence noting that continuity is automatic here, so it need not be assumed.
