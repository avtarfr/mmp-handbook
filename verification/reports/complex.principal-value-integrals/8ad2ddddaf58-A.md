[minor] application, Fermi's-golden-rule bullet (line 331): "$-i\pi\delta(E_i-E_f)$, whose modulus squared gives the transition rate" — a delta function has no modulus squared, and the rate is $2\pi\lvert V_{fi}\rvert^2\delta(E_i-E_f)$, i.e. the squared *matrix element* times the imaginary part. The bullet's next sentence ("the golden rule is the imaginary half of this identity") is correct, so this is a wording slip. Fix: "and $-i\pi\delta(E_i-E_f)$, which, weighted by the squared matrix element, gives the transition rate".
