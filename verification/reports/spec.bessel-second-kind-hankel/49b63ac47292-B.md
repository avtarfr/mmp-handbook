1. [blocking] frontmatter `summary` (line 6): it describes $Y_\nu$ as "a second solution of Bessel's equation with a logarithmic singularity at the origin", which the unit's own statement block contradicts. That block says, two paragraphs in, "$Y_\nu$ is unbounded at the origin for every $\nu\ge0$: **algebraically** for $\nu>0$, logarithmically for $\nu=0$", gives the leading form $-\frac{\Gamma(\nu)}{\pi}(2/x)^{\nu}$ for $\nu>0$, and then spends a whole paragraph insisting that at half-integer order the singularity is "a **pure power and no logarithm**". The summary's description is accurate only at $\nu=0$: at positive integer order the logarithm is present but is not the leading singularity, and at non-integer order there is no logarithm at all. Since the summary is what stands alone on the contents page, a reader meets exactly the false impression the unit works to dispel. Fix: "...is a second solution of Bessel's equation, singular at the origin - like $x^{-\nu}$ for $\nu>0$, and logarithmically for $\nu=0$, with a $\ln x$ term appearing at every integer order".
