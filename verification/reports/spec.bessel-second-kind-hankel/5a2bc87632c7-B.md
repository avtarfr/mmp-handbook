[blocking] statement "Behaviour at the origin, and independence from $J_\nu$" (line 98): "so at half-integer order the Neumann functions are elementary as well, and have no singularity worse than $x^{-1/2}$". The second clause is false for every half-integer order above $\tfrac12$, and it contradicts the display five lines earlier in the same block, $Y_\nu(x) = -\Gamma(\nu)\pi^{-1}(2/x)^{\nu}+\cdots$, which gives $x^{-3/2}$ at $\nu=\tfrac32$. In closed form $Y_{3/2}(x) = J_{-3/2}(x) = -\sqrt{2/\pi x}\,(x^{-1}\cos x + \sin x)$, which is $O(x^{-3/2})$. Fix: delete the clause, or replace it by what is true — that at half-integer order the singularity is a pure power $x^{-\nu}$ with no logarithm, unlike the integer case.
