## Findings
1. [minor] :::statement[Free motion: three regimes] (line 108): "Hassani states case 3 as: for
   $R \ge 2\sqrt{L/C}$ the circuit does not oscillate". Hassani's $\ge$ covers his cases (a) *and*
   (b), i.e. $\gamma \ge \omega_0$, whereas the unit's case 3 is strictly $\gamma > \omega_0$. The
   quotation itself is faithful (p. 448) and the application block gets the boundary right
   ("$R \ge 2\sqrt{L/C}$ — that is, $Q \le \tfrac12$"), so this is only loose labelling.
   Fix: "cases 2 and 3 together", or "for $Q \le \tfrac12$".
2. [minor] :::remark (line ~255, "Critical damping is the fastest return"): "The optimum is
   shallow, however: ... which is continuous and has a corner, not a sharp peak". The decay rate
   $\gamma \mapsto \min$ real-part magnitude is $\gamma$ for $\gamma\le\omega_0$ and
   $\gamma-\sqrt{\gamma^2-\omega_0^2}$ above, whose right derivative at $\gamma=\omega_0$ is
   $-\infty$: at $\gamma=1.01\omega_0$ the rate is already $0.859\omega_0$, a $14\%$ loss for $1\%$
   overdamping. So the optimum is shallow only on the underdamped side; on the overdamped side it
   is a square-root cusp. Fix: say the penalty is linear below $\omega_0$ and like
   $\sqrt{\gamma/\omega_0-1}$ above it.
3. [minor] :::statement[Resonance and three readings of $Q$] item 5 (line ~165): the display ends
   "$\longrightarrow Q$ as $\gamma/\omega_0\to0$", but $Q=\omega_0/2\gamma$ itself diverges in that
   limit, so nothing converges to a fixed number. The content is that the ratio of
   $2\pi E/|\Delta E|$ to $Q$ tends to $1$. Fix: write
   $2\pi E/|\Delta E| = Q\bigl(1+O(\gamma/\omega_0)\bigr)$.
4. [minor] :::proof[Steady state, amplitude and phase] (line ~196): labelled
   `adapted src="hirsch-smale-devaney-2013:§6.5, pp. 132-133"`, but the route used (complexify,
   divide by $p(i\omega)$, take real parts) is not the book's — the book uses variation of
   parameters and the invertibility of $\exp(2\pi A)-I$ on the system form. Step 3 says so
   explicitly, so nothing is misattributed in substance; the block label over-credits the source.
   Fix: keep `adapted` for Steps 3-4 and mark the amplitude/phase derivation as ours, or note in the
   `src` that only the uniqueness and attraction are the book's.
5. [minor] :::example item 5 (line ~303): "the response still leads the $\pi/2$ of resonance" —
   $\delta=0.758<\pi/2$ is a smaller *lag*, not a lead. Fix: "the lag is still below the $\pi/2$ of
   resonance".
