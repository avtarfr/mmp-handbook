## Findings
1. [minor] statement "Solution in a moving frame", part 1 (line 80): the hypotheses on $g$ (continuous, bounded, $g\ge0$, $\int g = M$) do not guarantee that the centre of mass and the variance exist, yet both are named. A continuous bounded $g\ge0$ with finite mass can have no first moment (for example $g(y)=c/(1+y^2)$) or a finite mean and infinite variance (for example $g(y)=c(1+y^2)^{-3/2}$), and Step 3's "convolution adds means and adds variances" needs those moments finite. Fix: add "and suppose $\int|y|^2 g(y)\,dy<\infty$" to part 1 (mass conservation alone needs nothing extra).
2. [minor] example "A release into a river" (line 172): the formula used is the statement's with $g=M\delta$, which is outside the statement's hypotheses (continuous and bounded), and nothing in the unit covers a point source. The text flags this with "Treating the release as a point source", but the resulting $u$ is not licensed by any block here. Fix: say that $S_D(x-Vt,t)$ solves the equation for every $t>0$ and reproduces a point release in the limiting sense of `pde.heat-kernel`'s remark on $S\to\delta_0$, or replace the datum by a narrow Gaussian of mass $M$ and note that the numbers are unchanged to the digits quoted.
