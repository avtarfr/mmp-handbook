1. [minor] statement[Solution in a moving frame], part 1 (line 80): the parenthetical "Both moment
   hypotheses are needed" is, read literally, false. For $g \ge 0$ with $\int g = M < \infty$ and

   the first-moment hypothesis is implied by the second and is redundant in the hypothesis list. The

   proof is unaffected; only the list is longer than it needs to be. Fix: drop
   $\int|y|g\,dy < \infty$ and write "(the moment hypothesis is not automatic: a bounded continuous
