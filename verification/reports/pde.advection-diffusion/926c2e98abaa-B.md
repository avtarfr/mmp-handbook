Part 1's extra hypotheses (finite, positive mass and finite second moment) are enough for mass, mean and variance, and the claimed strictness $\mathrm{var}(0)>0$ for continuous $g\ge0$ of positive mass is right. The two counterexamples offered in part 1 are correct: $(1+y^2)^{-1}$ has finite mass and divergent first moment ($|y|g\sim|y|^{-1}$); $(1+|y|^3)^{-1}$ has finite mass and mean but $y^2g\sim|y|^{-1}$, so infinite variance.
