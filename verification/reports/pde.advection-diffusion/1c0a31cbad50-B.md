## Findings
1. [blocking] statement "Solution in a moving frame", part 2 (line 81): "By the time the cloud has travelled a distance $L$ ... its standard deviation has grown to $\sqrt{2DL/V} = L\sqrt{2/\mathrm{Pe}}$" contradicts part 1 of the same statement, which gives the variance as $\mathrm{var}(0) + 2Dt$. Part 2 is false for any datum with non-zero initial spread.
   Counterexample: $g(y) = e^{-y^2/2a^2}$ with $a = D = V = L = 1$. Part 1 (correctly) gives variance $a^2 + 2Dt = 1 + 2 = 3$ at $t = L/V = 1$, so the standard deviation is $\sqrt3 = 1.7321$; part 2 asserts $\sqrt{2DL/V} = \sqrt2 = 1.4142$. Step 3 of the proof (line 100) compounds this: "substituting $t = L/V$ gives part 2" drops $\mathrm{var}(0)$ without saying so.
   Fix: either add the hypothesis that the release is concentrated at a point ($\mathrm{var}(0) = 0$), as the river example in fact assumes, or state part 2 as the *diffusive* contribution: "its standard deviation has grown by $\sqrt{2DL/V} = L\sqrt{2/\mathrm{Pe}}$, to $\sqrt{\mathrm{var}(0) + 2DL/V}$".
2. [minor] statement part 1 (line 80): the centre of mass and variance are asserted for any continuous bounded $g \ge 0$ with $\int g < \infty$, but such a $g$ need have no first or second moment (e.g. $g(y) = (1+y^2)^{-1}$: $\int g = \pi$ but $\int yg$ is not absolutely convergent and $\int y^2g = \infty$), so $\bar x(0)$ and $\mathrm{var}(0)$ can be undefined. Fix: assume $\int(1+|y|^2)g(y)\,dy < \infty$ for part 1.
