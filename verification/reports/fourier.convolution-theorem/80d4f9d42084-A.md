## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Algebra of convolution" (line 103): "The function $(x,z)\mapsto f(x)g(z-x)$ is measurable" is asserted bare. For merely Lebesgue-measurable $g$ this is the one point in the Tonelli step that is not immediate (it needs a Borel representative, or the completeness argument Folland runs in Prop. 8.6). Fix: add "measurable, because $(x,z)\mapsto z-x$ is continuous and $g$ may be replaced by a Borel function equal to it a.e.", or cite Folland for it.
2. [minor] proof "Algebra of convolution" (line 119): "Associativity and distributivity follow the same way, by Fubini and a substitution respectively" misassigns the mechanism — distributivity is just linearity of the integral in the second slot, no substitution involved; the substitution belongs to associativity, alongside Fubini. Fix: "associativity by Fubini together with the substitution $u=y-x$; distributivity by linearity of the integral".
3. [minor] proof "The convolution theorem" (line 183): "it needs the inversion theorem anyway to make sense of $\hat f*\hat g$" is imprecise — what is needed is only that $\mathcal{F}$ maps $\mathcal{S}$ into $\mathcal{S}$ (part 1 of the inversion unit's statement), not the inversion formula itself. Fix: say "needs part 1 of :ref[]{to=fourier.fourier-inversion} anyway".
4. [minor] definition (line 43): "whenever the integral converges" is attached to the convolution but not to the cross-correlation, although the same caveat applies to $f\star g$. Fix: state the proviso once for both.
