## Findings
1. [minor] proof of "Partial derivatives, continuity and the total derivative", part 2 (line 93): "Fix $a \in U$ and $r > 0$ with the closed ball $\lvert v\rvert \le r$ contained in $U$" describes a ball of displacements, not a ball of points; what is needed is $\{a + v : \lvert v\rvert \le r\} \subseteq U$, which is how the rest of the paragraph uses it ("$c_k$ lies in the ball"). Fix: write the closed ball of radius $r$ about $a$.
2. [minor] same proof, part 2 (line 99): the mean value theorem is applied to the $k$th bracket without excluding $v_k = 0$, where the interval degenerates and there is no interior point. The bracket is then $0$ and the displayed identity holds with $c_k = a + v^{(k-1)}$, so nothing breaks. Fix: a parenthesis saying the case $v_k = 0$ is trivial.
3. [minor] proof of "Equality of mixed partial derivatives", "Higher orders for a $C^r$ function" (line 204): "Those two act on a partial derivative of $F$ of order $m - 2 \le r-2$" states the worst case as if it were the only one; swapping the differentiations at positions $i$ and $i+1$ makes them act on a derivative of order $i-1$, which is anything from $0$ to $m-2$. The argument is unaffected, since a derivative of order at most $m-2 \le r-2$ is $C^2$ in every case. Fix: "of order at most $m-2$".
