4. [minor] remark, bullet "Cramer's rule is not a method" (line 169): "its cost grows factorially" holds only if the $n+1$ determinants are evaluated from the defining sum or by cofactors. Fix: "its cost is $O(n^4)$ even with determinants computed by elimination, and factorial if they are computed from the definition".
