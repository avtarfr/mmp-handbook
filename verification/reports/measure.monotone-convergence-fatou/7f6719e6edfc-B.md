I opened p. 47 and confirmed it: no non-negativity and no integrable lower bound is imposed, and as printed the inequality is false — $f_j = -j\,\mathbf{1}_{(0,1/j)}$ on $\Omega=(0,1)$ has $\liminf_j f_j = 0$ everywhere, so the left side is $0$, while $\int f_j = -1$ for every $j$ and the right side is $-1$. Their Thm 0.7.2 immediately above does carry $0 \le f_i \le f_{i+1}$, so the omission looks accidental. Already on record in `verification/discrepancies.md` (two entries, measure.monotone-convergence-fatou / stakgold-holst-2011, §0.7 Thm 0.7.3, p. 47). The unit follows Folland Thm 2.18 and Evans Thm 3, states that Stakgold and Holst's hypotheses differ, and gives this very counterexample, so it reports the source faithfully. No action on the unit.
2. [minor] proof "Monotone convergence", step 6 (line ~106): "$f - f\mathbf{1}_E$ and $f_n - f_n\mathbf{1}_E$ ... have zero integral; so $\int f = \int f\mathbf{1}_E$". The passage from "$\int (f - f\mathbf{1}_E) = 0$" to "$\int f = \int f\mathbf{1}_E$" uses additivity of the integral on $\mathcal{L}^{+}$, which is proved only in step 1 of the *next* proof (and the Fatou proof's step 4 does name that dependence explicitly). There is no circularity — the additivity proof uses steps 1–5 of this proof, not step 6 — but the reader is not told. Fix: either say "by additivity on $\mathcal{L}^{+}$, proved in step 1 of the next proof from steps 1–5 above", or argue directly from $f\mathbf{1}_E \le f \le f\mathbf{1}_E + \infty\cdot\mathbf{1}_Z$ and claim 6.
