## Findings
1. [blocking] application (line 232): the sawtooth counterexample is described with a false property that contradicts the next clause. With $u_k$ linear of slope $+1$ on $(\tfrac{j-1}{2k},\tfrac{j}{2k})$ for odd $j$ and slope $-1$ for even $j$, the function does **not** vanish "at every endpoint $j/(2k)$": it vanishes exactly at the even-indexed endpoints $j/(2k)$ with $j$ even, i.e. at the points $0, 1/k, 2/k, \dots, 1$, and it attains its maximum $1/(2k)$ at the odd-indexed endpoints. The sentence is self-contradictory as written, since vanishing at every $j/(2k)$ together with slope $\pm1$ on intervals of length $1/(2k)$ would force $u_k \equiv 0$ and hence $\lVert u_k\rVert_\infty = 0$, not the $1/(2k)$ asserted five words later. Everything the example actually needs is still true ($j = 2k$ is even, so $u_k(0) = u_k(1) = 0$; $\lVert u_k\rVert_\infty = 1/(2k)$; $E(u_k) = 0$; $E(0) = 1$), so only the description is wrong. Fix: write "it vanishes at the endpoints $j/k$, $j = 0,\dots,k$ (the even-indexed partition points), so $u_k(0) = u_k(1) = 0$, and $\lVert u_k\rVert_\infty = 1/(2k)$, attained at the odd-indexed points $(2i-1)/(2k)$".
2. [minor] remark (line 151) and application (line 234): the phrase "lower semicontinuity of energy functionals ... a use Stakgold and Holst single out / highlight" over-attributes. What :cite[Stakgold and Holst]{loc="§0.7, p. 47"} actually print, in the sentence introducing Thm 0.7.3, is that the relationship between the Lebesgue integral and the lim-inf "is fundamentally important to the study of variational problems in partial differential equations"; the words "lower semicontinuity" and "energy functional" do not occur there. The specialisation is correct and is ours, but it is attributed to them. Fix: attribute to them only "fundamentally important to variational problems in PDE", and present the lower-semicontinuity reading as our own gloss (it is accurate and the application block develops it well).
