In $\mathbb{R}^{3}$ take $B_{0.75}(0)$, $p = (0, \tfrac12, \tfrac12)$ and $q = (\tfrac12, 0, 0)$: $|p| = 0.7071 < 0.75$ and $|q| = 0.5 < 0.75$, yet the first vertex of the staircase from $p$ to $q$ is $(\tfrac12, \tfrac12, \tfrac12)$, at distance $0.8660 > 0.75$ from the centre.
