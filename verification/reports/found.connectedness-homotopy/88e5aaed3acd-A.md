1. [blocking] :::note[What this unit quotes without proving] (line 229): "Both halves are proved with covering-space theory, which he takes up immediately afterwards, pp. 615-616". This is false for the second half. Covering-space theory gives $\pi_1(S^1, (1,0)) \cong \mathbb{Z}$ by path lifting, exactly as the sentence that follows describes, but there is no covering-space proof that $S^n$ is simply connected for $n > 1$: simple connectivity of the base is an *input* to the covering-space results, not an output (Lee's own Props A.79 and A.80, p. 616, both hypothesise it). The standard proofs of A.68(b) deform a loop off a point and use $S^n \setminus \{\text{pt}\} \cong \mathbb{R}^n$, or apply the Seifert-van Kampen theorem. The same bullet also says Lee "states them as Prop A.68" and leaves the impression that he proves them later; in fact he sets both A.68 and A.69 as Exercise A.70, p. 614, before the covering-maps section begins. This is our own claim about where a quoted result comes from, and it misdirects the reader. Fix: restrict the covering-space sentence to $S^1$ and give the loop-compression (or van Kampen) route for $n > 1$, and say that Lee leaves A.68 as Exercise A.70.
2. [source] :::statement[Properties of connected spaces], item 5 (line 51), with its proof (line 81): "Components are nonempty, pairwise disjoint and closed, and $X$ is their union" is false for $X = \emptyset$. Under this unit's own definitions $\emptyset$ is connected (a separation needs two nonempty pieces) and, when $X = \emptyset$, it is a maximal connected subset, hence a component - and it is not nonempty. The proof's inference "Every $x$ lies in the component $C(x)$, so the components are nonempty" establishes only that each $C(x)$ is nonempty, not that every component is some $C(x)$; the same silent assumption appears at "So $P$ is nonempty" in proof 3(b), line 127. Item 5 is a faithful report of Lee's Prop A.39(d), p. 608, which omits the hypothesis in the same way, so this is not a blocking finding; recorded with `node tools/discrepancy.mjs add found.connectedness-homotopy lee-2013 "Prop A.39(d), p. 608" ...`. Fix (optional, and in the spirit of the "nonempty" the unit already adds to item 3): say "for $X \neq \emptyset$" in item 5, or add one clause to the proof - a component of a nonempty $X$ cannot be empty, because $\{x\}$ is a strictly larger connected set for any $x \in X$.
