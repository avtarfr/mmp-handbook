## Findings

1. [source] statement "Path-connectedness against connectedness" (line 66): the unit draws item 2
   from Lee's Prop. A.41(a), which says Prop. A.39 holds verbatim with "connected" replaced by
   "path-connected" and "component" by "path component" *throughout*. Read literally that makes
   A.39(d) say path components are closed, which is false, and the unit's own remark (line 157)
   says the opposite. The unit handles this correctly - it imports only the A.39(a) and A.39(c)
   analogues, names them "the two analogues of items 2 and 4 above", and states the closedness
   failure as its own - so there is nothing to fail. Recorded with `discrepancy.mjs` against
   lee-2013 "Prop. A.41(a), p. 608". Fix (optional, not required): one clause in the line-157
   remark saying that Lee's A.41(a) is to be read with this exception would spare a reader who
   checks the book.
2. [source] statement "Properties of connected spaces", item 5 (line 51): "Components are
   nonempty" is Lee's Prop. A.39(d) reported faithfully, but it fails for $X = \emptyset$ under the
   definitions this unit uses: the unit's own item 3 (line 49) correctly observes that $\emptyset$
   is connected, and $\emptyset$ is then the unique maximal connected subset of $\emptyset$, hence a
   component that is empty. The same vacuous gap affects "the path components partition $X$"
   (line 59). Per `source-authority.md` this is the source's imprecision, not infidelity, so it is
   not blocking; recorded against lee-2013 "Prop. A.39(d), p. 608". Fix: add "for $X$ nonempty"
   to item 5, or define a component as the class of a point rather than as a maximal connected
   subset.
3. [minor] statement "Properties of connected spaces", item 8 (line 54): the axis-parallel
   refinement of the polygonal criterion is stated for $\mathbb{R}^{n}$. Ahlfors makes that remark
   for the plane (p. 56) and separately says Thm 3 "generalizes easily to $\mathbb{R}^n$ and
   $\mathbb{C}^n$" (p. 57); he does not put the two together. The $\mathbb{R}^{n}$ axis-parallel
   version is true (the same staircase-inside-a-ball argument works), but it is our extension of the
   source. Fix: mark it as ours in a parenthesis, as the unit already does scrupulously for item 3,
   item 9 and the Euclidean paragraph at line 69.
4. [minor] note (line 33, `provenance=original`): "The failure is measured by the fundamental
   group." For plane regions this is exact, but in general the obstruction to a curl-free field
   having a potential, or to an exact-differential integral vanishing, is homological
   ($H^1$ / $H_1$), and a trivial $\pi_1$ is sufficient rather than necessary: an open subset of
   $\mathbb{R}^5$ built as a regular neighbourhood of a presentation complex of a nontrivial perfect
   group has $\pi_1 \neq 1$ and $H_1 = 0$, and there every closed 1-form is exact. Fix: "the
   fundamental group detects the failure" or "measured by the first homology, which a trivial
   fundamental group kills".
5. [minor] frontmatter `prereqs` (line 7): `group.groups-subgroups` and
   `group.homomorphisms-quotients` both come after this unit in `content/map.yaml` (the automated
   check warns on exactly this), and `map.yaml` line 67 lists only
   `found.continuity-homeomorphism` as a prerequisite for this id. The two group units are
   genuinely used (the fundamental group, the induced homomorphism), so the list is honest, but the
   unit now depends forward on Part-level material 650 lines later in the map. Fix: an
   architecture decision - either move the two group units earlier, or drop them from `prereqs`
   and keep only the `:ref` links, since nothing in the unit needs more than the word "group".
6. [minor] statement "Properties of connected spaces", item 1 (line 47): the definition of
   *interval* tracks Lee's sentence on p. 607 clause for clause ("containing more than one point"
   -> "holding more than one point"; "having the property that whenever ... it follows that
   $c \in J$ as well" -> "for which ... always has $c \in J$ as well"). Not blocking - it is one
   short definitional clause whose content is a quantified condition with essentially one
   formulation, and the block as a whole is a genuine restructuring into nine named items that Lee
   does not have - but it is the closest passage in the unit and worth one more pass. Fix: state it
   as convexity ("$J$ has at least two points and contains the whole segment between any two of
   them").
