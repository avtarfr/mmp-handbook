## Findings

1. [minor] application (block 13): the cost argument conflates two grids. It places scheme (H)
   on the *vertical* diffusion, then computes the refinement cost by halving the *horizontal*
   spacing, so the "companion stability constraint on $\Delta t$" that supplies the factor of
   four in time steps is the constraint attached to a $\Delta x$ that the paragraph has not
   said is being halved. The arithmetic quoted (order two, so error $\div 4$; two horizontal
   dimensions, so columns $\times 4$; $\Delta t \propto (\Delta x)^2$, so steps $\times 4$;
   total $\times 16$) is correct for an explicit parabolic scheme refined in the directions it
   is discretised in. Fix: either say the horizontal diffusion/mixing term is discretised by
   (H), or keep the vertical placement and refine the vertical spacing. Note also that "at
   least sixteen" is the diffusive figure; an advective CFL limit $\Delta t \propto \Delta x$
   would give eight, which is less, so "at least" is doing work the diffusive case alone
   supports.

2. [minor] example 3 "Truncation error of the wave scheme" (the $\lambda = 1$ paragraph): the
   figure "$5\times10^{-39}$ in $40$-digit arithmetic" is pure roundoff noise and is not
   reproducible as a number. Recomputing the residual at 40 digits for the same solution
   $u = \sin x\,\cos 2t$, $c=2$, $x=1$, $t=0.3$ gave exactly $0$ at $\Delta x = 0.1$ and
   $1.84\times10^{-38}$ at $\Delta x = 0.05$. The substantive claim (the residual is zero, and
   provably so) is right and is proved in the next sentences. Fix: write "to roundoff level
   (below $10^{-37}$ in 40-digit arithmetic)" rather than a single spurious-precision value.

3. [minor] block order: the three `definition` blocks (local truncation error; the heat scheme;
   the wave scheme) sit after the `remark`, whereas STYLE_GUIDE.md §"Block order" puts
   `definition` first. The arrangement is defensible here (they define the objects the `method`
   and examples need, and the earlier statement/proof concerns only the difference quotients),
   and the automated check accepts it, but it is a departure worth noting.
