[blocking] note "Part 1 is quoted, not proved here", minimisation bullet (line 142): the displayed bound is false as written, and the symbol $\mathcal R$ changes meaning inside one sentence. $\mathcal R$ is introduced as the Rayleigh quotient ("the minimum principle gives $\lambda_{N+1}\le\mathcal R[r_N]$"), i.e. numerator over denominator. From that, $\lVert r_N\rVert^2\le\lVert\sqrt p\,r_N'\rVert^2/\lambda_{N+1}$, and the energy identity bounds the numerator by that of $f$, giving $\lVert r_N\rVert^2\le\lVert\sqrt p\,f'\rVert^2/\lambda_{N+1}$ — Strauss's (12), p. 313, $\lVert r_N\rVert^2\le\lVert\nabla f\rVert^2/\lambda_N$. The unit instead writes $\lVert r_N\rVert^2\le\mathcal R[f]/\lambda_{N+1}$, which differs from the true bound by the factor $\lVert f\rVert^2$ and is simply false for small $\lVert f\rVert$ (replace $f$ by $\varepsilon f$: the left side scales by $\varepsilon^2$, $\mathcal R[f]$ is unchanged). The preceding clause, "bounds the numerator of $\mathcal R[r_N]$ by that of $f$", is correct, so this is a slip in the final formula. Fix: write $\lVert r_N\rVert^2\le\lVert\sqrt p\,f'\rVert^2/\lambda_{N+1}\to 0$ (or "the numerator of $\mathcal R[f]$, divided by $\lambda_{N+1}$").

Strauss gives the sum as $\tfrac12[f(x+)+f(x-)]$ for $a<x<b$ only; on the whole line the limit is $\tfrac12[f_{\mathrm{ext}}(x+)+f_{\mathrm{ext}}(x-)]$ for the periodic, odd-periodic or even-periodic extension.
