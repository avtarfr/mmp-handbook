## Findings
1. [source] statement "Heaviside expansion theorem": Arfken's Exercise 20.10.11 (p. 1045) states the formula $f(t)=\sum_i g(s_i)\mathrm{e}^{s_it}/h'(s_i)$ for *any* analytic $g,h$ with $h$ having simple isolated zeros, with no growth condition. Without one the formula is false: $F(s)=\mathrm{e}^{-s}/s$ (the transform of $u(t-1)$) is $g/h$ with $g=1$, $h(s)=s\mathrm{e}^{s}$ entire and $h$ having the single simple zero $s=0$, $h'(0)=1$; the formula returns $f(t)\equiv1$, while the true inverse vanishes for $t<1$. The unit reports the exercise faithfully (it even flags the generality as Arfken's), so this does not block. Recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs add fourier.partial-fractions arfken-2013 ...`.
