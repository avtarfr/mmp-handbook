## Findings
1. [blocking] `application`, first bullet "Multipole expansions outside a source" (line ~200): the multipole terms are mislabelled, off by one. The text says the regular part is "a constant and a possible $\log$" and then that "the negative powers $a_{-1}/z, a_{-2}/z^2, \dots$ are exactly the monopole, dipole, quadrupole, ... moments". In two dimensions the monopole (net charge, or net source strength) is carried by the *logarithmic* term: the complex potential of a localised 2D source of strength $m$ is $w(z) = \frac{m}{2\pi}\log z + c_{1}z^{-1} + c_2 z^{-2}+\cdots$, whose $z^{-1}$ coefficient is the **dipole** moment and whose $z^{-2}$ coefficient is the quadrupole. As written, every moment in the list is named one order too low, and the sentence is also internally inconsistent, because a $\log$ is not a term of any Laurent series yet is listed as part of one. This is an `original` block, so it is our own claim to get right. Fix: either (a) keep the potential and write "the logarithmic term carries the monopole (net source strength) and the negative powers $a_{-1}/z, a_{-2}/z^2,\dots$ carry the dipole, quadrupole, ... moments", stating that the $\log$ sits outside the Laurent series and that the series proper is the expansion of $w(z) - \frac{m}{2\pi}\log z$; or (b) expand the complex *field* $w'(z)$, which is single-valued and genuinely has a Laurent series on $\lvert z\rvert > a$, and whose $a_{-1}/z$ coefficient then is the monopole strength.
