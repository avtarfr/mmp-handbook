## Findings
1. [minor] remark "Conventions and where the hypotheses are used" (lines 202-209): the letters $A$ and $B$ carry two incompatible meanings inside one block. Paragraph 1 uses them for Riley, Hobson and Bence's kinetic and potential matrices (their $A$ is the *kinetic* one, so it corresponds to our $B$), while paragraph 4 ("$B$ must be definite, not merely invertible") uses them for this unit's pencil, where $B$ is the positive definite one. Each use is signposted, so nothing is false, but the roles are exactly swapped between the two paragraphs. Fix: in paragraph 1 write "their kinetic matrix $A_{\text{RHB}}$" and "their potential matrix $B_{\text{RHB}}$", or say in words which of ours each is half of.
2. [minor] application, first paragraph (line 302): "The three-mass chain of the second remark above". The phrase "three-mass chain" is the *third* remark's wording (Arfken's CO$_2$ chain, "their three-mass chain"); the second remark instead describes Riley, Hobson and Bence's linear triatomic. Both are three-mass linear models with a zero-frequency translation, so the sentence is true of either, but the pointer is ambiguous - and the example at line 256 points to "the third remark" for the same Arfken material. Fix: name the model rather than the paragraph number ("the linear triatomic of Riley, Hobson and Bence §9.1", or "Arfken's CO$_2$ chain").
3. [minor] example, step 4 (line 253): the display writes $M^{-1}K = \tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&3\end{pmatrix}\cdot\mu\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$. In fact $M^{-1} = \tfrac{1}{2ml^2}\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&3\end{pmatrix}$ and $K = ml^2\mu\begin{pmatrix}3&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, so neither displayed factor equals the matrix it names; only the product is right, the $ml^2$ cancelling. Fix: either carry the $(ml^2)^{\pm1}$ in both factors or say that it has been cancelled between them.
4. [minor] statement "Spectral theorem for a definite pencil", part 4 (lines 46-50), labelled `provenance=original`: the content (the quotient lying between the extreme eigenvalues, both attained, stationary exactly at eigenvectors with the eigenvalue as stationary value) is what Riley, Hobson and Bence state on p. 328 for the mechanical pencil, and the frontmatter gives rhb-2006 the role "the quotient bound". I judge `original` defensible, because the version here is strictly more general (Hermitian $A$, not positive definite) and is proved by transport from this handbook's own `linalg.quadratic-forms` Rayleigh-bounds and stationary-value statements rather than by RHB's route. Noting it only so the judgement is on record; no change required.
