## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Bessel's inequality…", step 4: "If $A_n$ had more than $n^2\|u\|^2$ elements, a finite subfamily $F \subseteq A_n$ of that size…" — $n^2\|u\|^2$ need not be an integer, and for $u = 0$ the phrase is vacuous but awkward. Fix: "choose a finite $F \subseteq A_n$ with $|F| > n^2\|u\|^2$; then $\sum_{\alpha \in F}|(u,e_\alpha)|^2 > |F|/n^2 > \|u\|^2$."
2. [minor] remark, the "index set need not be countable" bullet: the almost-periodic functions under $(f,g) = \lim_{T\to\infty}(2T)^{-1}\int_{-T}^{T} f\bar g$ form an inner product space that is not complete as written; the non-separable *Hilbert* space is its completion (the Besicovitch space $B^2$). The orthonormal family $\{e^{i\lambda t}\}_{\lambda\in\mathbb{R}}$ is correct. Fix: say "the completion of the almost-periodic functions under …".
3. [minor] statement 5 and proof step 7: both are fine for $H = \{0\}$ only under the convention that the empty family is an orthonormal basis, which the unit never states (statement 4 excludes $H = \{0\}$ explicitly, statement 5 does not). Fix: add "with the convention that $\varnothing$ is a basis of $\{0\}$", or restrict item 5 to $H \ne \{0\}$ as well.
