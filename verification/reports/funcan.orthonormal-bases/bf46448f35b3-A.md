## Findings
1. [minor] proof "The equivalence, existence, separability and classification", step 8 (classification): the sentence "a unitary map carries orthonormal bases to orthonormal bases, so isomorphic spaces have bases of equal cardinality" establishes that *some* pair of bases match in cardinality, but the statement's phrase "their orthonormal bases have the same cardinality, the **Hilbert dimension**" presupposes that all total orthonormal sets of one space are equinumerous. That lemma is not proved here and is not needed anywhere else in the unit; Kreyszig flags it (p. 168) as requiring set theory beyond his scope and defers to Hewitt-Stromberg. The handbook's own separable case is fully proved by item 5, so nothing downstream is affected. Fix: add half a sentence after step 8 saying that well-definedness of the Hilbert dimension in the non-separable case is taken from Kreyszig p. 168 and not proved here.
2. [minor] remark "Where the hypotheses are used", first bullet: "it converges to the projection of $u$ onto the closed span of the family, which is $u$ only when the family is total" — read for a single $u$ this is false ($u$ already in the closed span suffices); read for all $u$ at once, as intended, it is right. Fix: "which is $u$ for every $u$ only when the family is total".
