For additivity, I verified both parallelogram applications — $u=x\pm z$, $v=y\pm z$ give $u+v=x+y\pm2z$ and $u-v=x-y$ in both, so the $\|x-y\|^2$ terms do cancel on subtraction — and that the subtracted identity is exactly $4s(x,z)+4s(y,z)=2s(x+y,2z)$; setting $y=0$ gives $s(x,2z)=2s(x,z)$, and resubstituting turns the right side into $4s(x+y,z)$, so the argument is not circular.
