Part 2 (line 141) hypothesises that $w$ solves $-\nabla^2w = k^2w$ on **all** of $\mathbb{R}^3$; the remark at line 234 says so explicitly ("It is a statement about solutions on *all* of $\mathbb{R}^3$ with no source.
