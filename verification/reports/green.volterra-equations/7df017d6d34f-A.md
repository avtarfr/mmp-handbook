## Findings
1. [blocking] application, "The tautochrone" (line 271): "$T=\pi\sqrt{a/(2g)}$ is its period" is wrong, and it contradicts the unit's own definition of $T$ three displays earlier. $T$ is there defined as the time of descent from rest at height $y$ to the origin; on the cycloid that is a quarter of the period of oscillation. With $a=2r$ the formula gives $T=\pi\sqrt{r/g}$, whereas the period of Huygens's isochronous pendulum is $4T=2\pi\sqrt{2a/g}=2\pi\sqrt{4r/g}$ (the pendulum of length $4r$). As written the stated "period" is too small by a factor of $4$. Fix: "is a quarter of its period", or give the period as $4T=2\pi\sqrt{2a/g}$.
2. [minor] proof, step 5 (line 135): "Its sum is the limit of the iterates $T^{n}f$ ... and those converge to $y$ by step 3." Step 3 (Kreyszig's Lemma 5.4-3) delivers convergence of $B^{n}u_{0}=T^{mn}u_{0}$ only. The full sequence needs one more line: for each $j=0,\dots,m-1$, $T^{mn+j}f=B^{n}(T^{j}f)\to y$, so every residue class converges to the same limit and hence $T^{n}f\to y$. Fix: add it.
3. [minor] proof, step 4 (line 122): "since $y=Tu$ with $f$ arbitrary continuous is solvable, it is onto $C[a,b]$ as well" is garbled — $T$ depends on $f$, so "$y=Tu$ with $f$ arbitrary" does not parse. The content meant is that part 1 solves $(1-\lambda\mathcal K)y=f$ for every $f\in C[a,b]$, which is surjectivity. Fix: rephrase.
