  - Heat proof: $\tilde G=\theta(t)e^{-\kappa q^2t}$; the completion of the square
    $-\kappa tq^2+i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r = -\kappa t|\mathbf q - i\mathbf r/2\kappa t|^2 -

    $(2\pi)^{-n}(\pi/\kappa t)^{n/2} = (4\pi\kappa t)^{-n/2}$ verified; $\int G\,d^nr=1$.
  - Wave proof: $\tilde G=\theta(t)\sin(cqt)/cq$ satisfies $\tilde G(0)=0$, $\tilde G_t(0^+)=1$;
    the prefactor $4\pi/(2\pi)^3=1/2\pi^2$ and the division by $cr$; the product formula and
    $\int_0^\infty\cos(qa)dq=\pi\delta(a)$ giving $\tfrac\pi2[\delta(r-ct)-\delta(r+ct)]$; the
    scaling $\delta(r-ct)=c^{-1}\delta(t-r/c)$; and the assembly
    $G=\delta(r-ct)/(4\pi cr)=\delta(t-r/c)/(4\pi c^2r)$. Every step correct.
  - Example 1: $u=(1-e^{-t})\cos x$ has $u_t-u_{xx}-\cos x=0$ symbolically and $u(x,0)=0$;
    $u(0,\ln 2)=0.50000000$ exactly as printed; the relaxation rate $\kappa q^2=1$ and the steady
    state $-u_{xx}=\cos x$ both check.
  - Example 2: re-derived the substitution $\xi=r/2\sqrt{\kappa\tau}$ step by step — $d\tau=
    -r^2d\xi/2\kappa\xi^3$, $(4\pi\kappa\tau)^{3/2}=\pi^{3/2}r^3/\xi^3$, the $\xi^3$ cancelling, and
    the constant $(2\pi^{3/2})^{-1}(\sqrt\pi/2)=(4\pi)^{-1}$ — giving
    $u=Q\,\mathrm{erfc}(r/2\sqrt{\kappa t})/(4\pi\kappa r)$. Checked the printed number two ways:
    $\mathrm{erfc}(1)=0.15729920705$, and a direct numerical quadrature of
    $Q\int_0^{1/4}e^{-1/4\tau}(4\pi\tau)^{-3/2}d\tau$ with $Q=4\pi$ gives $0.15729920705$ —

    $\mathrm{erfc}^{-1}(1/2)=0.4769363$ and $1/(4\cdot0.4769363^2)=1.09905$, so "$0.4769$" and
    "$t\approx1.10\,r^2/\kappa$" are both right. The wave part's static value
    $Q/(4\pi c^2r)=0.5$ at $Q=4\pi$, $c=1$, $r=2$ checks, as does its being the solution of
