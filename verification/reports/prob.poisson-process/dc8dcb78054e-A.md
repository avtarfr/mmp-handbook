[blocking] proof "4: conditional uniformity, and the conditional property on a region", the block **Quoted classical input: Dynkin's $\pi$-$\lambda$ theorem** (line 220), part (ii): the theorem as stated is **false** for $k \ge 3$, and it is applied with general $k$ at line 297. As written the hypothesis is only the single full-intersection identity, "$\mathbb{P}(E_1 \cap \cdots \cap E_k) = \prod_i \mathbb{P}(E_i)$ for every choice of $E_i \in \mathcal{C}_i$". That does not imply independence of $\sigma(\mathcal{C}_1), \dots, \sigma(\mathcal{C}_k)$. Counterexample (checked numerically): $\Omega = \{1,\dots,8\}$ with the uniform measure, $\mathcal{C}_1 = \{A\}$, $\mathcal{C}_2 = \{B\}$, $\mathcal{C}_3 = \{C\}$ with $A = \{1,2,3,4\}$, $B = \{1,2,3,5\}$, $C = \{1,5,6,7\}$. Each singleton family is a $\pi$-system ($E \cap E = E$). The only available choice gives $\mathbb{P}(A \cap B \cap C) = \mathbb{P}(\{1\}) = 1/8 = \tfrac12\cdot\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac12$, so the stated hypothesis holds; but $\mathbb{P}(A \cap B) = 3/8 \neq 1/4 = \mathbb{P}(A)\mathbb{P}(B)$, so $\sigma(\mathcal{C}_1), \sigma(\mathcal{C}_2), \sigma(\mathcal{C}_3)$ are not independent. The correct statement needs either the product identity over **every subcollection** $S \subseteq \{1,\dots,k\}$, or the extra hypothesis that each $\mathcal{C}_i$ contains $\Omega$ (which then supplies the subcollection identities by padding with $\Omega$). This is our own material: the block is explicitly flagged as a quoted classical result that is in none of the sources, so the deference rule of `docs/contributing/source-authority.md` does not apply to it. As a consequence, the conclusion drawn at line 297 — that $\sigma(\Pi \cap A_1), \dots, \sigma(\Pi \cap A_k)$ are independent — is not licensed by the theorem as the unit quotes it, for $k \ge 3$. Fix: state (ii) with "each $\mathcal{C}_i$ containing $\Omega$" (or over all subcollections), and add the one-line observation that $\mathcal{C}(A_i) \ni \{N(\emptyset) = 0\} = \Omega$, since $\emptyset$ is an admissible Borel subset of $A_i$ in the definition at line 222. With that sentence the application at line 297 goes through unchanged, and the verified identity already covers every subcollection.
