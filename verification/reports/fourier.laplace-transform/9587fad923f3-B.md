Recorded with `node tools/discrepancy.mjs add fourier.laplace-transform arfken-2013 "§20.7, p. 1010 vs (20.140), p. 1011" ...`. Not a defect in our text; no action needed in the unit.
2. [minor] statement "Existence, and standard pairs" (line ~59): "Arfken, Weber and Harris require $\mathrm{Re}\,s \ge \beta$ for convergence when $f$ grows no faster than $e^{\beta t}$". Arfken (p. 1039) requires $\beta$ *strictly* greater than the growth rate $\alpha$ before imposing $\mathrm{Re}\,s\ge\beta$, and at equality the integral need not converge ($f(t)=e^{\beta t}$ at $s=\beta$). The sentence as written is a shade looser than the source at the boundary. Fix: "require $\mathrm{Re}\,s\ge\beta$ with $\beta$ chosen strictly above the growth rate".
3. [minor] frontmatter `prereqs` (line 7): the automated run reports "prerequisite fourier.heaviside-step comes after fourier.laplace-transform in the map". The dependency is real (the $H$ and $\delta$ rows of the table, and rule 4), so the fix is a `content/map.yaml` reordering, which this wave's drafters are told not to make. Recorded for the chapter owner.
4. [minor] remark (line ~187): "where the two terms of the integrand must be closed on opposite sides depending on whether $t<a$ or $t>a$". In Arfken's Example 20.10.2 the opposite-side closure happens only in the band $0<t<a$; for $t>a$ and for $t<0$ both terms are closed on the same side. The sentence is readable as correct but invites the wrong reading. Fix: "where, for $0<t<a$, the two terms must be closed on opposite sides".
