## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Parts 1 and 2, from part 3" (line 65): "Repeating the step on $R'$" is not quite a repetition. The first pass established measurability of the integrand from the continuity of $f$ on $R$; the new integrand $h(x') = \int_{a_n}^{b_n} f(x',x_n)\,dx_n$ (zero off $R'$) is not handed to us as continuous, so the induction needs either a line saying $h$ is continuous on $R'$ (which it is, by uniform continuity of $f$ on the compact box) or, more simply, the observation that part 3 itself already returns the inner integral as a measurable function of $x'$, with $\int|h| \le \int|\tilde f| < \infty$, so only measurability and integrability are needed at every later pass. Fix: add that clause.
2. [minor] example "(b) Spherical coordinates" (line 238): this is Riley, Hobson and Bence's own worked example. Their pp. 205–206 compute $\rho\int(x^2+y^2)\,dV$ over the sphere of radius $a$ by exactly this route — spherical polars, the integrand $r^4\sin^3\theta$, the separation into three factors, $\int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\,d\theta = \tfrac43$, and the value $\tfrac8{15}\pi a^5\rho$ — and the block is labelled `provenance=original`, while `docs/contributing/phase-full.md` asks for examples invented here rather than a book's worked example. The prose is not close to theirs and every number is right, so I record this as a provenance matter for the overlap check rather than a mathematical defect; the application's ellipsoid, which reaches $M(a^2+b^2)/5$ through a linear change of variables, is genuinely ours and does not have this problem. Fix: change the integrand or the region in (b) — e.g. $\int_{B_R}e^{-|x|}\,dV$ or an integral over a spherical shell or cone — so it is not RHB's integral.
