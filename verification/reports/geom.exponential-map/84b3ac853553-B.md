## Findings

[source] frontmatter role (line 21) / hassani-2013 §29.1, p. 925: the Hassani page cited for "completeness of vector fields on a Lie group" argues from $(F_t)^n(g) = F_{nt}(g) \in G$ that *every* vector field on a Lie group is complete. That step is valid only for left-invariant fields, whose flow is right translation; $x^2\,d/dx$ on the Lie group $(\mathbb{R},+)$ is a smooth vector field on a Lie group that is not complete.
