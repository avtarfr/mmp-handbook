## Findings
1. [minor] application (line 359): the expected number of self-contacts is written as the unbounded sum $\sum_{m \ge 1}(n + 1 - 2m)\,p_0(2m)$. Read literally the weight $n+1-2m$ is negative for $2m > n+1$ and, since $p_0(2m) \sim 0.2333\,m^{-3/2}$, the series diverges to $-\infty$; the intended range is fixed only by the words "there being $n+1-2m$ pairs". Fix: write $\sum_{m=1}^{\lfloor n/2 \rfloor}$. The quoted values $5.80$, $39.9$, $476$ are the truncated sum and are correct.
2. [minor] statement, part (d) (line 109) against proof (line 246): the statement asserts $p_0(2n) \le C n^{-3/2}$ for a finite constant $C$, while the proof derives $p_0(2n) \le C n^{-3/2}(1 + o(1))$ with the explicit $C = 3\sqrt3/(2\pi\sqrt\pi) = 0.4666$. The two are reconciled only by enlarging $C$ over the finitely many small $n$, which the proof does not say. (The displayed $C$ in fact works for every $n \ge 1$, since $\binom{2n}{n}4^{-n} \le 1/\sqrt{\pi n}$ and $n\max_{i+j+k=n} w_{ijk}$ increases to $3\sqrt3/(2\pi)$ from below.) Fix: one clause in the proof saying the constant is enlarged to absorb small $n$, or state (d) with the $(1+o(1))$.
3. [minor] remark, "Higher dimensions" (lines 265-271): the asymptotic $p_0(2n) \sim 2(d/4\pi n)^{d/2}$, from which transience for every $d \ge 4$ is deduced, rests on a local central limit theorem that is only gestured at ("can be made local"), and the coordinates of the lattice walk are uncorrelated but not independent, so the product Gaussian is not immediate. The formula is correct (it reproduces the verified $d = 1, 2, 3$ values) and the block is flagged as our own extension, but the deduction is a sketch. Fix: say explicitly that this is a heuristic, or get $d \ge 4$ transience from $d = 3$ by a coordinate argument.
4. [minor] front matter, `loc` and `role` (lines 11, 13): Exercise (3.9.1) is cited in the statement and proof blocks and is genuinely used (the geometric tail and the finiteness of all moments of $T$), but the front-matter `loc` lists only Examples (3.9.6) etc. for pp. 77-80. The page range covers it; only the enumeration is incomplete.
