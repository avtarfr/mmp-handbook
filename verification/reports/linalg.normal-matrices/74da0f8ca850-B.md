[source] hassani-2013, Prop 6.4.11, p. 181: Hassani states "An operator on a complex inner product space is normal iff it is diagonalizable", and on the same page says "it is straightforward to show that diagonalizability implies normality". That is false under his own Def 6.2.9 (p. 174), which calls an operator diagonalizable when *some* basis consists of eigenvectors, with no orthonormality required: $\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ has distinct eigenvalues, hence an eigenbasis, but is not normal. The unit does not repeat the error — its Conventions remark (line 82) tells the reader to read "diagonalizable" in such statements as "with an orthonormal basis", and its first trap bullet gives precisely this counterexample. Correct handling; recorded with `node tools/discrepancy.mjs add linalg.normal-matrices hassani-2013 "Prop 6.4.11, p. 181" ...`. Passes.
