## Findings
1. [blocking] definition "Closed, closable, closure" (line 63): "This is weaker than continuity, which would require $T\psi_n$ to converge whenever $\psi_n$ does" is false. Closedness is not implied by continuity, nor by the stronger condition the sentence substitutes for continuity. Counterexample: let $D \subsetneq H$ be a dense non-closed subspace and $T = I|_D$. Then $T$ is bounded, and $T\psi_n$ converges whenever $\psi_n$ does, yet for $\psi_n \to \psi \notin D$ the limit $\psi$ is not in $D(T)$, so $T$ is not closed. The correct relation is in the unit's own source list and is quoted correctly elsewhere in the unit: Kreyszig Thm 10.3-2(c) (p. 536) says a *bounded* operator is closed **iff** its domain is closed, and Stakgold-Holst Remark 2 (p. 309) says a bounded operator defined on a closed set is closed. This also contradicts the idea block (line 122), which correctly writes "A bounded operator defined on a closed subspace is closed". Fix: replace with something like "This is a different condition from continuity: continuity would make $T\psi_n$ converge whenever $\psi_n$ does, but it does not force the limit into $D(T)$; closedness says only that *if* both limits exist, they are related correctly. Boundedness gives closedness exactly when $D(T)$ is closed (Kreyszig Thm 10.3-2(c))."

2. [minor] definition "The adjoint of a densely defined operator" (line 94): "the bound lets the Riesz representation produce $\eta$" skips a step — the functional $\psi \mapsto (T\psi,\varphi)$ is bounded only on $D(T)$ and must first be extended to $H$ by density before Riesz applies. Reed-Simon (p. 252) are equally terse, so this is presentation, not error. Fix: add "extend by density to all of $H$ and apply Riesz".

3. [minor] statement "The adjoint controls closability" (line 112): "Stakgold and Holst state (a) and (c)" slightly overstates their Property 1 (p. 318), which asserts that $A^*$ is closed under the hypothesis that $A$ is closed and densely defined, whereas our (a) carries no closedness hypothesis. Our (a) is correctly sourced to Reed-Simon Thm VIII.1(a) (p. 253) and Kreyszig Thm 10.3-3 (p. 536). Fix: "state (a) for closed $A$, and (c)".

4. [minor] remark (line 193): "Density of $D(T)$ is used to define $T^{*}$ at all, and nowhere else" is slightly too strong. Step 6 of the second proof uses it again for a different purpose — to conclude that $D(T^{**}) \supseteq D(T)$ is dense, so that (b) may be applied to $T^{*}$. Fix: "... and, through that, once more in Step 6".

5. [minor] remark (line 194): Reed-Simon's Example 4 fixes $\psi_0 \in L^2$; the conclusion that $D(T^{*}) = \{\psi_0\}^{\perp}$ fails to be dense needs $\psi_0 \neq 0$. Fix: say "a fixed non-zero $\psi_0 \in L^2$".
