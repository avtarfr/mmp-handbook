## Findings
1. [minor] application (line ~205): "A particle in a box, a particle on a ring, and a particle on a half-line all carry the same differential expression $-\hbar^{2}\psi''/2m$ and differ only in the domain." The box and the ring do share $L^{2}(0,L)$ and differ only in the operator domain, but the half-line lives in $L^{2}(0,\infty)$, a different Hilbert space, so "differ only in the domain" overstates it for the third case. This is an `original` block. Fix: say "differ only in the domain (and, for the half-line, in the underlying $L^2$ space)", or replace the half-line by a particle on a ring of a different circumference.
2. [minor] proof "The adjoint controls closability" (line ~141): the opening sentence lists "it is linear, preserves the product inner product, and is onto, hence unitary; and $V^{2} = -I$, so $V[E]^{\perp} = V[E^{\perp}]$ ... and $V[E]$ is closed whenever $E$ is." The complement and closedness identities follow from unitarity alone; $V^{2} = -I$ is needed only in Step 3. A reader can misread the "so" as deriving the complement identity from $V^{2}=-I$. Fix: split the sentence, attributing $V[E^{\perp}] = V[E]^{\perp}$ to unitarity and reserving $V^{2} = -I$ for Step 3.
