- Calculations.
  - Midpoint (Stratonovich) limit, done from scratch: with $\Delta_j=A_j+B_j$ and $A_j,B_j$ independent $N(0,t/2n)$, $W_{\theta_j}=\tfrac12(W_{t_j}+W_{t_{j+1}})+\tfrac12(A_j-B_j)$; the first part telescopes to $\tfrac12W_t^2$ exactly and the second is $\tfrac12\sum_j(A_j^2-B_j^2)$, with mean $0$ and variance $n\cdot\tfrac14\cdot4(t/2n)^2=t^2/(4n)\to0$. So the mean-square limit is $\tfrac12W_t^2$, confirming the unit and contradicting the book's prose (finding 2).
  - Itô and right-endpoint rows: $2I_n=W_t^2-\sum(\Delta_j)^2\to W_t^2-t$ and the right-endpoint sum exceeds the left by $\sum(\Delta_j)^2\to t$, giving $\tfrac12(W_t^2-t)$ and $\tfrac12(W_t^2+t)$. Table correct, and the midpoint entry is the average of the two, as the unit notes.
  - Integrating factor: $\Phi'=-\alpha\Phi$, $\Phi''=(\alpha^2-\alpha')\Phi$, $(\Phi^{-1})'=\alpha\Phi^{-1}$, $(\Phi^{-1})''=(\alpha^2+\alpha')\Phi^{-1}$. All four verified by hand; the signs in the unit are right.
