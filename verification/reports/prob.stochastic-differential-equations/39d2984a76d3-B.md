5. [source] statement[Existence and uniqueness] (line 68): Problem (13.12.21)(b), p. 629, asks the reader to deduce that $V_t = \int_0^t \operatorname{sign}(W_s)\,dW_s$ solves $dX = \operatorname{sign}(X_t)\,dW$ with the *same* $W$. That is false pathwise, because $\operatorname{sign}(V)$ and $\operatorname{sign}(W)$ differ on a set of positive measure (Tanaka's formula gives $V = |W| - L$); the true statement is the weak one, with driving process $\int_0^\cdot \operatorname{sign}(V_s)\,dV_s$. The unit reports the source's formulation and flags the reuse of the letter. Already recorded in verification/discrepancies.md; not re-added.
