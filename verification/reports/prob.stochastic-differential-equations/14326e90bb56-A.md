3. [source] statement "Existence and uniqueness: what the source asserts" (line 68): "The process $V_t = \int_0^t\operatorname{sign}(W_s)\,dW_s$ is itself a standard Wiener process and solves this equation". With the same $W$ this is false: Tanaka's formula gives $V_t = |W_t| - L_t$ with $L$ the local time of $W$ at $0$, so $\operatorname{sign}(V_s)$ and $\operatorname{sign}(W_s)$ differ on a set of positive measure and $\int_0^t\operatorname{sign}(V_s)\,dW_s \ne V_t$. The true statement is the weak one, that $V$ solves $dV = \operatorname{sign}(V)\,dB$ for the Wiener process $B_t = \int_0^t\operatorname{sign}(V_s)\,dV_s$. The unit is however reporting Problem (13.12.21)(a)-(b), p. 629, faithfully, including the source's reuse of the letter $W$ for both processes, so this is not a failure of our text; recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs`. The conclusion drawn (pathwise non-uniqueness with unique fdds) is correct and matches (c)-(d). Fix (optional): add half a sentence saying that the driving process in the SDE is the Wiener process reconstructed from $V$, not the $W$ used to define it.
