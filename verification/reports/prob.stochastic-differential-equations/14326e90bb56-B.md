5. [source] statement "Existence and uniqueness: what the source asserts" (line ~62): G&S Problem (13.12.21)(b) asks the reader to deduce that $V_t = \int_0^t \operatorname{sign}(W_s)\,dW_s$ solves $dX = \operatorname{sign}(X_t)\,dW$ with the *same* $W$, which is false (by Tanaka's formula $V_t = |W_t| - L_t$, so $\operatorname{sign}(V_s) \neq \operatorname{sign}(W_s)$ on a set of positive measure); the true statement is the weak one, with a different driving process. The unit reports the source's formulation, and the conclusion it draws - failure of pathwise uniqueness with uniqueness of the finite-dimensional distributions - is correct. Already recorded in verification/discrepancies.md under this unit / "§13.12, Problem (13.12.21)(b), p. 629". Passes.
