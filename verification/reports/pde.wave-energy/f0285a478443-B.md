## Findings
1. [minor] application, first paragraph (line ~210): "the identity (E) is then Poynting's theorem, with $e$ the field energy density and $\mathbf{S}$ the Poynting vector" overstates the correspondence. Applied to one Cartesian component $u$ of $\mathbf{E}$ or $\mathbf{B}$, (E) gives $\partial_t\tfrac12(u_t^2+c^2|\nabla u|^2) + \nabla\cdot(-c^2u_t\nabla u)=0$; neither quantity is the electromagnetic energy density or $\mathbf{E}\times\mathbf{H}$, and recovering Poynting's theorem needs the full field (or the vector potential, and then only up to divergence-free rearrangements). Neither cited page (Strauss p. 40, Evans p. 79) makes this identification, so it is ours. Fix: "has exactly the form of Poynting's theorem", or restrict the sentence to the statement that the same multiply-by-$u_t$ computation yields Poynting's theorem for the full field.

2. [minor] statement "Conservation on a bounded domain" (line ~104): "Strauss sets both of these as exercises after deriving the conservation law (4)–(6)" is correct — they are §9.1 Exercises 6 and 7 — but those exercises are on printed p. 234, outside the declared `pages: "40-41, 230-233"`. Fix: extend the frontmatter range to `40-41, 230-234` (and name p. 234 in the block's `src`).

3. [minor] proof of "Conservation of energy on the line", Step 3 (line ~95): "by Step 1 both $u_x$ and $u_t$ are zero at $x=\pm R_T$". Step 1 establishes this on the open set $|x|>R_T$; at the endpoints themselves it follows from continuity of $u_x,u_t$ (and at $(\pm R_T,T)$ the point sits on the boundary of the influence sector, so the limit argument is the only route). Fix: add "and hence, by continuity, at $x=\pm R_T$".
