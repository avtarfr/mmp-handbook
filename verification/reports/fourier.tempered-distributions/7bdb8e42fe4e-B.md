3. [minor] note[Convention] (line 87): "The translation rule is mechanical: a factor $2\pi$ appears for each $2\pi$ missing from the exponent" is not reliable, for the same reason as finding 1. Fix: state the rule as a rescaling of the transform variable, or restrict the claim to the $\delta$ and $1$ lines.
