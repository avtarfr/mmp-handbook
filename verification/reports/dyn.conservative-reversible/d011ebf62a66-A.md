## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Nonlinear centres in a reversible system", step 1 (line ~122): the expansion is written $x\dot y-y\dot x=Q(x,y)+O(r^3)$ and then $\varepsilon\dot\theta=\varepsilon Q/r^2+O(r)$. Those orders need $f\in C^2$ (or Lipschitz $Df$); the statement hypothesis is only "continuously differentiable", for which Taylor gives $f(x)=Ax+o(|x|)$ and hence $x\dot y-y\dot x=Q+o(r^2)$, $\varepsilon\dot\theta=\varepsilon Q/r^2+o(1)$. The conclusion of the step, $\varepsilon\dot\theta\ge\tfrac12\mu$ for $0<r\le r_0$, follows from the weaker form unchanged. Fix: replace $O(r^3)$ by $o(r^2)$ and $O(r)$ by $o(1)$.
2. [minor] remark "The closed-contour step, proved" (line ~157): from $\partial g/\partial\rho\ge\rho(\lambda_{\min}+o(1))$ one cannot conclude $g(\rho_0,\theta)\ge\tfrac12\lambda_{\min}\rho_0^2$, since the $o(1)$ may be negative; integrating gives only $g(\rho_0,\theta)\ge\tfrac12(\lambda_{\min}-\delta)\rho_0^2$ after shrinking $\rho_0$. Nothing downstream uses the value of the constant, which is immediately renamed $2\varepsilon_0$. Fix: choose $\rho_0$ so that $\partial g/\partial\rho\ge\tfrac12\lambda_{\min}\rho$ and quote $g(\rho_0,\theta)\ge\tfrac14\lambda_{\min}\rho_0^2$, or simply assert a positive constant uniform in $\theta$.
3. [minor] proof "Nonlinear centres in a conservative system", step 5 (line ~113): "a surjection $\mathbb R\to\gamma$" presumes the solution through $q$ is defined for all time. It is, because $\gamma$ is a compact subset of $U$ and the orbit never leaves it, but the step is not stated. Fix: one clause invoking the escape-from-compacta form of the continuation theorem already listed in `ode.existence-uniqueness`.

[minor] proof "Nonlinear centres in a conservative system", steps 2 and 4 (lines ~108-111): the loop supplied by the remark lives in the disc of radius $\rho_0$ fixed there, while the no-other-fixed-point and $E\ge c$ conditions were arranged on $\overline B_r(p)$ at the head of the proof. The two radii are never compared. Fix: add "shrinking $\rho_0$ so that $\rho_0\le r$".
5. [minor] proof "Nonlinear centres in a conservative system", step 4 (line ~111): the parenthetical ":ref[flow box]{to=dyn.poincare-bendixson}" points forward to a unit that is not in `prereqs`. It is illustrative only - the step proves the local picture it needs from the implicit and inverse function theorems, both listed - so no circularity arises, but the cross-reference is to an unlisted unit.
