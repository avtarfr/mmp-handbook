## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Nonlinear centres in a conservative system", step 2 (line 96): the fact borrowed from Strogatz is phrased as "the connected component of $\{E = c+\varepsilon\}$ that meets every neighbourhood of $p$". No component of a fixed level $c+\varepsilon$ meets every neighbourhood of $p$: since $E$ is continuous and $E(p)=c$, the whole level set $\{E=c+\varepsilon\}$ is bounded away from $p$. The property the proof actually needs, and the one the remark delivers, is "the component of $\{E=c+\varepsilon\}$ lying in the disc of radius $\rho_0$". Fix: replace the descriptor with "the component contained in a fixed small punctured disc about $p$", matching the remark's conclusion.
2. [minor] proof "Nonlinear centres in a conservative system", preamble and step 2 (lines 92, 96) vs remark (line 116): the proof fixes a radius $r$ and the remark produces a different radius $\rho_0$, with no stated relation. Step 3's appeal to "the choice of $r$" to exclude other fixed points therefore applies to the wrong disc as written. Fix: say explicitly that $\rho_0$ may be shrunk so that $\overline B_{\rho_0}(p)\subseteq \overline B_r(p)$.
3. [minor] remark "The closed-contour step, proved" (line 116): "$g(\rho_0,\theta) \ge \tfrac12\lambda_{\min}\rho_0^2$" does not follow from the inequality just established. Only $\partial g/\partial\rho>0$ on $(0,\rho_0]$ was secured; integrating the sharper bound $\partial g/\partial\rho \ge \rho(\lambda_{\min}+o(1))$ gives $\tfrac12\lambda_{\min}\rho_0^2(1+o(1))$, so the stated constant needs $\rho_0$ shrunk further. The conclusion (some $\varepsilon_0>0$ exists) is unaffected, since the value of $\varepsilon_0$ is never used. Fix: shrink $\rho_0$ so that $\partial g/\partial\rho \ge \tfrac12\lambda_{\min}\rho$, and write $g(\rho_0,\theta)\ge \tfrac14\lambda_{\min}\rho_0^2$.
4. [minor] notation (lines 96, 113-116 vs 126-139): $\varepsilon$ denotes a small positive level increment in the conservative proof and its remark, and a sign $\pm1$ in step 1 of the reversible proof and in steps 3 and 6. Both are introduced locally, so nothing is garbled, but the reuse inside one unit is avoidable. Fix: call the sign $\sigma$ or $s$ in the reversible proof.
5. [minor] statement "Nonlinear centres in a conservative system" (line 68): "proves the theorem in full, under the extra hypothesis that ..." reads as self-cancelling. Fix: "closes that gap, and so gives a complete proof under the extra hypothesis that ...".
