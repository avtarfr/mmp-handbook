## Findings
1. [minor] remark "Where the hypotheses are used in the counting lemma" (line 155): the second counterexample, $G=\{\pm1\}$ acting on $A=\mathbb{Z}$, is said to show that "the average is not defined, although the number of orbits is". Both sides are in fact infinite here ($\sum_g|\mathrm{Fix}(g)| = \infty$, and the number of orbits $\{0\},\{\pm1\},\{\pm2\},\dots$ is countably infinite), so the example does not exhibit a mismatch the way the first one ($\infty/\infty$) does. Fix: either say plainly that the sum on the right diverges so the quotient carries no information, or replace it with an example in which $A$ is infinite but the orbit count is finite — e.g. $G=\{\pm1\}$ acting on $A=\mathbb{R}$ by $\varepsilon\cdot a=\varepsilon a$, where there are infinitely many orbits still, or better, note that no finite-orbit-count example can exist and drop the second case.
2. [minor] frontmatter, hassani-2013 (lines 14-17): `pages: "713-715"` and the role "the rotation-group examples used in the remark". Pages 713-714 are used (Defs 23.3.1, 23.3.3, Box 23.3.4, cited in the definition block), but the SO(2) examples on pp. 714-715 are not used anywhere, and no remark in the unit cites Hassani for a rotation-group example. Fix: narrow the pages to 713-714 and drop the second clause of the role, or add the Hassani SO(2) example where the remark discusses orbits.
3. [minor] frontmatter, lee-2013 (lines 18-21): the locator claims "the GL(n,R) and O(n) examples" on pp. 161-163 and the role claims "the orbits of O(n) acting on R^n". Lee's Example 7.22 on p. 163 gives the trivial action, GL(n,R) on R^n, left translation, conjugation and Z^n on R^n; there is no O(n) example on those pages, and the unit does not use one. Fix: remove the O(n) claim from the locator and the role.
