Uncountable $A$: handled, since Step 5's reindexing lets $\psi_\alpha$ be the zero function for all but countably many $\alpha$. Repeated $X_\alpha$: handled by the same device. $A$ empty: then $M=\varnothing$ and the claim is vacuous. The converse direction asserted in the first bullet of the hypotheses remark is right — $\{\psi_\alpha\neq 0\}$ is open, refines $\mathcal{X}$ (it sits inside $\operatorname{supp}\psi_\alpha\subset X_\alpha$), is locally finite and covers (since $\sum\psi_\alpha=1$), so partitions of unity for every cover force paracompactness, and the long line is indeed a Hausdorff locally Euclidean non-paracompact space. The analytic bullet also survives: if $\operatorname{supp}\psi_0\subset X_0\subsetneq M$ then $M\setminus\operatorname{supp}\psi_0$ is a non-empty open set (it contains the non-empty $M\setminus X_0$) on which $\psi_0$ vanishes, so on a connected real-analytic manifold $\psi_0\equiv 0$, likewise $\psi_1$, and the sum cannot be $1$. The two-element cover in Part 2 really is a cover, since $S\subset U$. I could not break anything.
