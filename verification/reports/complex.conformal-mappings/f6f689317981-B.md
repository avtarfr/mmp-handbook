4. [minor] `:::proof` (line 141): "a Mobius transformation fixing three points has $(az+b) - z(cz+d) = 0$, a polynomial of degree at most $2$, with three distinct roots in $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$". If one of the three fixed points is $\infty$ it is not a root of that polynomial; $\infty$ is fixed exactly when $c=0$, which drops the degree. The conclusion is right either way, and in homogeneous coordinates "root in $\widehat{\mathbb{C}}$" is standard shorthand, but the sentence as written skips the case. Fix: add half a line -- "if $\infty$ is one of them then $c=0$ and the polynomial has degree $\le 1$ with the two remaining distinct roots" -- or phrase the argument via eigenvectors of the coefficient matrix on $\mathbb{P}^1$.
5. [minor] `:::example` (line 291): "the finite-difference Laplacian [was] computed in double and in $25$-digit arithmetic and agree". The 25-digit value is right (I reproduce $1.48893\times10^{-10}$ against the stated $1.5\times10^{-10}$), but at $h=10^{-6}$ the double-precision stencil is pure cancellation noise: at $z=0.3+0.4i$ it returns exactly $0.0$, and at a nearby point ($0.3+0.41i$) it returns $-2.8\times10^{-2}$. "Agree" overstates it. Fix: say the two are both consistent with zero at that step size, or quote the double result separately.
6. [source] hassani-2013, Proposition 10.3.1(b), p. 306 -- part (b) is stated of the symbol $f$ that its own hypotheses give to the mapping, so it is vacuous read literally.
