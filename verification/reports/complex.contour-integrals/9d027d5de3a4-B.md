## Findings

1. [minor] proof, part 2, reversal sentence (line ~95): "For $-\gamma$, parametrized by $t\mapsto z(-t)$ on $[-b,-a]$, the derivative is $-z'(-t)$ and substituting $s=-t$ reverses the limits, producing one sign change from each, hence a net sign change." The bookkeeping as written does not add up: one sign change from each of two sources would cancel. There are in fact three sign factors — the derivative $-z'(-t)$, the substitution $dt = -ds$, and the reversal of the limits from $b$ to $a$ — of which the first two cancel, leaving one. The conclusion $\int_{-\gamma}f\,dz = -\int_\gamma f\,dz$ is correct (I redid it: $\int_{-b}^{-a} f(z(-t))(-z'(-t))\,dt = \int_b^a f(z(s))z'(s)\,ds = -\int_a^b f(z(s))z'(s)\,ds$). The $\lvert dz\rvert$ half of the sentence is right as it stands: there the derivative factor contributes no sign and the remaining two cancel. Fix: "three factors of $-1$ appear — the derivative, $dt=-ds$, and the reversal of the limits — and two of them cancel".

2. [minor] statement part 4, equivalence (a): "depends only on the endpoints of $\gamma$, for all paths $\gamma$ in $\Omega$". For a disconnected $\Omega$ the condition is vacuous between components, and (c) then needs a primitive built component by component. The proof handles this correctly ("Fix a point $z_0$ in a component of $\Omega$"), but the statement does not warn the reader. One clause would fix it.

3. [minor] frontmatter `rhb-2006` role mentions "worked path-dependence comparisons" for §24.8, pp. 845–848; the unit's own path-dependence example ($\bar z$ from $0$ to $1+i$, two routes) is labelled `original` and is not RHB's. Reword the role to "the explicit reduction to four real integrals; the ML inequality" and drop the comparisons.
