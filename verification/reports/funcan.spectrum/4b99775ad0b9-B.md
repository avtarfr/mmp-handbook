Reed and Simon's table on p. 194 has two rows: their $T$ (the **left** shift on $\ell_1$) with spectrum $|\lambda|\le1$, point spectrum $|\lambda|<1$, residual spectrum $\varnothing$ — this matches our second row; and their $T'$ (the **right** shift on $\ell_\infty$) with spectrum $|\lambda|\le1$, point spectrum $\varnothing$, residual spectrum $|\lambda|\le1$ — the *closed* disc. Their p. 193 proves $\{|\lambda|<1\}$ residual for $T'$ and then p. 194 proves separately, by exhibiting a ball of radius $\tfrac14$ about $c_n = \bar\lambda^{\,n}$ disjoint from $\operatorname{Ran}(\lambda-T')$, that $\{|\lambda|=1\}$ is residual for $T'$ as well. So their right-shift row has empty continuous spectrum, whereas our first row puts $|\lambda|=1$ in $\sigma_c$. Our table is the correct one for $\ell^2$ and I verified it independently (below); the difference is genuine and is caused by the Banach adjoint on $\ell_\infty$, where the range of $\lambda - T'$ fails to be dense on the unit circle while on $\ell^2$ it is dense.
