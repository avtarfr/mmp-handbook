## Findings
1. [blocking] proof "Part 7 for $C[a,b]$, and part 8", step 2 of the $d_1$ argument (line 141): the parenthetical "which is an honest metric on the piecewise-continuous functions" is false. On the piecewise-continuous functions $d_1(f,g)=\int_{-1}^{1}|f-g|$ vanishes for distinct functions — e.g. $\operatorname{sgn}$ and the function equal to it except with value $5$ at $t=0$ — so (M2) fails and $d_1$ is only a pseudometric there. The claim also contradicts the unit's own first bullet in the "Where the hypotheses are used" remark (line 147), which says precisely that $\int|f-g|=0$ for distinct functions is "the standard situation in integration theory". The argument itself is unharmed, because only the triangle inequality is used, and that does hold for a pseudometric. Fix: replace the parenthesis with "which satisfies the triangle inequality on the piecewise-continuous functions — it is a pseudometric there, and (M2) is not needed for this step".
