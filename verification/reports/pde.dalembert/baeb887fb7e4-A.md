[blocking] example "A pulse reflected from a fixed end" (line 268): "(the odd extension of a function is $C^k$ when its even-order derivatives up to order $k$ vanish at $0$; here $\varphi'''(0) = 6 \neq 0$, so it is not $C^4$)". The criterion quoted is correct, but $\varphi'''$ is an *odd*-order derivative, so its non-vanishing is irrelevant to the odd extension's smoothness; the odd extension is automatically $C^k$-compatible at every odd order. The conclusion "not $C^4$" is right; the reason given does not support it. Fix: replace "$\varphi'''(0) = 6 \neq 0$" by "$\varphi^{(4)}(0) = -24 \neq 0$".
