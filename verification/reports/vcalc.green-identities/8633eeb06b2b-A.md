As printed, the sentence attributes our weakening to Evans. Fix: "...are what that derivation actually needs; they are Evans's, except that (G1) needs only $v \in C^1$, as the remark below notes."
2. [minor] `prereqs` (line 7) and proof[uniqueness], Step 1 (line 115): the step cites `:ref[line integrals]{to=vcalc.line-integrals}` for "a function with vanishing gradient on a connected open set is constant", but `vcalc.line-integrals` is not listed in `prereqs`. The fact itself is standard multivariable calculus, so nothing is unfollowable, but the unit chooses to lean on a handbook unit for it and should then list it; `vcalc.line-integrals` precedes this unit in `content/map.yaml`, so adding it is free. Fix: add `vcalc.line-integrals` to `prereqs`.
3. [minor] remark, third bullet (line 143): the witness offered against dropping $u \in C^2(\overline V)$ is $u = \operatorname{Re}\bigl((1+z)/(1-z)\bigr)$ on the unit disc, i.e. a two-dimensional example against statements posed for $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3$. The example is genuine — on $\lvert z\rvert = 1$ one has $(1+z)/(1-z) = 2i\sin\theta/\lvert 1-z\rvert^2$, purely imaginary, so $u$ vanishes at every boundary point but $z = 1$, and $u$ is unbounded there — but the reader has to supply the bridge. Fix: add a clause, "(stated in the plane for brevity; by Evans's $n$-dimensional version the same conclusion applies in any dimension, and $u$ may be read as a function of $(x,y)$ independent of $z$ on a cylinder)", or give the three-dimensional analogue directly.
4. [minor] statement[Uniqueness...], preamble (line 94): "All solutions below are required to be of class $C^2(\overline V)$" — this is the right class, and the remark explains why, but the data classes are then slightly over-specified relative to what is used: $f$ continuous on $\overline V$ is needed only to make the Neumann compatibility integral finite, and nothing in the three uniqueness proofs uses continuity of $f$ or $h$ at all (they subtract two solutions and the data cancel). Fix: say so in half a sentence, so a reader does not think continuity of the data is doing work in Steps 2, 4 and 5.
