2. [minor] definition "The surface as a set of germs" (line 47): "A global analytic function is a connected component $\mathcal{G}_0$ of $\mathcal{G}$" followed by "Ahlfors calls $\mathcal{G}_0$ the Riemann surface of that global analytic function" reads circularly, since the component is then both the function and its own surface. Ahlfors defines a global analytic function as an equivalence class of function elements (p. 285) and only *identifies* it with the component (p. 288), after which the component is regarded as the function's domain. Fix: "a connected component can be identified with a global analytic function, and is then regarded as its domain, which Ahlfors calls its Riemann surface".
3. [minor] same block (line 47): "by construction it consists of exactly the germs obtainable from any one of them by a chain of direct continuations". Ahlfors does not get this by construction; he argues on p. 288 that the set of such germs and its complement in $\mathcal{G}_0$ are both open. Fix: "as Ahlfors shows (p. 288)".
4. [minor] statement, item 1 (line 60): "analytic there ... near any point that is not a branch point". The preceding block has just said that branch points are not points of $\mathcal{G}_0$, so at this stage the qualifier is vacuous; it only has content after item 4 completes the surface. Fix: move the qualifier into item 4, or say "at every point of $\mathcal{G}_0$, and after the completion of item 4 at every non-branch point of the completed surface".
5. [minor] statement, item 3 (line 62): Ahlfors stresses (p. 299) that $h$ depends on the choice of initial germ, different choices possibly giving different $h$. The item speaks of "the initial germ" without having introduced one. Fix: "fix an initial germ of $f$ at a point of the punctured disc and let $h$ be the least positive integer such that ...".
6. [minor] frontmatter (line 20) and the check warn: `arfken-2013 pp. 499-503 never mention rieman/surfac`. Judged a false positive. The declared role of this source is only the order convention for branch points (p. 500) and the phase table of Example 11.6.4 (pp. 502-503); the unit attributes no Riemann-surface material to Arfken, and Examples 11.6.1-11.6.4 do span pp. 499-503. Narrowing `pages` to "500, 502-503" would silence the warn but is not required.
