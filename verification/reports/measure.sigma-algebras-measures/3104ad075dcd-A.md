## Findings
1. [minor] definition (line 35): the algebra axioms are given as three conditions (non-empty, closed under complements, closed under finite unions), and the next sentence says "Three facts come free from the two axioms". Fix: say "from these axioms", or fold non-emptiness into a parenthesis so the count matches.
2. [minor] idea (line 111): the sentence describing the Borel-generator argument says "a ray is a countable union of intervals", but the proof never uses that. For the four ray families it instead writes $(a,b) = (a,\infty) \cap (-\infty,b)$ and moves between ray types by a single complement or a single countable union. Fix: replace that clause with "and the four kinds of ray are obtained from one another by one complement or one countable union, after which an open interval is the intersection of two rays".
3. [minor] method (line 208): "Without it, $\mu \equiv \infty$ would satisfy (2) vacuously". $\mu \equiv \infty$ does satisfy (2) — both sides are $+\infty$ for every disjoint sequence — but not vacuously; the hypothesis of (2) is met. Fix: drop "vacuously", or state Folland's sharper remark (p. 25) that $\mu(\varnothing)=0$ may be replaced by "$\mu(E) < \infty$ for some $E$".
