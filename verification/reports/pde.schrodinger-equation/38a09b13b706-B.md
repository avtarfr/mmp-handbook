## Findings
1. [minor] example[The harmonic oscillator] (line 370): "the series exceeds $C\mathrm{e}^{cs^2}$ minus the finitely many terms below $k_0$" is not quite what the comparison gives. The term-by-term domination only starts at $k_0$, so what is proved is $v(s) \ge C\,\mathrm{e}^{cs^2} - P(s)$ where $P$ is the polynomial formed by the terms of $C\,\mathrm{e}^{cs^2}$ below $k_0$ (together with the actual $a_ks^k$ below $k_0$, which are a polynomial too). The conclusion is unaffected, since $P(s)\mathrm{e}^{-s^2/2}\to0$. Fix: "the series exceeds $C\mathrm{e}^{cs^2} - P(s)$ for a polynomial $P$ collecting the terms below $k_0$, and hence $|\tilde\psi(s)| \ge \mathrm{e}^{(c-1/2)s^2} - P(s)\mathrm{e}^{-s^2/2} \to \infty$". One clause.
