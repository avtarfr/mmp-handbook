## Findings
1. [source] note "The same four modes in probability" (line 41): the unit writes the $r$th-mean
   integrability condition as $\mathbb{E}|X_n^r| < \infty$, with the exponent inside the modulus.
   This is a faithful transcription of Grimmett and Stirzaker, Definition (1b), p. 352, which writes
   exactly that, and of their norm $\|Y\|_r = (\mathbb{E}|Y^r|)^{1/r}$ on p. 353 — even though the
   displayed integral on the same line of p. 353 uses $|y|^r$. The two forms agree numerically for
   real $Y$ and integer $r$, so nothing downstream is affected; it is the book's notation that is
   loose, not our mathematics. Recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs`, not failed.
2. [minor] method (lines 164, 169) and application (line 225): steps 4 and 5 of the method invoke
   "part 3" for passing from $L^p$ or in-measure convergence to an a.e. subsequence, and "part 6"
   for settling "the $L^p$ question", but parts 3 and 6 as stated in the statement block are about
   $L^1$ only. The application repeats this ("Part 3 supplies exactly that: $L^p$ convergence yields
   an a.e. convergent subsequence"). The claims are true for every $p$, but the route is part 1
   followed by part 2, not part 3. The example block already flags the $L^1$-only scope of part 6
   explicitly, so the unit is aware of the distinction; the method and application are just loose
   about which part does the work. Fix: cite "parts 1 and 2" in method step 5 and in the
   application, and say "the $L^1$ question" (or "parts 1, 2 and 6") in method step 4.
3. [minor] frontmatter (line 11): the locator "Exercises 32–39, pp. 63–64" is a page wider than it
   needs to be. Exercises 32 through 43 are all on printed p. 63; p. 64 carries Exercise 44 (Lusin)
   and then §2.5 on product measures. The cited pages exist and treat the topic, and Exercise 39
   (the one actually used, for part 5) is on p. 63, so nothing is misattributed. Fix: "Exercises
   32–39, p. 63".
