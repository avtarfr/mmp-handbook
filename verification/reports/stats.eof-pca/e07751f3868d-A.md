## Findings
1. [minor] proof, Part 1, uniqueness paragraph (line 119): the sentence "any admissible $e_1,\dots,e_{k-1}$ span the sum of the eigenspaces of $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_{k-1}$" is asserted, not derived. It needs the induction that *every* admissible $e_j$ is an eigenvector of $S$ for $\lambda_j$. The equality analysis two sentences earlier does give this, but it is carried out in the one eigenbasis fixed at the start of the proof, whereas the sentence quantifies over arbitrary admissible prefixes. The claim is true (the orthogonal complement of a span of eigenvectors is $S$-invariant, and the restricted Rayleigh quotient has maximum $\lambda_j$ attained only in the $\lambda_j$-eigenspace), so this is a presentation gap, not an error. Fix: add one sentence noting that the equality condition applies at every step $j$, so by induction each admissible $e_j$ is an eigenvector with eigenvalue $\lambda_j$, and the span is then forced by a dimension count.
