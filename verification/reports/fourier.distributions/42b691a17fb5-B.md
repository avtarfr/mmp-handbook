## Findings
1. [minor] frontmatter, `folland-1999` loc (line 23): the locator reads "Propositions 9.1–9.2 with the resulting definition of the support of a distribution". Proposition 9.2 (p. 284) is what yields the support definition; Proposition 9.1 (p. 283) is the statement that $f_t \to a\delta$ in $\mathcal{D}'$ and has nothing to do with support. Fix: split the two, e.g. "Proposition 9.1 (approximate identities) and Proposition 9.2 with the resulting definition of the support of a distribution", or drop 9.1 from that clause.
2. [minor] definition "The distributional derivative" (line 127): the hypothesis "$f \in C^1(\mathbb{R})$ with $f' \in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}$" is redundant — $f \in C^1$ already makes $f'$ continuous and hence locally integrable. Stakgold and Holst (p. 101) say "differentiable function whose first derivative is locally integrable", where the second clause does work. Fix: either say "differentiable with $f' \in L^1_{\mathrm{loc}}$" (the book's hypothesis) or just "$f \in C^1$".
3. [minor] definition "The distributional derivative" (line 148): "Term-by-term differentiation of a Fourier series, illegitimate pointwise, is therefore always legitimate in $\mathcal{D}'$". The preceding sentence conditions it correctly, but "always" overstates: what is proved is that *if* the partial sums converge in $\mathcal{D}'$ then the differentiated series converges to the derivative. Fix: "legitimate in $\mathcal{D}'$ whenever the series itself converges there".
4. [minor] definition "Test functions" (line 43): "every derivative of it is a rational function of $\lvert x\rvert$" — the derivatives are rational in the Cartesian components as well as in $r=\lvert x\rvert$; for $n>1$ "a rational function of $x$ with poles only on $\lvert x\rvert = 1$" would be accurate. Not wrong for the radial profile, loose in $\mathbb{R}^n$.
5. [minor] `tools/check` reports "prerequisite measure.lebesgue-integral comes after fourier.distributions in the map". This is a global map-ordering issue affecting ~45 units across the handbook, not a defect of this unit, and the drafter was instructed not to touch content/map.yaml.
