## Findings

1. [blocking] remark "Where the hypotheses are used, and the conventions", bullet **A further convention** (line 248): the stated conversion phase for the $x'$-middle Euler convention has the wrong sign. The text says the dictionary $(\varphi,\theta,\psi)=(\alpha+\pi/2,\ \beta,\ \gamma-\pi/2)$ "multiplies $D^{j}_{m'm}$ by $\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(m'-m)\pi/2}$ and spoils the reality of the middle matrix". The object that stops being real is that convention's reduced matrix $d^{x,j}_{m'm}(\theta)=\langle jm'|\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\theta J_x}|jm\rangle$, and the correct relation is
   $$d^{x,j}_{m'm}(\theta)=\mathrm{e}^{+\mathrm{i}(m'-m)\pi/2}\,d^{j}_{m'm}(\theta),$$
   because $\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\theta J_x}=\mathrm{e}^{+\mathrm{i}\pi J_z/2}\,\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\theta J_y}\,\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}\pi J_z/2}$ in our own sign convention. The stated factor is the complex conjugate of the right one; the two agree only when $m'-m$ is even and are opposite whenever $m'-m$ is odd. Concretely, at $j=\tfrac12$, $m'=-\tfrac12$, $m=+\tfrac12$, $\theta=\pi/2$: $d^{1/2}_{-1/2,1/2}=1/\sqrt2=+0.70711$ while $\langle-\tfrac12|\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(\pi/2)J_x}|\tfrac12\rangle=-\mathrm{i}/\sqrt2$, so the true ratio is $-\mathrm{i}$, whereas the unit's $\mathrm{e}^{-\mathrm{i}(m'-m)\pi/2}=\mathrm{e}^{+\mathrm{i}\pi/2}=+\mathrm{i}$. Numerically over $j=\tfrac12,1,\tfrac32,2$ at $\theta=0.83$ the ratio $d^{x,j}_{m'm}/d^{j}_{m'm}$ equals $\mathrm{e}^{+\mathrm{i}(m'-m)\pi/2}$ to $2.4\times10^{-16}$. The block is `provenance=original`, so this is our own material; Arfken's Exercise 3.4.1 (p. 142) supplies only the angle dictionary, which the unit reports correctly. Fix: write $\mathrm{e}^{+\mathrm{i}(m'-m)\pi/2}$, and say explicitly which matrix is being multiplied — $D^{j}(R)$ itself is unchanged, since it is the same physical rotation in two parametrisations; only the reduced matrix changes, $d^{x,j}_{m'm}(\theta)=\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}(m'-m)\pi/2}d^{j}_{m'm}(\theta)$.

2. Fix: "one column sum".

3. [minor] definition "Euler angles", last sentence (line 45): "his $(\psi,\theta,\varphi)$ is our $(\alpha,\beta,\gamma)$" states only the role each angle plays. The matrices do not agree at equal angles: Hassani's $\exp(M_{12}\psi)=\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}\psi J_z}$ is our $R_z(-\psi)$, so his $R(\psi,\theta,\varphi)$ is our $R(-\psi,-\theta,-\varphi)$, as the conventions remark later says correctly. Fix: add "up to the sign of the angles — see the remark on conventions" here, so the two passages cannot be read as clashing.

(30.16) without proof, attributing it to Hamermesh; the symmetric-power / monomial-expansion derivation given here is ours. The statement proved is indeed Hassani's, so `adapted` errs on the safe side, but a one-line note that the proof route is our own would be more honest. Not blocking.
