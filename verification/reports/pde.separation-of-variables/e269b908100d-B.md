## Findings

1. [blocking] note[Catalogue of separated equations], "Cylindrical polars" (lines 113–125): the three displayed separated equations are mutually inconsistent, and the axial factor contradicts the cited source. The unit writes the axial equation as $Z'' = \alpha^2 Z$ and the axial factor as $[Ee^{\alpha z}+Fe^{-\alpha z}]$, but gives the radial equation as $\rho^2P''+\rho P'+[(k^2-\alpha^2)\rho^2-m^2]P = 0$ with radial argument $\sqrt{k^2-\alpha^2}$. Substituting $F = P\Phi Z$ into $\nabla^2F+k^2F=0$ and multiplying by $\rho^2$ gives
   $$\rho^2P''+\rho P' + \bigl[(k^2 + Z''/Z)\rho^2 - m^2\bigr]P = 0,$$
   so $Z''/Z = +\alpha^2$ forces $(k^2+\alpha^2)$ and the argument $\sqrt{k^2+\alpha^2}$, while $\sqrt{k^2-\alpha^2}$ requires $Z''/Z = -\alpha^2$. Riley, Hobson and Bence, p. 740, have the second convention: their separated solution is $AJ_m(\sqrt{k^2-\alpha^2}\rho)+BY_m(\sqrt{k^2-\alpha^2}\rho)$ times $(C\cos m\varphi+D\sin m\varphi)$ times $[E\exp(i\alpha z)+F\exp(-i\alpha z)]$ — exponentials in $i\alpha z$, not $\alpha z$. Fix: write the axial equation as $Z''+\alpha^2Z = 0$ and the axial factor as $[Ee^{i\alpha z}+Fe^{-i\alpha z}]$, keeping $\sqrt{k^2-\alpha^2}$ as RHB do. (This repair is also what makes the next sentence correct: with $\alpha = ik$ and $k_{\text{Helmholtz}}=0$ one gets $J_m(k\rho)$ with $e^{\mp kz}$, which is RHB's Laplace solution (21.35), p. 729. With the unit's present $Z''=\alpha^2Z$ that sentence does not follow either.)

6. [minor] note[Catalogue of separated equations], Cartesian paragraph (line 103): "*Laplace's equation in a box always produces hyperbolic functions in at least one direction*".
