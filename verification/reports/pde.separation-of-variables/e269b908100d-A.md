## Findings
1. [blocking] note (lines 116, 122) and example (line 292): the cylindrical entry of the catalogue is internally inconsistent in the sign of the axial separation constant. It states $Z'' = \alpha^2 Z$ with axial factor $Ee^{\alpha z} + Fe^{-\alpha z}$, but then gives the radial equation $\rho^2P'' + \rho P' + [(k^2-\alpha^2)\rho^2 - m^2]P = 0$. Substituting $u = P\Phi Z$ into $\nabla^2F + k^2F = 0$ and dividing by $F$ gives $\frac{(\rho P')'}{\rho P} + \frac{\Phi''}{\rho^2\Phi} + \frac{Z''}{Z} + k^2 = 0$, so with $Z''/Z = +\alpha^2$ the radial coefficient is $k^2 + \alpha^2$, not $k^2 - \alpha^2$. Riley, Hobson & Bence (p. 740) have $[E\exp(i\alpha z) + F\exp(-i\alpha z)]$, i.e. $Z'' + \alpha^2 Z = 0$, which is what makes their $\sqrt{k^2-\alpha^2}$ correct; the unit has kept their radial argument and flipped their axial equation. The error also makes the catalogue contradict the unit's own cylindrical example, which sets $\alpha^2 = -(p\pi/L)^2$ for $Z = \sin(p\pi z/L)$ and then uses $\kappa^2 = k^2 - p^2\pi^2/L^2$ — which is $k^2 + \alpha^2$, not the catalogue's $k^2 - \alpha^2$. (The subsequent remark about $I_m, K_m$ appearing when $k^2 < \alpha^2$ also only makes sense in the $Z''+\alpha^2Z=0$ convention.) Fix: write $\Phi'' + m^2\Phi = 0$, $Z'' + \alpha^2 Z = 0$, $\rho^2P'' + \rho P' + [(k^2-\alpha^2)\rho^2 - m^2]P = 0$ with axial factor $Ee^{i\alpha z} + Fe^{-i\alpha z}$, as RHB have it; then correct the example to $\alpha = p\pi/L$ (its $\kappa^2$ is already right), and recheck the Laplace specialisation, which currently reads correctly only because it renames the axial constant.
