4. [blocking] statement "The separated solutions in spherical polars" (lines 139-147): the block contradicts itself about where integrality comes from. So as written the statement is false — $r^{-1/2}P^0_{-1/2}(\cos\theta)$ is a product solution of $\nabla^2u=0$ off the axis with $\ell = -\tfrac12$, and a non-integer $m$ gives one on any domain not encircling the axis.
