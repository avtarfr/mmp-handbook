## Findings
1. [minor] statement "The separation lemma" (line 57) versus proof, Steps 1 and 3 (lines 202, 240): the lemma is stated for real-valued $F : A\to\mathbb{R}$ and $G : B\to\mathbb{R}$, but it is applied to $F(r)=\frac{1}{R}(r^2R')'$, to $\Phi''/\Phi$ and to the $\theta$-group, which are complex-valued in general — part 1 of the spherical statement says explicitly that $m^2$ and $\lambda=\ell(\ell+1)$ are "complex numbers, subject to no further restriction", and $\Phi=\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}m\varphi}$ is the ordinary case. So the lemma is used outside its stated hypotheses. Nothing mathematical is missing — Steps 1 and 2 of its two-line proof never use the order or the realness of the values, and work verbatim for maps into $\mathbb{C}$ (or into any abelian group) — which is why this is minor rather than blocking: the repair is to write $\mathbb{C}$ in place of $\mathbb{R}$ in the lemma's statement, and the proof needs no change at all.

2. [minor] application "Normal modes and resonant cavities" (line 418): the paragraph motivates the scalar Dirichlet problem as "an acoustic cavity with pressure-release walls" or Arfken's particle in a cylindrical box, and then immediately computes $\omega/2\pi$ with $c=2.998\times10^{10}\,\mathrm{cm\,s^{-1}}$, the speed of light. The arithmetic is right ($ck/2\pi=13.71$ GHz for $k=2.8723835\,\mathrm{cm}^{-1}$), and the choice is clearly deliberate — it is what makes the $13.71$ GHz comparable with the $11.47$ GHz of TM$_{010}$ in the next paragraph — but neither of the two physical readings just offered propagates at $c$: an air-filled acoustic cavity of these dimensions resonates near $16$ kHz, and the particle-in-a-box reading has no $\omega=ck$ at all. Fix: say why $c$ is taken to be the speed of light, e.g. "take the scalar field to propagate at the speed of light, so that the number can be set beside the electromagnetic cavity below".

3. [minor] note "Catalogue of separated equations", frontmatter `sources` for arfken-2013 (line 19): the locator gives "Eqs. (9.74)–(9.84), pp. 424–427", and the `src` of the note block (line 94) repeats it. Equation (9.74) first appears on p. 425, not p. 424; p. 424 carries the "Spherical Polar Coordinates" heading and Eqs. (9.67)–(9.72). The range is not wrong — it contains everything cited — but "pp. 425–427" would match the equations named, with p. 424 cited for the heading as is done for §9.4 itself. Everything else in the Arfken locator checks out exactly (see below).
