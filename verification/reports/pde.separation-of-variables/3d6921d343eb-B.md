1. [minor] application[Normal modes and resonant cavities]: "The lowest mode of this cylinder is therefore TM$_{010}$ ... $11.47$ GHz, independent of the height $L$". The frequency of TM$_{010}$ is indeed independent of $L$, but which mode is lowest is not: TM$_{010}$ beats TE$_{111}$ only while $L/a\lesssim2.03$, and this cylinder sits just inside that at $L/a=2$ ($k=2.4048$ against $2.4202\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}$, a $0.6\%$ margin). A reader could carry "TM$_{010}$ is the lowest, independent of $L$" away. Fix: "whose frequency is independent of the height; for a taller cylinder, $L/a\gtrsim2.03$, TE$_{111}$ drops below it".
2. [minor] note[Catalogue], cylindrical entry: the displayed general solution writes the axial factor as $Ee^{\alpha z}+Fe^{-\alpha z}$, which is correct but degenerates at $\alpha=0$, where the second solution is $z$ rather than a second exponential. The Cartesian entry takes care to record the $l^2=0$ case ($A+Bx$); the cylindrical and spherical entries do not. Fix: one clause noting the $\alpha=0$ (and $m=0$) degenerate cases, as the statement block already does for $\ell=-\tfrac12$ and $m=0$.
