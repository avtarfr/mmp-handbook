## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Parts 4 and 5", the projection-form paragraph (line 167): the index translation asserts that "part 2 produces the $e_{n}$ grouped by eigenvalue in non-increasing order of modulus", so that the span of the first $m$ eigenspaces is exactly $M_{k}$. That grouping is not forced when two *distinct* eigenvalues share a modulus — $\lambda$ and $-\lambda$ with unequal multiplicities, say — because part 2 is then free to interleave them. The conclusion is unaffected: $A-\sum_{j\le m}\lambda_{j}P_{j}=\sum_{j>m}\lambda_{j}P_{j}$ has norm $\sup_{j>m}\lvert\lambda_{j}\rvert=\lvert\lambda_{m+1}\rvert$ either way (checked numerically, see below). Fix: add half a sentence saying the extremal principles may be run so as to exhaust each eigenspace before moving on, or derive the norm directly from $\sum_{j>m}\lambda_{j}P_{j}$.
2. [minor] remark, first bullet (line 177): in the singular-value display the numbers are written $\lambda_{n}$, the same letter that denotes the eigenvalues of $A$ everywhere else in the unit, and here they are positive by construction while the unit's $\lambda_{n}$ may be negative. Fix: write $s_{n}$ (the letter already used for the Volterra singular values in :ref[]{to=funcan.compact-operators}) and note that Reed and Simon call them $\lambda_{n}$.
3. [minor] note block (line 141): "We take them from Reed and Simon's Theorem VI.15, Hassani's Theorem 17.5.11 and Evans's Theorem 6." Reed and Simon's VI.15 carries the discreteness, eigenvalue and finite-multiplicity clauses but not $0\in\sigma(A)$; Hassani 17.5.11(1) and Evans Theorem 6(i) carry that one. Fix: attribute the two clauses separately.
