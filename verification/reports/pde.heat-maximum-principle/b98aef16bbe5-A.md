## Checked
I went through the proof line by line. Step 1: at an interior maximum in $x$ each one-variable second derivative is $\le0$, so $\Delta v(P)\le0$; for $0<t_0<T$ the time derivative vanishes, for $t_0=T$ the difference quotients $(v(x_0,T)-v(x_0,T-\delta))/\delta$ are $\ge0$ so the left derivative is $\ge0$; both cases give $v_t-k\Delta v\ge0$, contradicting the strict inequality, using $k>0$ exactly where the text says.
