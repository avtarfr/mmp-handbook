## Findings
1. [minor] idea (line 63): "a maximum there is one-sided in $t$ and gives only $u_t \ge 0$ — which is still the wrong sign for the contradiction, so the argument survives". As written this reads as self-defeating: $v_t \ge 0$ is precisely the sign that produces the contradiction with $v_t - k\Delta v < 0$, and the remark at line 129 says so in the opposite words ("which has the same sign as the two-sided conclusion $v_t = 0$ needs"). Fix: "gives only $u_t \ge 0$ — but that is still enough, since the contradiction needs only $v_t \ge 0 \ge k\Delta v$".
2. [minor] remark (line 129): "which has the same sign as the two-sided conclusion $v_t = 0$ needs" — $v_t = 0$ has no sign. Fix: "and $v_t \ge 0$ is all the contradiction uses; the two-sided case gives the stronger $v_t = 0$".
