The correct relation, and the one that actually follows from item 2 of this unit's own statement block, is $K_\nu' = -K_{\nu+1} + \tfrac{\nu}{x}K_\nu$. Derivation from item 2: $K_{\nu-1} = K_{\nu+1} - \tfrac{2\nu}{x}K_\nu$, substituted into $K_{\nu-1}+K_{\nu+1} = -2K_\nu'$, gives $2K_{\nu+1} - \tfrac{2\nu}{x}K_\nu = -2K_\nu'$, i.e. $K_\nu' = -K_{\nu+1} + \tfrac{\nu}{x}K_\nu$. The display then adds $+\tfrac{2\nu}{x}I_\nu K_\nu$ with no derivation, and only that insertion produces the stated conclusion. With the correct $K_\nu'$ the two $\tfrac{\nu}{x}I_\nu K_\nu$ terms cancel against each other and the step is clean with no extra term.
