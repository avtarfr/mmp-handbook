3. [minor] application, first bullet (line 119): "the ratio of potential energy to mass-weighted kinetic energy" misnames the denominator. The denominator of $\mathcal{R}$ is $\int_a^b w u^2\,dx$, which is not a kinetic energy — the kinetic energy of the mode is $\tfrac12\omega^2\int w u^2$, so the denominator is the kinetic energy divided by $\omega^2$ (what the vibration literature calls the *reference* kinetic energy). As written the sentence would make $\omega_1^2$ dimensionless. Fix: "to the reference kinetic energy $\tfrac12\int w u^2\,dx$, that is, the kinetic energy divided by $\omega^2$".
