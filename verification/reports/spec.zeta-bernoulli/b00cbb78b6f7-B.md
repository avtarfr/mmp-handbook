The tail formula is right; the conclusion drawn from it is wrong, and wrong by more than an order of magnitude. At $s=3$ the tail after $N$ terms is $N^{-2}/2$, so an absolute error below $5\times10^{-4}$ (four significant figures of $\zeta(3)=1.2020569$) is reached at $N=32$, and below $10^{-4}$ at $N=71$.
