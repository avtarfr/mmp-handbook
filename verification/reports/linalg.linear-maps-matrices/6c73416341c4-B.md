## Findings
1. [minor] definition "Linear map, kernel, image" (line 35): the notation sentence "We write $Tv$ rather than $T(v)$ except where brackets are needed for grouping" tracks Lee's parenthetical on p. 622 ("...unless parentheses are needed for grouping") almost word for word in its second half. The block is correctly labelled `adapted` and the overlap check does not flag it, and a notational convention carries little expression, so this is not blocking. Fix: recast as e.g. "Parentheses are kept only for grouping, so $Tv$ means $T(v)$."
2. [minor] proof of "Matrices represent linear maps faithfully" (line 75): "Linearity, used $n-1$ times" undercounts — passing from $T(\sum_j v_j e_j)$ to $\sum_j v_j Te_j$ uses additivity $n-1$ times *and* homogeneity $n$ times, and the count is vacuous at $n=1$. Fix: "by linearity, applied to the finite sum" or name both halves.
3. [minor] statement "Matrices represent linear maps faithfully" (line 62): part 1 is stated under the preamble's blanket finite-dimensionality of $V$, $W$, $U$, but needs only that $E$ be a basis of $V$ — and the proof says so ("Only linearity and the spanning half of 'basis' were used here"). Not wrong, just weaker than the proof delivers. Fix: optionally note that part 1 holds for any basis, finite or not.
