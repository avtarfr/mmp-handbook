## Findings
1. [minor] statement "Characterisation" (line 44): the sentence "The same four statements are equivalent for a real matrix $Q$ with $Q^{\mathsf T} Q = I$, real vectors and the real inner product $x^{\mathsf T} y$" is circular as written, because $Q^{\mathsf T} Q = I$ *is* item 1 of the list being asserted equivalent. Nothing mathematically wrong is being claimed, but the real case is stated as an equivalence with one of its members assumed. Fix: "The same four statements are equivalent for a real matrix $Q$, with $Q^{\mathsf T} Q = I$ in place of item 1, real vectors, and the real inner product $x^{\mathsf T} y$."
2. [minor] frontmatter `summary` (line 6) against statement "Rotations and reflections", part 3 (lines 76-80): "the real ones reduce to rotations and reflections" is exact for $n = 2$ (part 2) but not for what part 3 states in general. Part 3 gives a block form $\operatorname{diag}(1,\dots,1,-1,\dots,-1,R(\theta_1),\dots,R(\theta_m))$, and an element of $\mathrm{O}(n)$, $n \ge 3$, need be neither a rotation nor a reflection: $-I_3$ has determinant $-1$ and no fixed hyperplane, so it is not a reflection, and $\operatorname{diag}(R(\theta), -1)$ is a rotary reflection. Fix: "...and that the real ones reduce to blocks of plane rotations and $\pm 1$, which for $n = 2$ are exactly the rotations and reflections."
3. [minor] note (line 84) and remark "Real versus complex diagonalization" (line 98): the term **normal** carries the remark's claim ("A unitary matrix is normal, hence unitarily diagonalizable over $\mathbb{C}$"), is not defined in the unit, and `linalg.normal-matrices` is not in `prereqs` (lines 7). It is cross-referenced in the note, and the claim is an aside rather than load-bearing for any statement in the unit, which is why this is minor and not blocking. Fix: either add `linalg.normal-matrices` to `prereqs`, or give the one-line condition $U U^\dagger = U^\dagger U$ in the remark where the word first does work.
