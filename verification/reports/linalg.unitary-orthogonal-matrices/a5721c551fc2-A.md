## Findings
1. [blocking] remark "Conventions and traps", paragraph "Preserving the norm requires all $x$" (line 85): the counterexample is wrong. The matrix given is $M=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$, and $Me_2$ is its second column $(1,1)^{\mathsf T}$, with $\|Me_2\| = \sqrt 2 \neq 1$. So $M$ does **not** "preserve the length of $e_2$"; it kills $e_1$ and stretches $e_2$, which demonstrates nothing about part 4 of the characterisation (the point being made — that norm preservation on some vectors is not enough — is right, but this matrix does not witness it). Verified numerically: $Me_1=(0,0)$, $\|Me_2\|=1.41421356$. Fix: use $M=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$, for which $\|Me_2\|=\|e_2\|=1$ and $Me_1=0$, so $M$ preserves the length of $e_2$ (indeed of the whole line $\mathbb{R}e_2$) yet is not unitary.
2. [minor] remark, paragraph "\"Orthogonal matrix\" is not \"matrix with orthogonal columns\"" (line 83): "A matrix with merely orthogonal columns satisfies $Q^{\mathsf T}Q = D$ with $D$ diagonal and positive" is false if a column is zero — the zero vector is orthogonal to everything, and then $D$ has a zero entry and $Q$ is singular, so it does not merely "scale lengths". Fix: say "with $D$ diagonal and non-negative, positive exactly when no column vanishes", or require non-zero pairwise-orthogonal columns.
3. [minor] statement "Characterisation" (line 44): the block asserts the equivalence over $\mathbb{C}$ but justifies "4 implies 3" only with the real polarization identity. Over $\mathbb{C}$ the real identity recovers $\operatorname{Re}(x,y)$ only; the imaginary part needs a second application with $ix$ in place of $x$. Fix: give the complex polarization identity, or say explicitly that the displayed identity is the real case and the complex case needs the $x \mapsto ix$ variant.
4. [minor] statement "Rotations and reflections", part 3 (real canonical form): the $\theta_j$ are unconstrained, so the form is not canonical as stated — $R(0)=I$ and $R(\pi)=-I$ are already covered by the $\pm 1$ blocks, and the same $Q$ admits several such presentations. Hassani Thm 6.6.12 is equally silent on p. 201, so this is faithful to the source and is not blocking. Fix, to make the decomposition unique up to order: restrict to $\theta_j \in (0,\pi)$.
