## Findings
1. [source] definition, "Normal curvature" (line 39) and proof Part 2 (line 88): do Carmo's
   Definition 3 (p. 143) sets $k_n = k\cos\theta$ with $\cos\theta = \langle\mathbf{n},N\rangle$,
   where $\mathbf{n}$ is the principal normal of $C$. Where the curvature $k$ of $C$ vanishes
   $\mathbf{n}$ is undefined and the product is formally $0\cdot(\text{undefined})$; the intended
   value is $0$, and the equivalent $k_n = \langle\boldsymbol{\gamma}'',N\rangle$ (which p. 144 and
   our Part 2 in fact use) has no such gap. Our text reports the source faithfully, so this is not a
   failure. Recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs`.
2. [minor] definition `src` (line 24): the range "Defs. 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 and 8, pp. 141–149" starts at
   p. 141, but the earliest named item, Definition 2, is on p. 143 (p. 141 is Example 3, the
   cylinder). The block also states "line of curvature", which is do Carmo's Definition 5, p. 147,
   not named in the locator. Fix: "pp. 143–149" and add "Def. 5, p. 147".
3. [minor] application (lines 197–204): the block is `provenance=original` (correct under
   `docs/COPYRIGHT_POLICY.md`, which makes applications original) but it reproduces Gelfand &
   Fomin's Euler equation (25) and Evans' divergence expression and names both authors in prose
   without an inline locator. Both works are in the frontmatter `sources` with `loc`/`pages`/`role`
   that cover exactly these two items, so the attribution exists. Fix: add
   `:cite{work=gelfand-fomin-1963 loc="p. 24"}` and `:cite{work=evans-2010 loc="pp. 430–431"}` at
   those two sentences.
4. [minor] example, Step 5 (line 194): the chart is singular at $r=0$ ($EG-F^2 = r^2(1+r^2)$), so
   "the vertex is an umbilical point" is a statement about the limit plus the continuity of $k_1,k_2$
   on $S$, not a value computed in this chart. The remark block already flags the general point; one
   half-sentence here ("by continuity, since the chart misses $r=0$") would close the gap.
