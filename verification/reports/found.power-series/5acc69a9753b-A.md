## Findings
1. [minor] proof of "Termwise differentiation and integration", step 3 (the sentence beginning "With
   $|w_0^{n-1-j}|\le\rho^{n-1-j}$"): the stated intermediate bound drops a factor $|w-w_0|$. From
   $|w^j-w_0^{j}|\le j\rho^{j-1}|w-w_0|$ and $|w_0^{n-1-j}|\le\rho^{n-1-j}$ the inner sum is bounded
   by $|w-w_0|\,\rho^{n-2}\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}j = \tfrac12 n(n-1)\rho^{n-2}|w-w_0|$, not by
   $\tfrac12 n(n-1)\rho^{n-2}$. The displayed conclusion $\le\tfrac12 K|w-w_0|^2$ is the correct one
   and uses the factor, so this is a dropped factor in the prose only. Fix: insert $|w-w_0|$ in that
   bound.
2. [minor] note "The complex-path case of part 2 is deferred": "it depends on this unit in turn: the
   complex exponential is *defined* by its power series there" points at the wrong unit. The
   exponential is defined by its series in complex.elementary-functions (whose `prereqs` include
   found.power-series); complex.contour-integrals depends on found.power-series only transitively,
   through `prereqs: [complex.elementary-functions, ...]`. The circularity claim itself is correct --
   I checked the two frontmatter `prereqs` lists -- but as written the reader is told the definition
   lives in complex.contour-integrals. Fix: "because it depends on this unit in turn, through
   complex.elementary-functions, where the complex exponential is defined by its power series".
3. [minor] statement "Abel's limit theorem": "along any approach for which $|1-z|/(1-|z|)$ stays
   bounded, that is, within a **Stolz angle**" presents the two descriptions as the same set. They
   agree only near $z=1$ and within the disc -- which is exactly what the proof's penultimate
   paragraph establishes ("once $|1-z|\le\cos\theta$ the quotient is at most $2/\cos\theta$"); the
   full sector also contains points with $|z|>1$ where $f$ is undefined. Fix: "equivalently, for $z$
   in the disc close enough to $1$, within a Stolz angle", or point at the proof's last-but-one
   paragraph for the equivalence.
