## Findings

1. [minor] example "Abel's theorem at a boundary point", step 1 (line 264): uniform convergence of
   $\sum(-1)^n t^{3n}$ is established on $[0,\rho]$ with $\rho<1$, and term-by-term integration is then
   claimed "over $[0,x]$ for $\lvert x\rvert < 1$", which includes negative $x$, where $[0,\rho]$ is not
   the relevant interval. The claim is true (the M-test with $M_n = \lvert x\rvert^{3n}$ works on
   $[-\lvert x\rvert, \lvert x\rvert]$), and only $x \to 1^-$ is used later. Fix: state the M-test on
   $[-\rho,\rho]$, or restrict the step to $0 \le x < 1$.

2. [minor] proof of the convergence theorem, part 3 (line 84): the proof points forward to "the first
   three series of the remark below", and part 4 points forward to the next statement's proof. Both
   are honest and clear, but a reader meets the dependency before the material. Fix: a half-sentence
   saying the three examples are collected in the remark, or move part 3's justification there.

3. [source] hassani-2013, §10.6.1 Thm 10.6.2, p. 320: Hassani asserts uniform convergence "for all
   points within its circle of convergence", that is on the whole open disc, which $\sum z^n$
   contradicts (tail $\rho^N/(1-\rho)$ at $z=\rho$, unbounded as $\rho \to 1^-$). The unit states the
   Ahlfors $\rho$-form, says in its second remark bullet that this is where its two sources differ,
   says which one it follows, and restricts the Hassani locator on that statement to part 4. That is
   exactly what `source-authority.md` requires of two conflicting sources. Already on file in
   `verification/discrepancies.md`; no new entry added. Not blocking.

4. [source] hassani-2013, §10.6.1 Thm 10.6.3, p. 321: termwise integration is asserted along "any path
   lying in the circle of convergence", with no rectifiability condition, although the length estimate
   that proves it needs a finite length. The unit states Hassani's unqualified form in the statement
   and supplies piecewise smoothness in step 6 of the proof, saying there that it is doing so. Already
   on file in `verification/discrepancies.md`. Not blocking.
