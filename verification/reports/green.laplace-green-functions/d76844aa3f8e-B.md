[minor] note "Sign and naming conventions" (line 39): the reason offered for Strauss's naming — that he calls his $G$ "the Green's function for the operator $-\Delta$" "because he thinks of the Dirichlet problem in the form $-\Delta u =$ source" — is not supported by the cited source. Strauss's Poisson problem is written $\Delta u = f$ (Theorem 2, p. 190), and his Theorem 1 is for $\Delta u = 0$; the naming is his own and he gives no such reason. Everything else in the sentence (his $G$ equals ours; he names the operator $-\Delta$) is right, and nothing mathematical depends on it. Fix: drop the explanatory clause, or report the naming without rationalising it.
2. [minor] proof of symmetry, Step 3, second bullet (line 222): the order count "$O(\varepsilon^{-1})\cdot O(1)\cdot O(\varepsilon^{2}) = O(\varepsilon)$ for the same reason, with the roles exchanged" is not the right reason for this term. In $-\oint w\,\partial H_{\mathbf a}/\partial n\, dS$ both factors are bounded near $\mathbf a$ ($w$ is $C^2$ there and $H_{\mathbf a}$ is the corrector), so the term is $O(\varepsilon^{2})$, not merely $O(\varepsilon)$; no factor blows up like $\varepsilon^{-1}$. The stated bound is still true (a bounded quantity is $O(\varepsilon^{-1})$) and the conclusion is unaffected. The same bullet list is introduced as "the four pieces of $A_\varepsilon$" but then gives three items, the first of which silently covers two. Fix: bound this term as $O(1)\cdot O(1)\cdot O(\varepsilon^2)$, and either say "three groups" or split $v\,\partial w/\partial n$ into its $F_{\mathbf a}$ and $H_{\mathbf a}$ parts.
3. [minor] proof of uniqueness of $G_D$ (line 236) has no `idea` block before it, which `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` ("Idea first") asks of every proof. The proof is three lines and its idea is one sentence ("the singularities cancel, so the difference is harmonic with zero boundary values"). Fix: add a one-sentence `idea`, or fold that sentence into the proof's opening.
