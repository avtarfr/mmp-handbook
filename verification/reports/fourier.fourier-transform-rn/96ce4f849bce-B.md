## Findings
1. [blocking] note (line 56): "Arfken's opposite sign is invisible on radial functions, because their transforms are even (this is the corollary below with $R = -I$ for odd $n$ ...)". The parity is backwards. $-I$ has $\det(-I) = (-1)^n$, so $-I$ is a rotation exactly when $n$ is **even**; for odd $n$ (in particular $n = 3$, the dimension of every example in this unit) $\det(-I) = -1$ and $-I$ is not a rotation, so rule 4 as stated does not apply to it. The conclusion drawn is nevertheless true, by two routes the unit already has: $\tilde f$ radial (the corollary) gives $\tilde f(-\mathbf k) = \tilde f(\mathbf k)$ in every dimension, and step 1 of the proof in fact only uses orthogonality of $R$ (as the third remark bullet says), so it covers $R = -I$ for every $n$. Fix: delete "for odd $n$", or replace the parenthesis with "because $\tilde f$ is radial, hence even", or say "step 1 holds for every orthogonal $R$, including $R = -I$".
2. [minor] example (line 192): $e^{-0.5625} = 0.569782825\ldots$, so the printed factor $0.5697829$ is wrong in its last digit; it should be $0.5697828$. The product quoted from it, $0.2014486$, is correct, as is the radial-route value, so nothing downstream changes. Fix: print $0.5697828$.
