1. [blocking] statement "Splitting the contour: Hankel and Neumann functions" (line 200): the consistency check is stated with the factor of 2 lost. The sentence reads "the prefactor $1/\pi i$ is twice the $1/2\pi i$ of (S1), the two displayed integrals add to $J_\nu$, which matches $J_\nu = \tfrac12(H^{(1)}_\nu+H^{(2)}_\nu)$". The two displayed integrals are $H^{(1)}_\nu$ and $H^{(2)}_\nu$ themselves, and because $C_1+C_2 = C$ with a prefactor twice that of (S1) they add to $2J_\nu$, not to $J_\nu$. As written the clause asserts $H^{(1)}_\nu+H^{(2)}_\nu = J_\nu$ and is contradicted by the identity printed in the same sentence, so the Corollary contradicts itself on exactly the factor the paragraph exists to pin down. Confirmed numerically: at $\nu=1/2$, $z=2$ the two contour integrals give $0.5130161366 \pm 0.2347857104i$, summing to $1.0260322732 = 2J_{1/2}(2)$, and $|H^{(1)}+H^{(2)}-J_\nu| = 0.513$ (same at $\nu=1.3, z=2.5$; $\nu=-0.7, z=3$; $\nu=1, z=2.2$; $\nu=0.4, z=2+i$). Fix: "the two displayed integrals add to $2J_\nu$", or equivalently "half their sum is $J_\nu$". Everything else in the block, including the two displayed representations and their orientations, is correct.
2. [minor] example "A recurrence at general order..." (line 245): $|G| = e^{-\sinh x\,\operatorname{Re}z-\operatorname{Re}(\nu)x}$ is stated as an equality but holds only up to the constant factor $e^{\pm\pi\operatorname{Im}\nu}$ coming from $e^{-\nu(x\pm i\pi)}$ on the two ends of $C'$. For real $\nu$ it is exact, and the constant does not affect the conclusion that the boundary term vanishes, but the equality sign is wrong for complex $\nu$, which is the generality the example claims. Fix: write $|G| = \mathrm{const}\cdot e^{-\sinh x\operatorname{Re}z-\operatorname{Re}(\nu)x}$, or restrict the display to $\operatorname{Re}\nu$ with the constant absorbed.
