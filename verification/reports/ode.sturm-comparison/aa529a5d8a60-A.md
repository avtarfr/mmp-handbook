1. [source] statement[Sturm comparison theorem] / remark: Hassani's Thm 14.4.10, p. 431, writes the exceptional clause as "unless p = q and f is a constant multiple of g" with no restriction of the coefficient equality to the interval between the two zeros. Read on all of [a,b] the theorem is false, and the unit's own counterexample in the remark (G = 1, H = 1 on [0,pi] and 1 + (x-pi)^2 on [pi,2pi]) is genuine: H is continuous, H >= G, H != G on [0,2pi], u = sin x has consecutive zeros 0 and pi, and v with v(0)=0, v'(0)=1 equals sin x on [0,pi]. The unit states the clause on [x1,x2], which is what its proof delivers, and says so explicitly. Already recorded in verification/discrepancies.md. No fix needed.
2. [source] statement[Sturm separation theorem]: Hassani's Thm 14.4.8, p. 430, does not state that the two solutions are real-valued, although its proof argues from signs and the intermediate value theorem; y'' + y = 0 with sin x and e^{ix} shows the hypothesis is needed. The unit adds it and attributes it to Coddington Ch. 3, Sec. 4, Ex. 5, p. 117, which does state it. Already recorded in verification/discrepancies.md. No fix needed.
3. [source] statement[Oscillation, zero spacing, and the zeros of eigenfunctions], part 7: Hassani's Thm 19.1.9, p. 569, says u_n has exactly n zeros in its interval of definition, while Strauss, p. 278 and Thm 1, p. 281, gives n-1 nodes for the nth Dirichlet eigenfunction; the two agree only under a numbering starting at n=0, and Hassani's own Example 19.2.2(a), p. 571, numbers from n=1. The unit quotes both faithfully, says the count is quoted and not proved, names the numbering it uses (Strauss's), and points to the index shift recorded in ode.sturm-liouville-theory, which is what a conflict of sources requires. Recorded with tools/discrepancy.mjs during this review. No fix needed.

The conclusion drawn (nodes crowd into the heavy sections) is correct. Fix: say "part 5 applied window by window after the Liouville substitution", or drop the attribution.
