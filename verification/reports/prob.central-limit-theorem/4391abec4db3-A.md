2. [minor] definition (line 33): "Let $X_1, X_2,\dots$ be **independent** random variables ...; this is the set-up Grimmett and Stirzaker work in." G&S's Theorem (5.10.4), the theorem this unit proves, is stated for independent *identically distributed* variables; bare independence is the setting of their (5.10.5). The sentence is harmless because the next sentence adds a common mean and variance, but as written it attributes a weaker set-up to the source than the source uses. Fix: "this is the set-up of Grimmett and Stirzaker's Theorem (5.10.5); the theorem proved below adds identical distribution".
3. [minor] proof, step 4 (line 131): the bracket $1 - t^2/(2n) + o(n^{-1})$ is complex-valued, so "taking logarithms" needs the principal branch. The step is correct (for fixed $t$ the bracket tends to $1$, where the principal logarithm is analytic and $\log(1+z) = z + o(z)$ holds for complex $z \to 0$), but the branch is not named. Fix: add "the principal logarithm, which is defined since the bracket tends to $1$".
