2. [blocking] example "Using symmetry to make a full period" (lines 193-224) is labelled `provenance=original`, but it is Ahlfors's worked example from pp. 155-156 with different-looking constants. Ahlfors computes $\int_0^{\pi} d\theta/(a+\cos\theta)$ for $a>1$: he extends the half-period by the symmetry of $\cos$ between $(0,\pi)$ and $(\pi,2\pi)$, substitutes, factors the quadratic into $(z-\alpha)(z-\beta)$ with $\lvert\alpha\rvert<1<\lvert\beta\rvert$, takes the residue $1/(\alpha-\beta)$ and gets $\pi/\sqrt{a^2-1}$.
