## Findings

[minor] proof[Fejér's theorem], step 2 (line 151): "the quotient is $e^{i(N-1)y/2}\sin(Ny/2)/\sin(y/2)$" refers to the geometric quotient $(e^{iNy}-1)/(e^{iy}-1)$ alone, but the display that follows has silently reinstated the prefactor $e^{iy/2}$ and taken $\operatorname{Im}(e^{iNy/2})$. Both expressions are correct, yet the reader has to supply the multiplication themselves, which is the one place in the unit where a step is left implicit. Fix: write the combined line explicitly, e.g. "so $e^{iy/2}\,\frac{e^{iNy}-1}{e^{iy}-1} = e^{iNy/2}\,\frac{\sin(Ny/2)}{\sin(y/2)}$, whose imaginary part is $\sin^2(Ny/2)/\sin(y/2)$".
2. [minor] statement[Fejér's theorem], part 1 (lines 82–83): the chain ends "$\ge 0 \quad (y \notin 2\pi\mathbb{Z})$", which reads as if non-negativity were restricted to $y \notin 2\pi\mathbb{Z}$. The restriction belongs to the closed form only; $F_N \ge 0$ holds everywhere, and indeed $F_N(0) = N > 0$ is stated immediately after. Fix: attach the parenthetical to the middle expression and state $F_N \ge 0$ unconditionally.
3. [minor] idea (line 96): "the average of the Dirichlet kernels turns out to be a perfect square divided by $N$" — it is a *ratio* of two squares divided by $N$. The point being made (non-negativity) is right, but the phrase as written does not describe $\sin^2(Ny/2)/(N\sin^2(y/2))$. Fix: "a ratio of squares divided by $N$".
4. [minor] proof[The corollaries], part 3, second bullet (line 176): "Taking real parts if necessary, $P$ may be taken real" is redundant, because corollary 2 supplies $P = \sigma_M(\psi)$ and $\psi$ is real, so $P$ is already real. Harmless, but it suggests a gap where there is none. Fix: drop the clause, or note that the Fejér means of a real function are real.
