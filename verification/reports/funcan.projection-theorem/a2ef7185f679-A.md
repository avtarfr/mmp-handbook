[source] remark "Where the hypotheses are used" (the Banach-space bullet): the unit repeats Stakgold & Holst's Remark 2 (p. 267) that in $C[0,1]$ with the sup norm, with $M$ the continuous functions vanishing at $x=0$, the function $u=1$ "has no nearest element of $M$". Existence in fact holds: $f(0)=0$ forces $\sup|1-f|\ge 1$, and $f=0\in M$ attains $1$, so the distance is $1$ (as the unit itself says) and it is attained. What the example really shows is failure of *uniqueness* in a Banach space (every $f\in M$ with $0\le f\le 2$ attains it). The unit reports the source faithfully, so this is not blocking; recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs`. Fix (optional, not required): the bullet would be sharper if it said the sup norm destroys uniqueness here, and cited a genuine non-attainment example separately.
