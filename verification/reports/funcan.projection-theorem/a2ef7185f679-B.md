[source] remark "Where the hypotheses are used" (line 154) and application (line 215): the unit attributes to Hassani Prop. 6.1.3 the decomposition $\mathcal{V} = \mathcal{M}\oplus\mathcal{M}^\perp$ "for any subspace of a finite-dimensional inner product space". Hassani's printed statement (p. 170) says only "inner product space", with no dimension hypothesis; the proof on that page extends a finite orthonormal basis of $\mathcal M$ to a finite basis of $\mathcal V$, so it is a finite-dimensional argument, and the unqualified statement is false in infinite dimensions (a dense non-closed subspace has $\mathcal M^\perp=\{0\}$). The unit's reading is the correct one and its commentary is right; the imprecision is the source's. Recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs` against hassani-2013, Prop. 6.1.3, p. 170. Not blocking.
