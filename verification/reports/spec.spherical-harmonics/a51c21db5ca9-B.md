## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Part 6, the addition theorem" (Step 5, line 216): "the $l$th term on the left obviously does too" uses a word the style guide bans. The claim is true and immediate - that term is $\sum_{m}\overline{Y_{l}^{m}(\Omega')}\,Y_{l}^{m}(\Omega)$, a linear combination of the $Y_{l}^{m}(\Omega)$ with constants depending only on the fixed $\Omega'$ - so the fix is to say that rather than to assert it. Fix: replace with "and the $l$th term on the left is by construction a combination of the same $Y_{l}^{m'}(\Omega)$, with coefficients $\overline{Y_{l}^{m'}(\Omega')}$ that do not depend on $\Omega$".
2. [minor] statement, part 7: "(Strauss), a homogeneous polynomial of degree $l$ in $x,y,z$" credits Strauss with slightly more than he says. He proves on p. 277 that the solid harmonic is "a polynomial in $x$, $y$, and $z$" (his words, via eq. (26): $(x+\mathrm{i}y)^{m}$ times a polynomial in $z$ and $x^{2}+y^{2}+z^{2}$); he does not state homogeneity or the degree, though both follow at once from that factorisation. Fix: attribute the polynomiality to Strauss and present the homogeneity and the degree as our own one-line consequence of his eq. (26).
