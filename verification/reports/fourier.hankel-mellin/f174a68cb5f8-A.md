## Findings
1. [minor] statement "Operational rules for the Mellin transform", rule 5 (line ~163): the Mellin
   transform is *defined* in this unit only where the integral converges **absolutely**, but the
   hypotheses given for rule 5 ($f \in C^1$ and $t^{z}f(t) \to 0$ at both ends) do not force
   $\int_0^\infty \lvert f'(t)\rvert t^{\sigma}\,dt < \infty$. Take $f(t) = e^{-t}\sin(e^{2t})$ for
   $t \ge 1$ (smoothly continued on $(0,1]$): $t^{z}f \to 0$ at both ends and $F$ has a strip, yet
   $f'(t) = -e^{-t}\sin(e^{2t}) + 2e^{t}\cos(e^{2t})$ makes $\int_1^\infty\lvert f'\rvert t^\sigma dt$
   divergent, so $\mathcal{M}[Df](z)$ is undefined in the sense of the definition block while the
   improper integral still equals $-zF(z)$. The formula is right; only its well-posedness is not
   covered. Fix: add "and $t\mapsto f'(t)t^{\,\sigma}$ is absolutely integrable" to rule 5, or say
   explicitly that the left-hand side is read as an improper integral there. (The same elision is
   present in the published fourier.laplace-transform rule 1, so this is the house convention
   rather than an isolated slip, which is why it is logged as minor.)
2. [minor] application "Diffraction by a circular aperture, and a wedge", first paragraph (line ~441):
   the aperture function (the indicator of a disc of radius $a$) is not Schwartz, so "by part 1 of the
   Hankel theorem" invokes a statement whose stated hypothesis fails. The conclusion is nevertheless
   correct and already justified inside the unit: the two-dimensional radial reduction of
   fourier.fourier-transform-rn needs only $\int_0^\infty\rho\lvert f\rvert d\rho < \infty$, and the
   remark block says so in its fifth bullet. Fix: cite the radial reduction (or that bullet) instead
   of part 1 as stated.
3. [minor] proof "The Mellin theorems", step 2, first paragraph (line ~247): the unit :refs
   found.differentiation-under-integral and quotes its hypotheses (bounded interval, real parameter,
   real integrand) in order to rule it out, but that unit is not in `prereqs`. The argument does not
   depend on it, so nothing is unsupported; the list is simply incomplete. Fix: add it to `prereqs`,
   or name the obstruction without the :ref.
4. [minor] definition "The Mellin transform" (line ~103): "The domain of $F$ is therefore a vertical
   **strip**." The convexity argument just given shows the set of admissible $\sigma$ is an interval,
   which may be empty, a single point, or contain one or both endpoints — as the unit's own example
   $f(t) = 1/t$ (empty) shows two paragraphs later in the proof. Fix: "a vertical strip, possibly
   empty or degenerate, and possibly containing one or both of its edges".
5. [source] rhb-2006, §13.3, example (ii), p. 459: the Mellin inversion contour is printed from
   $-i\infty$ to $i\infty$, with no abscissa and no strip; for $f(t)=1/(1+t)$, whose strip is
   $0<\operatorname{Re}z<1$, the forward integral diverges on that line. The unit reports this
   faithfully in its note block, states that it follows Arfken (21.20) and Hassani (Problem 16.1)
   instead, and explains when the two agree — which is exactly what docs/contributing/source-authority.md
   requires of two conflicting sources. Recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs add`; not a fault of the
   unit.
