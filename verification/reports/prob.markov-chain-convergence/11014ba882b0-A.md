## Findings
1. [minor] proof, Step 2 (line 116): "the set $\{n : p_{aa}(n)>0\}$ is closed under addition and has greatest common divisor $1$, so it contains every sufficiently large integer" uses a number-theoretic lemma (a sub-semigroup of $\mathbb{N}$ with gcd $1$ contains all large integers) that is neither proved nor listed among the prerequisites; it is not standard calculus or linear algebra, and the source defers it to Problem (6.15.4). The step is true and the two ingredients named are the right ones, but the only genuinely non-trivial implication in the proof's one load-bearing use of aperiodicity is asserted. Fix: add the two-line argument (take $m,m'\in D_a$ with $\gcd=1$ by passing to a finite subset, then every integer $\ge mm'$ is a non-negative combination), or cite it explicitly as the source does.
2. [minor] remark (line 183): "**Irreducibility** is used in Step 4 (to reach $(s,s)$) and in Step 3 (uniqueness of $\pi$)." It is also used in Step 2 — "composing with one path from $a$ to $b$" needs $p_{ab}(m)>0$ for some $m$, which is irreducibility, not aperiodicity. The remark's claim about aperiodicity ("used once, in Step 2") is accurate; the companion list for irreducibility is not complete. Fix: add Step 2 to the list.
3. [minor] remark (line 185): the heading "**Positivity of $\pi$ in Step 6**" does not name what the paragraph is about, which is that $\pi$ has finite total mass (positive recurrence), not that its entries are positive. Fix: "**Positive recurrence in Step 6**", or "**$\pi$ being a distribution, not just a measure**".
4. [minor] statement, the reversible bound (line 90): $\sup\{|v_r(i)| : r \in S\}$ indexes the eigenvectors by the state space. That is exactly G&S's own notation in (6.14.10) (they write $r \in \Theta$), so it is faithful, but this unit has just listed the eigenvalues as $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_N$ and the eigenvectors as $v_1,\dots,v_N$, so the index set changes within the same block. Fix: write $\sup\{|v_r(i)| : 1 \le r \le N\}$.
5. [minor] front matter (line 7): `prob.convergence-random-variables` is listed as a prerequisite but appears to be unused — every limit in the unit is a limit of numbers ($p_{ij}(n)$, $\mathbb{P}(T>n)$, $d_{\mathrm{TV}}$), no mode of convergence of random variables, and no Borel-Cantelli, is invoked; the almost-sure finiteness of $T$ comes from recurrence of $Z$, i.e. from the Markov-chain prerequisite. Fix: drop it, or use it where the coupling's $\mathbb{P}(T<\infty)=1$ is turned into a statement about $X_n$ and $Y_n$.
6. [minor] statement, point 3 (line 59): $\mu^{(0)}_i$ (initial distribution, from the prerequisite unit) and $\mu_j$ (mean recurrence time, defined two lines earlier) appear in the same display with different meanings. Both are the source's notation and the superscript keeps them apart, but it is one character of distinction. Fix: a half-sentence reminder, e.g. "(here $\mu^{(0)}$ is the initial mass function of :ref[...], not a mean recurrence time)".
