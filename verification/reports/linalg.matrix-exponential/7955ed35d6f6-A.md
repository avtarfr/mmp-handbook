## Findings
1. [minor] statement "Convergence", part 2 (line 40): the bounding constant is wrong, though the conclusion is not. With $|(A^k)_{ij}| \le n^{k-1}a^k$ for $k \ge 1$ one gets $\sum_{k\ge1}|(A^k)_{ij}|/k! \le \frac1n\sum_{k\ge1}(na)^k/k! = (e^{na}-1)/n$, not $e^{na}/n$; and if the sum is read from $k=0$ the bound fails on the $k=0$ term, since a diagonal entry of $A^0 = I$ is $1 > e^{0}/n$ for $n \ge 2$. Absolute convergence, which is all the part claims, is unaffected. Fix: write $\|\exp A\|_{\max} \le 1 + (e^{na}-1)/n$, or simply bound the tail $k \ge 1$.
2. [minor] definition "Power series in a matrix" (line 33): "$(I-A)^{-1} = \sum_k A^k$" is asserted in a sentence whose only proviso ("provided the series converges") appears several lines earlier. The identity does hold whenever the series converges (convergence forces $A^k \to 0$, hence $\rho(A) < 1$), so nothing is false, but the reader is not told that $\rho(A)<1$ is needed. Fix: state the condition $\rho(A) < 1$ inline.
3. [minor] method, step 3 (line 82): "split it as $A = D + N$ ... from the Jordan form, where $D$ is the diagonal part and $N$ the nilpotent part of each block" — in the original basis $A = S(D+N)S^{-1}$, so the pieces are $SDS^{-1}$ (diagonalizable, not diagonal) and $SNS^{-1}$ (nilpotent). The displayed formula is correct for any commuting diagonalizable-plus-nilpotent split, so this is wording. Also, "the polynomial degree being one less than the size of the largest Jordan block for $\lambda$" is the maximum degree, not necessarily the degree of every entry. Fix: say "after the change of basis to Jordan form", and "of degree at most".
4. [minor] method, steps 2-4: the recipe uses `linalg.normal-matrices` (step 2), `linalg.jordan-form` (step 3) and the Cayley-Hamilton theorem (step 4), none of which is in `prereqs` (Cayley-Hamilton is not a unit anywhere in `content/map.yaml`). The first two are cross-referenced inline and the `prereqs` list matches the map's spec for this unit, and Cayley-Hamilton is standard undergraduate linear algebra, so I do not read this as a defect. Fix, if prereq hygiene is wanted: add the two units, and give Cayley-Hamilton a home in the determinants unit.
5. [minor] statement "Properties of the exponential", part 7: $\exp(A^\dagger) = (\exp A)^\dagger$ and its two consequences are not covered by either cited source (Hirsch-Smale-Devaney p. 126 gives parts 1-3, p. 128 part 4 and the derivative; RHB Eq. (8.104) gives part 6). The claims are correct and one-line. Fix: label part 7 as ours, or cite a page for it.
