[minor] :::example (line 249): "the same construction with more than $2/\varepsilon$ subdivisions per side works for any $\varepsilon$" is a correct sufficient criterion but is not the one used in step 1, where $\varepsilon = 1/10$ and only $16 < 20$ subdivisions are taken. Fix: write $\sqrt{2}/\varepsilon$, or note that $2/\varepsilon$ is a convenient over-estimate.
