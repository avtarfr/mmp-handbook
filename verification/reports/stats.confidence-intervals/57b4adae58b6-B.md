## Findings
1. [blocking] definition "Confidence interval, confidence level, confidence limits" (line 57): the one-sided clause is the wrong way round. The block has just defined $\alpha$ as the LOWER tail of $\hat a$ ($\Pr(\hat a < \hat a_\alpha(a)\mid a)=\alpha$), $\beta$ as the UPPER tail, and $a_+$, $a_-$ by $\hat a_\alpha(a_+)=\hat a_{\mathrm{obs}}=\hat a_\beta(a_-)$. Setting $\beta=0$ makes $\hat a_\beta(a)\equiv+\infty$, so the equation for $a_-$ has no solution and $a_-=-\infty$: the finite limit is $a_+$, not $a_-$. The unit's own statement block confirms this - with tails $\alpha,\beta$ the Gaussian limits are $\overline X - z_{1-\beta}\sigma/\sqrt n$ and $\overline X + z_{1-\alpha}\sigma/\sqrt n$, and $\beta=0$ gives $z_1=\infty$ in the lower limit - as does RHB (31.31)-(31.32) on p. 1238. So the text contradicts both its own equations and the statement block two blocks later. RHB p. 1237 mentions the one-sided variant without saying which limit survives, so this is our own addition, not a faithful report of the source. Fix: write "taking $\beta = 0$, so that only $a_+$ is finite" (or equivalently "taking $\alpha = 0$, so that only $a_-$ is finite").
2. [minor] remark "Where the hypotheses are used" (line 148): "independence of $\overline X$ and $S^2$ ... is equivalent to normality for a sample of size $n \ge 2$ from a distribution with finite variance" fails for a degenerate parent, where $S^2 \equiv 0$ is trivially independent of $\overline X$ and the variance is finite. Fix: add "non-degenerate", or say "normal (possibly degenerate)".
3. [minor] remark "Where the hypotheses are used" (line 150): "the true standard deviation of $S^{2}$ is $\sqrt{8\sigma^4} = 2\sqrt2\,\sigma^2$ against the assumed $\sqrt2\,\sigma^2$" drops the $1/\sqrt n$: these are the standard deviations of $\sqrt n\,(S^2-\sigma^2)$, as the preceding sentence states. The ratio, and hence the coverage computation, is unaffected. Fix: say "the asymptotic standard deviation of $\sqrt n\,(S^2-\sigma^2)$".
