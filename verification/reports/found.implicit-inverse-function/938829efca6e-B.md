[minor] `application[Thermodynamic derivatives and the cyclic relation]` (line 256): "So wherever $\Phi_V = 0$ the isotherm is flat, the isothermal compressibility is infinite, and the two-phase region begins." The first two clauses are right. The third is imprecise: $\Phi_V=0$ is the *spinodal*, the locus where local mechanical stability is lost, and below $T_c$ it lies strictly inside the coexistence region, whose boundary (the binodal) is fixed by the Maxwell equal-area construction and not by any derivative of $\Phi$. Between binodal and spinodal the single-phase states are metastable, with $(\partial p/\partial V)_T<0$, i.e. $\Phi_V>0$; the two have already separated there. Only at the critical point do spinodal and binodal meet, which is why the sentence reads correctly for the critical-point discussion that follows it. Fix: "...and the single-phase description loses stability: $\Phi_V=0$ is the spinodal, inside the two-phase region, whose boundary requires the Maxwell construction and not the implicit function theorem."
