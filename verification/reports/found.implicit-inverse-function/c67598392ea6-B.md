- Calculations: sympy confirmed every number in the remark. For $f(x) = x + 2x^2\sin(1/x)$: $f'(x) = 1 + 4x\sin(1/x) - 2\cos(1/x)$ for $x \ne 0$, $f'(0) = \lim_{x\to 0} f(x)/x = 1$, $f'(1/(2\pi j)) = -1$ (checked $j = 3, 7$) and $f'(1/((2j+1)\pi)) = 3$ (checked the exponents $3\pi$, $9\pi$). For $n = p = 1$ the formula reduces to $g' = -\Phi_x/\Phi_y$ as stated.
