## Findings

1. [blocking] statement, part (c) (line 64): "The side in question is the one into which the returning branch of $W^{u}(p)$ enters, the interior of the loop in the coordinates of the proof below." The two identifications of the side disagree in the case $\lambda_u > \lambda_s$, and the unit's own example falls on the wrong side of the disagreement. In the coordinates of Step 0 the loop leaves along $u>0$ and returns along $s>0$, so the interior of the loop near the saddle is the quadrant $u>0$, $s>0$, and both the entry edge segment $\{s=\delta,\ 0<u\le u_1\}$ and the segment $\{u=\delta,\ 0<s\le s_1\}$ used after the reversal of time lie in it. On the entry edge $s=\delta$ the separation is $u_0$, the position of the returning branch of $W^u$. On the edge $u=\delta$ it is, by the Step 2 relation $u_{\rm in}=u_0+a\,s_{\rm out}+O(s_{\rm out}^2)$ with $a>0$, equal to $-u_0/a + O(u_0^2)$ (the global stable curve reaches that edge at the $s_{\rm out}$ for which $u_{\rm in}=0$). Step 6 runs the whole argument on the edge $u=\delta$, so in the case $\lambda_u>\lambda_s$ the closed orbits exist for the sign of $u_0$ that makes $-u_0/a$ positive, that is for $u_0<0$: the returning branch of $W^u$ then enters the **exterior**, while the one-sided neighbourhood Step 6 controls is still the interior. The worked example is exactly this case ($\lambda_u-\lambda_s=\epsilon\alpha>0$), its unstable cycle lies inside $\Gamma$ (it separates the stable spiral at $(1,0)$ from $W^u$), and the unit itself says the cycle exists for the $\alpha$ at which "the unstable manifold ... falls outside the stable manifold". Fix: identify the side as the interior of the loop in both cases - equivalently as the side into which $W^u$ *leaves* the saddle - and either drop the "returning branch" description or restrict it to $\lambda_u<\lambda_s$.

[blocking] proof (c), Step 6 (line 226): "by the section-independence established in Step 3 that replaces $u_0$ by a positive smooth multiple of itself, which changes neither its vanishing at $\mu_c$, nor its sign, nor the non-degeneracy." The vanishing at $\mu_c$ and the non-degeneracy do transfer; the sign does not, and it is the only one of the three that Step 6 did not need, so asserting it is what puts the cycle on the wrong side of $\Gamma$ in finding 1. Related, in Step 3 (line 219): "Between any two cross-sections of $\Gamma$ near the saddle the flow furnishes a transition map that ... carries the point where the stable curve meets the first section to the point where it meets the second" is not true of the pair (entry edge $s=\delta$, exit edge $u=\delta$): the local stable curve does not meet the exit edge at all, and the map of Step 2 that joins them carries the $W^u$ point to the $W^u$ point, reversing the orientation of the separation. Fix: state only the vanishing and the non-degeneracy in Step 6, and in Step 3 say that the factor is a non-zero (not necessarily positive) smooth multiple, or restrict Step 3's claim to sections on one branch of $\Gamma$.

On the second of those edges the quantity Step 6 brackets between $u_0$ and $2u_0$ is the $s$-coordinate, i.e. the distance from the local **unstable** curve $\{s=0\}$ (the reversed field's stable curve), not the distance from $W^s$ - as the clause's own description of that edge concedes.
