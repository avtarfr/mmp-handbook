[blocking] proof (c), Step 6 (line 226): "by the section-independence established in Step 3 that replaces $u_0$ by a positive smooth multiple of itself, which changes neither its vanishing at $\mu_c$, nor its sign, nor the non-degeneracy". The multiple is negative, and Step 3 does not cover this pair of sections. Step 3's section-independence is for sections joined by a transition map that carries the stable curve's crossing of the first to its crossing of the second; the entry edge $s = \delta$ and the exit edge $u = \delta$ are joined only by the global map $\Psi$ of Step 2 (going the other way round the loop) or by the passage through $N$, and neither has that property. Explicitly, with $\Psi(s_{\rm out}) = u_0 + a\,s_{\rm out} + O(s_{\rm out}^2)$ and $a > 0$, the reversed separation read on $u = \delta$ is $\tilde u_0 = \Psi^{-1}(0) = -u_0/a + O(u_0^2)$. The sign therefore reverses, which is exactly why the cycle lives on the opposite side of $\mu_c$ in the two cases (finding 1). The magnitude is unchanged up to a bounded factor, so the period law of Step 5 survives; only the statement about the side does not. Fix: say that reversing time leaves $|u_0|$ comparable but reverses its sign, and carry that through to the statement.
