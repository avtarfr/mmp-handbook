## Findings

1. [minor] statement "Three prototypes", part (c) (line 65): the bracket is given as "between $u_0$ and $2u_0$" on the proof's entry section in *both* cases, but in the case $\lambda_u > \lambda_s$ that section is the edge $u = \delta$ and the quantity the proof actually brackets there is the *reversed* separation $\tilde u_0 = -u_0/a + O(u_0^2)$ of Step 6, not $u_0$. The two are comparable in magnitude and opposite in sign, so "between $u_0$ and $2u_0$" is right only when $u_0$ is read as the separation measured on that same section. Fix: say "between that section's own separation and twice it", or add "(which in the second case is $\tilde u_0 = -u_0/a$ of Step 6)". The headline word "comparable" and the "up to a positive factor" clause already cover the substance; this is the one place the restriction has not been carried all the way through.

2. [minor] statement, part (c) (line 65): "for $\mu$ on the one side of $\mu_c$ fixed by the sign of $u_0$ there is at least one closed orbit" does not say *which* side, and on a first reading suggests that the sign of $u_0$ alone settles it - the very thing the remark now (correctly) denies, since the answer also needs the sign of $\lambda_u - \lambda_s$. The statement is not false (the sign of $u_0$ does label the two sides) but it is weaker than the remark and reads as if it were in tension with it. Fix: "on the side where $u_0 > 0$ if $\lambda_u < \lambda_s$, and where $u_0 < 0$ if $\lambda_u > \lambda_s$".

3. [minor] proof (c), Step 6 (line 226): two editorial leftovers from the correction. (i) The sentence "The separation is now read on the edge $u = \delta$, which has become the entry edge of the square, rather than on $s = \delta$; with one change of sign." is a fragment that the very next sentence restates in full; one of the two should go. (ii) "the same separation of the two manifolds: reversing time renames which curve is which, not how far apart they lie" is immediately qualified by $\tilde u_0 = -u_0/a$, i.e. comparable rather than equal, and by a sign change. Fix: delete the fragment and soften "the same separation" to "a separation of the same order".

4. [minor] proof (c), Step 6 (line 226): "Hence the cycle is unstable" uses the definite singular, although the statement is deliberately careful to claim only "at least one closed orbit" and to speak of "the set of them". The preceding clause ("attraction becomes repulsion") is the correct form. Fix: "Hence the set of closed orbits repels the one-sided neighbourhood".

5. [minor] proof (c), Step 4 (line 220): "Those points make up one side of $\Gamma$, the side into which the unstable curve returns" is correct inside Step 4's standing case $\lambda_u < \lambda_s$ (there $u_0 > 0$, so the two descriptions agree) and correct again for the reversed field in Step 6, but a reader who carries the phrase out of Step 4 will collide with the statement's new warning that this is *not* the side $W^u$ returns into when $\lambda_u > \lambda_s$.
