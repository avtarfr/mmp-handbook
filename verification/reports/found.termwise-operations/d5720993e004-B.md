## Findings

1. [minor] remark "Where the hypotheses are used", last-but-one bullet (line 120): the ranking of the
   two Riley-Hobson-Bence warnings is inverted and the second is overstated. §4.4(v) on p. 131 says
   termwise differentiation or integration need not give a series with the same *convergence
   properties*; §4.5.2(iii) on p. 135 says only that power series keep their *interval of
   convergence* under these operations and that "this is not true for series in general". The p. 135
   remark is therefore no broader than the p. 131 one (if anything narrower), and neither page says
   that termwise operations "are not to be performed as a matter of course". Fix: describe p. 135 as
   the place where power series are singled out as the exception and the interval of convergence is
   said to be unchanged there, and drop the general/narrower ranking; the frontmatter `role` for
   rhb-2006 ("the warning that termwise operations are not generally valid") needs the same softening,
   since both pages warn about convergence behaviour rather than about validity.

2. [minor] proof of term-by-term differentiation (line 102): part 2 of the integration theorem is
   invoked "on $[a,x]$" for every $x \in [a,b]$, but that statement is set up for a non-degenerate
   interval ("Let $a < b$ be finite"). At $x = a$ the identity $S(a) = S(a) + 0$ is trivial. Fix:
   restrict the appeal to $x > a$ and note that $x = a$ is trivial.

3. [minor] remark, third bullet (line 118): the telescoped counterexample is built on $\mathbb{R}$
   and then said to satisfy "hypotheses 1 and 2", which are stated for a finite $[a,b]$. The
   conclusion is unaffected on any finite interval containing $\pi$. Fix: name such an interval
   (e.g. $[0,2\pi]$).

4. [source] arfken-2013, §1.2 property 3 and Eq. (1.39), p. 24: Arfken's hypotheses for term-by-term
   differentiation ask for uniform convergence of $\sum u_n$ itself, which is stronger than needed
   (convergence at one point suffices). The unit states Arfken's form and records the weakening in its
   fourth remark bullet. Already on file in `verification/discrepancies.md`; no new entry added. Not
   blocking.
