1. [minor] definition (line 47): "$H^*$ is a Banach space (see normed and Banach spaces)". The target unit says it covers nothing about maps between normed spaces and defers to funcan.bounded-operators, so the cross-reference does not support the claim. Fix: point the reference at funcan.bounded-operators (completeness of the space of bounded maps into a complete space), or note that it also follows from part 4, since $J$ is an isometric bijection from the complete space $H$.
2. [minor] proof Step 2 (line 81): it cites Stakgold and Holst "§4.5, p. 268", which exists and does state the corollary. That page is missing from the frontmatter `pages` for stakgold-holst-2011, which lists only 288-292. Fix: add 268 to `pages` and the loc.
3. [minor] application, Green's functions (line 178): "the map 'solution evaluated against a test function' is a bounded functional of the data, and its representer is the Green's function". Strictly, the representer of $f \mapsto (L^{-1}f,\varphi)$ is $\xi \mapsto \int \overline{G(x,\xi)}\,\varphi(x)\,dx$, the adjoint solution operator applied to $\varphi$, not $G$ itself. $G(\cdot,\xi)$ is the formal representer of point evaluation, which the unit itself stresses is not bounded on $L^2$. Fix: say "its representer is $L^{-*}\varphi$, the Green's function integrated against $\varphi$; the Green's function itself represents point evaluation whenever that is bounded on the space in use".
4. [minor] application, estimation (line 182): "its norm is the standard error" holds in the Gauss-Markov setting only up to the noise scale. The BLUE $a^{\mathsf T}y$ has variance $\sigma^2\|a\|^2$. Fix: say "its norm, times the noise standard deviation, is the standard error".
5. [minor] application, Dirac notation (line 174): this paragraph uses the physicists' bracket, which is linear in the second slot, without saying so. The rest of the unit is linear in the first slot, and the statement block makes a point of the slot convention. Fix: add a clause saying that the bracket here is linear in the ket, as in Reed and Simon's convention.
