## Findings
1. [minor] proof, Step 2 (line 145): item 1 of part 1 is asserted "for all $x$", but only the removable singularity at $t=0$ is addressed ("extends continuously to $t=0$ with value $M$"). The integrand $\sin(Mt)/\sin t$ also has removable singularities at every other multiple of $\pi$; it is continuous there because $M=2n+2$ is even (near $t=\pi$ it tends to $-M$, consistent with $S_n'(\pi)=-\tfrac{2}{\pi}M$), so the claim is true, but the reader is told only half of what is needed. Fix: say that $M$ is even, so the quotient extends continuously at every multiple of $\pi$, not just at $0$.
2. [minor] proof, Step 5b (line 194): two slips of presentation in one sentence. The bound is written $\pi u/(12M^{2})\le\pi c'/(6M^{2})$ on $[0,c']$, where $\pi c'/(12M^{2})$ is what the previous line gives — correct but gratuitously loose by a factor $2$. Also the Jordan-inequality step inherited from Step 5 needs $c'/M\le\pi/2$, i.e. $M\ge 4c/\pi$, which holds for all large $N$ but is not stated here as it is in Step 5. Fix: keep the constant $12$, and add "for $N$ large enough that $Mc'/\ldots$", matching the explicit $M\ge2j$ of Step 5.
3. [minor] proof, Step 7 (line 212) and remark (line 246): Folland's Corollary 8.45(b) is stated for period $1$ and an open interval of length $<1$ (p. 268), whereas this unit works at period $2\pi$. The transfer is legitimate and the prerequisite unit records the coordinate change explicitly, but neither invocation here mentions it, nor that $I'$ must be shorter than a full period. Fix: one clause noting the rescaling to period $2\pi$ and that $I'$ has length $<2\pi$.
4. [minor] idea (line 112): "absolute continuity is exactly the hypothesis under which partial sums are known to converge uniformly on compact sets" overstates Corollary 8.45(b), which asks for absolute continuity *and* $g'\in L^{p}$ for some $p>1$. The proof does supply the extra condition (Step 7 gets $g'\in L^{p}$ for every $p>1$), so nothing downstream is affected. Fix: "absolute continuity with an $L^p$ derivative".
5. [minor] remark (line 222): "Stakgold and Holst subtract Heaviside steps on an interval" — on p. 136 the decomposition into a continuous function of class $D$ plus $\sum_k J_k H(x-x_k)$ is made for the whole circle, not on a sub-interval. The contrast being drawn (their model has one jump, ours has two) is still right. Fix: drop "on an interval".
6. [minor] front matter, `prereqs` (line 7): Step 1 and part 1 item 4 both use `fourier.fourier-series` (its symmetry statement, part 3, and its definition of the symmetric partial sum), which is reached only transitively, through the single listed prerequisite `fourier.dirichlet-convergence`. That is enough for the graph check, but a reader of the proof is sent to a unit that is not listed. Fix: list `fourier.fourier-series` alongside `fourier.dirichlet-convergence`.
