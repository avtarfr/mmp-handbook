## Findings
1. [blocking] idea (line 82): "The first application bounds $\int f$ below by $\liminf \int f_n$; the second ... bounds $\int f$ above by $\limsup \int f_n$." Both directions are stated backwards relative to the proof. Step 2 (Fatou on $g + f_n$) gives $\int f \le \liminf_n \int f_n$, so $\liminf_n \int f_n$ is an *upper* bound for $\int f$; step 3 (Fatou on $g - f_n$) gives $\limsup_n \int f_n \le \int f$, so $\limsup_n \int f_n$ is a *lower* bound. Taken in the standard sense of "bounded below by", the idea block describes inequalities the proof does not prove, and the sentence that follows it - "Since always $\liminf \le \limsup$, the two bounds squeeze together" - then becomes a non-sequitur, since $\liminf \int f_n \le \int f \le \limsup \int f_n$ together with $\liminf \le \limsup$ yields nothing. The squeeze works only with the directions the proof actually establishes: $\int f \le \liminf \le \limsup \le \int f$. Fix: write the two inequalities out, "the first gives $\int f \le \liminf_n \int f_n$, the second $\limsup_n \int f_n \le \int f$", and keep the closing sentence as it stands.
2. [minor] remark, first bullet (line 144): "The envelope is used twice, in step 0 to put $f$ in $L^1$ and in step 1 to make Fatou's lemma applicable; it is used nowhere else, and the proof never looks at $g$ again." Steps 2 and 3 do look at $g$: $\int g$ appears on both sides of each display and is cancelled, which is licensed only because $\int g < \infty$. The accounting is defensible, since step 0 already notes that integrability of $g$ "is what makes every integral below finite", but the clause as phrased is contradicted by the two displays that follow it. Fix: "...and thereafter only through the finiteness it guarantees, which is what lets $\int g$ be cancelled in steps 2 and 3."
3. [source] proof "The series corollary and the parameter theorems", domination bullet (line 135): Folland's proof of Thm 2.27b (§2.3, p. 56) bounds the difference quotient by the mean value theorem although the theorem is stated for complex-valued $f$, for which that theorem fails. The unit notices this and supplies the correct constant ($|h_n| \le 2g$, via real and imaginary parts separately), while attributing to Folland only the real-valued bound he actually writes - the right handling. Already on record in verification/discrepancies.md (measure.dominated-convergence / folland-1999, §2.3, proof of Thm 2.27b, p. 56), so no new entry was added; not a reason to fail.
