[blocking] method "Analysing a quadratic form", step 5 (line 113): "The set is unbounded in the direction $u_k$ precisely when $\lambda_k = 0$" is false under the natural reading of the phrase, and the sentence it sits in makes that reading the only available one. Take $A = \operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$, $c = 1$, $u_1 = e_1$, $u_2 = e_2$: the level set is the hyperbola $x_1^2 - x_2^2 = 1$, which contains $(\sqrt{1+t^2},\, t)$ for every $t$, so *both* the $u_1$-component and the $u_2$-component are unbounded on it, while neither eigenvalue is $0$. As written, a reader following the recipe on $\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ concludes the hyperbola is bounded along both axes.
