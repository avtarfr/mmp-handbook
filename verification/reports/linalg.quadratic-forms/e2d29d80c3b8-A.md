## Findings
1. [source] rhb-2006, §8.17, the paragraph following Eq. (8.112), p. 290: the source defines positive definiteness by "$Q = x^{\mathsf T}Ax$ is greater than zero for all column matrices $x$", with no exclusion of $x = 0$; read literally no matrix qualifies, since $Q(0) = 0$. The unit's definition block correctly requires $x \neq 0$. This is the source's quantifier, not our error; recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs`, and the unit passes.
2. [minor] statement "Principal axes" (lines 46 and 54): the non-emptiness criterion is given together with its proof ("If instead every $\lambda_k \le 0$, then $Q(x) = \sum_k \lambda_k x_k'^2 \le 0 < 1$ for *every* $x$, not merely on the axis lines, so $L$ is empty"), which step 2 of the proof then repeats verbatim in substance. Proof material in a statement block. Fix: state the criterion; leave the one-line argument to step 2.
3. [minor] statement "Rayleigh bounds" (line 192): the closing parenthetical "(Expand $x$ in an orthonormal eigenbasis: $\mathcal{R}(x)$ is the weighted average of the $\lambda_k$ with weights $|x_k'|^2/\|x\|^2$ summing to $1$.)" is the idea block, which follows two lines later and says the same thing. Fix: delete the parenthetical.
4. [minor] application, "The second-derivative test" (line 423): "the eigenvalues of $H$ (divided by the masses) are the squared frequencies of the small oscillations about it". That reading is exact only for a scalar mass matrix; in general the squared frequencies are the eigenvalues of the pencil $(H, M)$, which is what the referenced :ref{to=linalg.normal-modes} states and which is not "eigenvalues of $H$ divided by the masses" (the eigenvectors differ too). The cross-reference saves the sentence, but the parenthesis as written is wrong for unequal masses. Fix: "the eigenvalues of the pencil $(H, M)$ formed with the mass matrix are the squared frequencies".
