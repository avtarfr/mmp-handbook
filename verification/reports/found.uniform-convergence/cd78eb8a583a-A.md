## Findings
1. [minor] application, "Uniform convergence away from $t = 0$" (line 228): the convergence of $\sum M_n$ with $M_n = C e^{-n^2 k t_0}$ is justified "by the ratio test, since $M_{n+1}/M_n = e^{-(2n+1)kt_0}$", but the ratio test as stated in the listed prerequisite found.series-convergence-tests (part 2, "The five tests") requires $u_n > 0$, and $M_n = 0$ for all $n$ in the degenerate case $C = (2/\pi)\int_0^\pi\lvert f\rvert = 0$, where the quotient is $0/0$. The conclusion is unaffected (all $M_n$ vanish and the series converges trivially), and the later paragraphs assume a jump, hence $C > 0$. Fix: add "with $C > 0$; if $C = 0$ every $M_n$ vanishes and there is nothing to prove", or bound $M_n \le C e^{-nkt_0}$ and compare with a geometric series instead.
2. [minor] example "A series handled completely by the M-test", Step 3 (line 168): the inline derivation "For $n \ge N+1$ the function $x \mapsto x^{-2}$ is at least $n^{-2}$ on $[n-1, n]$" needs $n \ge 2$, i.e. $N \ge 1$; at $N = 0$ the interval is $[0,1]$, on which $x^{-2}$ is not defined at $0$ (the final bound $1/N$ is likewise vacuous there). Fix: say "for $N \ge 1$ and $n \ge N+1$". The step is used only at $N = 1000$, so nothing downstream is affected.
3. [minor] application, "A usable error bound" (line 252): "against a true tail of $3.0\times10^{-10}\,C$" names the exact sum $C\sum_{n>N}e^{-n^2kt_0}$ of the majorant, not the true tail $\sup_E\lvert u - u_N\rvert$ of the solution, which the dropped $\lvert b_n\rvert \le C$ and $\lvert\sin(nx)\rvert \le 1$ can make far smaller. Fix: call it "the exact majorant tail" or "the unrelaxed bound".
