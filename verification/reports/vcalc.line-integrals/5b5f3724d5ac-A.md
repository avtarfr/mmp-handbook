## Findings
1. [minor] `prereqs` (line 7): the curl is central to the unit — it is condition 4 of the main statement, the whole of Step 4, the hypothesis of Step 5 and the subject of both examples — yet `vcalc.divergence-curl`, which defines $\nabla\times$, is not listed. The symbol is in effect pinned down inside the unit, because Step 4 writes out $(\nabla\times\mathbf{F})_1 = \partial_yF_3 - \partial_zF_2$, so nothing is unfollowable; but `vcalc.divergence-curl` precedes this unit in `content/map.yaml`, so adding it costs nothing and removes the only symbol in the statement that no listed prerequisite defines. Fix: add `vcalc.divergence-curl` to `prereqs`.
2. [minor] definition (line 34) against statement, condition 2 (line 56): the definition says a potential is "single-valued and of class $C^1$", while condition 2 asks for $\phi \in C^2(\Omega)$. The two agree under the theorem's hypothesis $\mathbf{F} \in C^1$ (a $C^1$ potential of a $C^1$ field is automatically $C^2$), and Step 2 says exactly that, but a reader matching the definition against the condition sees two different smoothness classes for the same object. Fix: in condition 2 write "single-valued $\phi \in C^1(\Omega)$ — hence $C^2$, since $\mathbf{F}$ is $C^1$ — with $\mathbf{F} = \nabla\phi$".
3. [minor] proof, Step 1 (line 95): "this is where the hypothesis that $\phi$ is *single-valued* is used". Nothing in Step 1 uses single-valuedness as a hypothesis: $\phi$ is a function by assumption, so $\phi(\gamma(b)) - \phi(\gamma(a))$ is well defined for that reason alone. The real content is the contrapositive, which the remark's fifth bullet states correctly (the azimuthal angle is not a function on $\Omega$, and its $2\pi$ jump *is* the circulation). Fix: delete the clause in Step 1, or reword to "note that nothing here would survive if $\phi$ were only locally defined up to a constant — see the remark".
4. [minor] remark, first bullet heading (line 153): "*Simple connectivity, in Step 5 only.*" Step 5 assumes $\Omega$ star-shaped, not simply connected; the simply connected case is deferred to the note at line 147, which is explicit about that. Fix: head the bullet "*Star-shapedness (and, through the note, simple connectivity), in Step 5 only.*"
5. [minor] frontmatter `sources`, `rhb-2006` `pages: "377-379, 382-383, 387-389"` (line 12): the application (line 222) cites "Riley, Hobson and Bence, p. 381" for Ampère's law. The citation itself is sound — p. 381 does carry $\oint_C \mathbf{B}\cdot d\mathbf{r} = \mu_0 I$ with $I$ the enclosed current — but 381 is not in the declared page range, so the citation check has nothing to verify it against. Fix: extend `pages` to include 381 and name it in `loc`.
6. [minor] statement[Parameter independence] (line 39): "a $C^1$ bijection with $\psi'$ of one sign" leaves open whether $\psi'$ may vanish. Lee's Prop. 11.37 (p. 290) is stated for a diffeomorphism $\varphi:[c,d]\to[a,b]$, i.e. $\psi' \ne 0$ throughout. The conclusion in fact survives $\psi'$ vanishing at isolated points, so nothing is wrong, but the hypothesis as phrased is not the source's. Fix: write "with $\psi' > 0$ throughout or $\psi' < 0$ throughout".
