3. [source] statement "Hölder's inequality" (line 60), reporting folland-1999 Thm 6.2 / (6.3), p. 182. Folland's equality clause is an iff: equality holds in $\|fg\|_1\le\|f\|_p\|g\|_q$ exactly when $\alpha|f|^p=\beta|g|^q$ a.e. for constants with $\alpha\beta\ne0$. It fails in the degenerate direction: if $f=0$ a.e. and $g$ is any element of $L^q$ that is not $0$ a.e., then $\|fg\|_1=0=\|f\|_p\|g\|_q$, so equality holds, yet no pair $\alpha,\beta$ with $\alpha\beta\ne0$ can satisfy $\alpha\cdot0=\beta|g|^q$ a.e. Reed and Simon (Thm S.3, p. 348) state the clause only "if neither $f$ nor $g$ are a.e. zero" and so are not exposed to this. The unit reports Folland faithfully and prints the Reed-Simon form in the very next sentence, so per `docs/contributing/source-authority.md` this is not a fault of the unit. Recorded with `node tools/discrepancy.mjs add measure.lp-spaces folland-1999 "Thm 6.2 (6.3), p. 182" ...`; no change to the unit is required.
