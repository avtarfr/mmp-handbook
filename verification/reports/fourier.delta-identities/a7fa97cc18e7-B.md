## Findings
1. [source] frontmatter, `arfken-2013` loc (line 11), and the `src` of the one-dimensional table (line 45): Arfken's Exercise 1.11.6 (p. 81) is printed as "$\frac{d}{dx}\,x\delta(x) = -\delta(x)$". Read literally that is false in $\mathcal{D}'$ — $x\delta(x)=0$, so $(d/dx)(x\delta) = 0 \ne -\delta$ — and it is almost certainly a bracketing slip for $x\,(d/dx)\delta(x)$, which is what the exercise's Gaussian-sequence hint produces. Our table states the correct identity $x\,\delta'(x) = -\delta(x)$ and the frontmatter quotes Arfken's printed form verbatim, so this is faithful reporting of the source and not a failure. Recorded with `node tools/discrepancy.mjs add fourier.delta-identities arfken-2013 "§1.11, Exercise 1.11.6, p. 81" ...`.
2. [source] Arfken's Exercise 1.11.3 (p. 80) states the Fejér limit as $\lim_n (2\pi n)^{-1}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t)[\sin(nt/2)/\sin(t/2)]^2 dt = f(0)$, over the whole line. The kernel is $2\pi$-periodic, so it concentrates at every $t = 2\pi k$ with weight $1$ and the limit over the line is $\sum_k f(2\pi k)$. Our unit gets this right — "it converges to $\delta$ on an interval of length $2\pi$ rather than on the line" — but does not say that this differs from the exercise as printed. Recorded as a discrepancy; the unit passes. Fix (optional): one clause noting that Arfken's exercise writes the limit over $\mathbb{R}$.
