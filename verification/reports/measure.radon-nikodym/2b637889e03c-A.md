## Findings
1. [minor] definition (line 42): "unique up to $\mu$-a.e. equality *provided $\mu$ and $\nu$ are $\sigma$-finite* ... without it uniqueness fails". The stated condition is sufficient but stronger than needed: $\sigma$-finiteness of $\mu$ alone already forces $\mu$-a.e. uniqueness of a non-negative density (split $X_j \cap \{f>g\} \cap \{g \le n\}$, on which $\int g \,d\mu \le n\mu(X_j) < \infty$, and let $n \to \infty$). The counterexample offered, $X=\{1\}$ with $\mu(\{1\})=\nu(\{1\})=\infty$, has *both* measures non-$\sigma$-finite, so it does not separate the two halves of the hypothesis, and the sentence reads as if both were needed. Fix: say that $\sigma$-finiteness of $\mu$ is what uniqueness needs, and that the unit assumes both because Step 9 obtains it from the partition of Step 8; or keep the hypothesis as stated but say "this hypothesis cannot simply be dropped" rather than implying necessity of each half.

2. [minor] proof, Step 1 (line 80): "$\nu \le \alpha$ as set functions, so $\int |g|\,d\nu \le \int |g|\,d\alpha$". Monotonicity of the integral in the *measure* (as opposed to the integrand) is not one of the listed prerequisites' headline statements; it follows from the simple-function definition plus monotone convergence, exactly as in Step 4. Fix: add a half-sentence ("first for simple $|g|$, then by monotone convergence"), or forward-reference the argument of Step 4.

3. [minor] proof, Step 9 (line 137): "Using the partition $\{X_j\}$ of Step 8, refined so that in addition $\rho(X_j) < \infty$". No refinement is needed: $\rho \le \nu$ and $\nu(X_j) < \infty$ by construction in Step 8, so $\rho(X_j) < \infty$ already. Fix: delete the clause and note $\rho \le \nu$ instead.

4. [minor] statement "Calculus of Radon-Nikodym derivatives" (line 141) and proof of part 2 (line 161): $\lambda$ is the singular part of $\nu$ throughout the main statement and Steps 5-9, and an unrelated third reference measure here; the cross-reference "the $\lambda$-a.e. uniqueness of a density (Step 9)" therefore uses $\lambda$ in two senses within two lines. Likewise Step 9 (line 131) calls the $\mu$-null carrier $C$, whereas Steps 5-8 and the definition block call that set $B$ and use $C$ for its complement. Both uses are locally re-declared and follow the source's own lettering, so nothing is wrong, but the reuse is a reading hazard. Fix: use a different letter (e.g. $\kappa$) for the third measure in the calculus block, and keep $B$ for the $\mu$-null carrier in Step 9.

5. [minor] application "Densities and likelihood ratios" (line 228, line 236): $X$ denotes the ambient measurable space in every other block and a real random variable here, so "$Q(X>1)$" collides with the standing notation. Fix: call the random variable $Z$, or say explicitly that $X$ is reused.

6. [minor] method, step 4 (line 180): the uniqueness clause of the Caratheodory extension theorem is invoked via :ref to measure.lebesgue-measure, which is not in `prereqs`. This matches the convention of the sibling units (measure.dominated-convergence refs measure.lebesgue-integral without listing it), so it is not a defect, but adding it would make the dependency explicit.
