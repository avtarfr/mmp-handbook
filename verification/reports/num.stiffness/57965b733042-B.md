## Findings
1. [blocking] method "Diagnosing and treating stiffness", step 3 (line 215): "If $h_{\mathrm{acc}} \gg h_{\mathrm{stab}}$, it is stiff, and the ratio is roughly $\sigma$." This contradicts the unit's own step 2 and its own example. With step 2's formulas, $h_{\mathrm{stab}} = |z_{\mathrm{edge}}|/\mu_{\max}$ and $h_{\mathrm{acc}} \sim \eta^{1/p}/\mu_{\min}$, the ratio is $h_{\mathrm{acc}}/h_{\mathrm{stab}} = \sigma\,\eta^{1/p}/|z_{\mathrm{edge}}|$, which is $\sigma$ only when $\eta^{1/p} \approx |z_{\mathrm{edge}}|$ — never, for a useful tolerance. In the unit's own example $\sigma = 1000$, while the stated accuracy and stability steps are $h_{\mathrm{acc}} \approx 0.02$ and $h_{\mathrm{stab}} = 0.002$, a ratio of $10$ — and the example says so in as many words, "The accuracy step is ten times too large". The method step therefore overstates the ratio by a factor of $100$ on the unit's own problem (step 2's formulas with $\eta = 0.01$, $p=1$, $|z_{\mathrm{edge}}|=2$ give $5$). Fix: say that the ratio is proportional to $\sigma$, with the tolerance-dependent factor $\eta^{1/p}/|z_{\mathrm{edge}}|$ in front — or state it as $h_{\mathrm{acc}}/h_{\mathrm{stab}} = \sigma\,\eta^{1/p}/|z_{\mathrm{edge}}|$ and point at the example's value of $10$.
2. [blocking] example "A system with eigenvalues $-1$ and $-1000$", third bullet (line 254): "It cost $800$ steps to get three figures that backward Euler obtained in $50$." Backward Euler in $50$ steps did not obtain three figures. From the unit's own table, forward Euler at $h = 0.00125$ ($800$ steps) returns $0.3676493967$, relative error $-6.253\times10^{-4}$, which is three correct significant figures ($0.368$); backward Euler at $h = 0.02$ ($50$ steps) returns $0.3715278821$, relative error $9.917\times10^{-3}$ — $0.372$ against the exact $0.368$, so two figures, and an error $16$ times larger. I reconfirmed both values independently ($R_{\mathrm{FE}}(-0.00125)^{800} + R_{\mathrm{FE}}(-1.25)^{800} = 0.3676493967$; $R_{\mathrm{BE}}(-0.02)^{50} = 0.3715278821$; exact $e^{-1}+e^{-1000} = 0.3678794412$). Since both methods are first order on the surviving mode, backward Euler needs about $790$ steps of its own to reach $6.3\times10^{-4}$ — its advantage here is that $50$ steps give a stable one-percent answer where forward Euler gives none, not that $50$ steps match $800$. (The trapezoidal rule at $50$ steps does reach $8.601\times10^{-5}$, which would make the sentence true if that were the method meant.) Fix: either name the trapezoidal rule, or say that backward Euler got two figures in $50$ steps where forward Euler at that step size is unusable.
