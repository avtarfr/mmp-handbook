Kramers degeneracy follows from antiunitary time-reversal symmetry with $T^2=-1$; the double cover is what makes the half-integer-$j$ sector exist, but it is not by itself the source of the degeneracy. Fix: say that the double cover supplies the half-integer representations on which Kramers' time-reversal argument then bites.
