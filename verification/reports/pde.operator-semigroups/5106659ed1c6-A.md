That is true — apply a bounded functional and use scalar Laplace uniqueness, the functionals separating points by Hahn-Banach — but neither the scalar theorem nor Hahn-Banach is a listed prerequisite, and the handbook's own `fourier.laplace-transform` does not state a uniqueness theorem.
