## Findings
1. [blocking] proof "(I5) additivity", steps 3 and 4 (lines ~113-117): the two steps ask for an object that cannot exist. Step 3 takes $W$ to be an **open simply connected** neighbourhood of $D^\ast$ inside $U\setminus Z$, and step 4 then asserts that the boundary circuit $\Gamma$ - which by its own description contains **all of $C$**, the bridges $\beta_k$ and the circles $\sigma_k$ - is a loop **in $W$**, so that step 1 makes its angle change zero. But if $C\subseteq W$ with $W$ open, simply connected and disjoint from $Z$, then $C$ is null-homotopic in $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{p_1\}$ and so has winding number $0$ about the enclosed fixed point $p_1$, whereas it has winding number $1$. So no such $W$ contains $\Gamma$. (A simply connected $W$ containing $D^\ast$ does exist - for $n=1$, $D$ the unit disc, $\sigma_1$ the circle $r=1/4$ and $\beta_1$ the segment $[1/4,1]$ on the positive axis, one may take the slit annulus $\{1/8<r<2,\ 0<\theta<2\pi\}$ - but it omits the bridge and the two points of $C$ and $\sigma_1$ that the bridge meets, which is exactly the part of $\Gamma$ the argument needs.) As written the proof therefore never justifies "the net change of the angle of $f$ along $\Gamma$ is $0$", which is the whole content of (I5); and since (I5) carries the main theorem, the gap is load-bearing. Fix (either route, both short): (a) drop $W$ and argue by homotopy instead - $\Gamma = C\cdot\prod_k(\beta_k\sigma_k^{-1}\beta_k^{-1})$ is null-homotopic in $D\setminus\{p_1,\dots,p_n\}\subseteq U\setminus Z$, hence $f\circ\Gamma$ is null-homotopic in $\mathbb R^2\setminus\{0\}$ and its winding number is $0$; or (b) keep the slit domain and apply the lifting criterion to the **cut region** itself (the compact simply connected, locally path-connected space obtained by cutting $D$ along the bridges, on which each bridge appears as two boundary arcs mapping to the same arc of the plane) rather than to an open neighbourhood - then step 5's pairwise cancellation is exact for the reason given.
2. [minor] proof "(I5) additivity", step 3 (line ~113): "leaves a compact set $D^\ast$". $D^\ast$ is $D$ with the bridges deleted, so it is not closed and not compact; in the model above it is $\{1/4\le r\le1,\ 0<\theta<2\pi\}$. Only its simple connectivity is used. Fix: delete "compact".
