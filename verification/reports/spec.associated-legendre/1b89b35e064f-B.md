## Findings
1. [blocking] remark[The sign convention, stated before anything is proved], first bullet (line 62): "Hassani's eq. (13.35) carries the same phase" misreports the source. Hassani's (13.35) is $P_l^m(u)=(-1)^{l+m}\frac{(l+m)!}{(l-m)!}\frac{(1-u^2)^{-m/2}}{2^ll!}\frac{\mathrm{d}^{l-m}}{\mathrm{d}u^{l-m}}(1-u^2)^l$, which equals $(-1)^m$ times the unit's $P_l^m$ — i.e. it is the **phase-free** convention, the same one Riley-Hobson-Bence and Strauss use. I checked this symbolically for every $0\le m\le l\le4$: Hassani agrees with the unit for even $m$ and is the negative of it for odd $m$ (Hassani's $P_1^1=+\sqrt{1-u^2}=+\sin\theta$, the unit's is $-\sin\theta$; likewise $P_2^1$, $P_3^1$, $P_3^3$, $P_4^1$, $P_4^3$). Hassani puts the $(-1)^m$ into the spherical harmonic instead, in Box 13.4.2 eq. (13.34), $Y_{lm}=(-1)^m[\frac{2l+1}{4\pi}\frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}]^{1/2}P_l^m(\cos\theta)\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{i}m\varphi}$ — structurally identical to Riley-Hobson-Bence (18.45). Hassani therefore belongs in the *second* bullet, next to Riley-Hobson-Bence and Strauss, not the first. Fix: move Hassani to the "Without the phase" bullet and cite (13.34) as the place he reinserts $(-1)^m$; then the unit's own summary sentence ("the two conventions differ by $(-1)^m$ in $P_l^m$ and agree in $Y_l^m$") covers him correctly.
2. [minor] application, "Atomic structure" (line 350): "Hassani's construction, which produces $P_{l}^{m}$ by lowering from the top state with $L_{-}$, is the cleanest way to see where the phase comes from." Given finding 1, this is misleading: the phase does not emerge from Hassani's ladder construction, which yields the phase-free $P_l^m$; it is inserted by hand in (13.34). Fix: say the ladder construction shows where the $\sqrt{(l-m)!/(l+m)!}$ normalisation comes from, and that the $(-1)^m$ is a separate convention.
3. [minor] remark[The sign convention], first bullet (line 62): "one of its effects is to alternate the sign with $m$ among the positive-$m$ functions". Arfken, Weber and Harris p. 758 say "among the positive-$m$ **spherical harmonics**". In a bullet whose subject is $P_l^m$, "functions" reads as the associated Legendre functions. Fix: keep the source's word.
4. [minor] statement, part 3 (line 77): "the series diverges at $x=\pm1$ unless the numerator vanishes for some non-negative integer $j$". Arfken p. 742 says non-negative **even** $j$, for the indicial root $k=0$, and then says the second indicial root $k=1$ "extends our present result to values of $l$ that are larger than $m$ and of opposite parity". The unit's single sentence collapses two Frobenius branches into one and so loses the parity bookkeeping, although the conclusion ($l$ an integer $\ge m$) is the same. Fix: mention the two indicial roots in half a sentence.
5. [minor] proof[Parts 4, 6 and 7], part 7 (line 265): the stated relation is asserted for $m,k\ge1$ but the Sturm-Liouville argument given covers $\lvert m\rvert\neq\lvert k\rvert$, which is Riley-Hobson-Bence (18.39). Worth saying why $m=0$ is excluded from the constant (it is $\infty$) rather than leaving the restriction unexplained.
