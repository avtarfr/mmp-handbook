1. [blocking] proof "Parts 4, 6 and 7" (line 280): the displayed eigenvalue equation for the
   orthogonality in $m$ has the wrong sign on the right-hand side. It reads
   $\frac{d}{dx}[(1-x^2)y'] + l(l+1)y = -m^2(1-x^2)^{-1}y$, which rearranges to
   $[(1-x^2)y']' + l(l+1)y + m^2 y/(1-x^2) = 0$ — not (AL). This contradicts the unit's own
   Sturm-Liouville form in the definition block (line 36), where the $m^2$ term enters with a
   minus sign, and it is false for the actual $P_l^m$: computing
   $[(1-x^2)(P_l^m)']' + l(l+1)P_l^m + m^2P_l^m/(1-x^2)$ symbolically gives
   $-6x/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ for $(l,m)=(2,1)$, $120x$ for $(3,2)$, $(3-15x^2)/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ for
   $(3,1)$ and $420x^2-60$ for $(4,2)$, while the same expression with $-m^2$ in place of
   $+m^2$ is identically zero in every case. The block states that "the family
   $\{P_l^m\}_m$ consists of eigenfunctions of" the displayed equation, so as written the claim
   is false. Riley, Hobson and Bence (p. 591) state the same reading correctly, as
   $(py')' + qy + \lambda\rho y = 0$ with $q=\ell(\ell+1)$, $\lambda=-m^2$, $\rho=(1-x^2)^{-1}$.
   Fix: change the right-hand side to $+m^{2}\frac{1}{1-x^{2}}y$. The labels that follow
   ($q=l(l+1)$, eigenvalue $-m^2$, weight $(1-x^2)^{-1}$) are then correct as printed, and
   nothing else in the block is affected — the $|m|\ne|k|$ conclusion and the constant are right.
