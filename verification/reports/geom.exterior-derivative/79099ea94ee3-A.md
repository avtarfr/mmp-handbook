## Findings
1. [blocking] definition (line 40): "Part 4 of the statement below is what makes this independent of the chart." Part 4 is the statement that $df$ is the ordinary differential on functions; it says nothing about charts and cannot establish that $\varphi^{*}(d[(\varphi^{-1})^{*}\omega])$ is chart-independent. The property that does this is **Part 5 (naturality)**, exactly as the unit's own step 5 argues ("so step 4 applies to it", step 4 being the Euclidean case of property 5), and exactly as Lee's proof of Thm 14.24 does, invoking Prop. 14.23(d) (p. 366). Fix: change "Part 4" to "Part 5", or say "naturality (part 5 below), proved in step 4 of the proof".
2. [blocking] remark, "Bump functions, in uniqueness only" (line 184): "Uniqueness of $d$ is therefore a genuinely $C^{\infty}$ statement, while existence is not." This does not follow and is false as stated. What step 6 shows is that *this proof* of uniqueness uses bump functions; it does not show that uniqueness fails without them. On $M=\mathbb{R}^{n}$ with its real-analytic structure — a real-analytic manifold — uniqueness holds with no bump functions at all: the coordinate functions $x^{i}$ and the coefficients $\omega_I$ of any analytic form are already globally defined, so expanding $\omega=\sum_I'\omega_I\,dx^{i_1}\wedge\cdots\wedge dx^{i_k}$ and applying properties 1, 2, 3 and 4 directly gives $D\omega=\sum_I' d\omega_I\wedge dx^{I}=d\omega$. Fix: say that the *argument given here* is $C^{\infty}$-specific (locality has to be proved some other way when bump functions are unavailable), and drop the claim about the statement itself.
