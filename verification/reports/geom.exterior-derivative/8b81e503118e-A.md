## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Properties 1–5", step 7: "On the left, $d(u\,dv) = du\wedge dv$ by the definition". This is not the coordinate definition, since $v$ is an arbitrary smooth function and not a coordinate function; it is step 2 (antiderivation, left factor of degree $0$) together with step 3 ($d(dv)=0$). Fix: cite steps 2 and 3 instead of "the definition".
2. [minor] statement, property 6, and example step 3: both use the determinant normalisation $(\alpha\wedge\beta)(X,Y)=\alpha(X)\beta(Y)-\alpha(Y)\beta(X)$ (no $1/2!$). This is inherited from geom.differential-forms and is the convention Lee uses, but since the invariant formula and every number in the example change under the Alt normalisation, the unit would be safer saying once that it uses the determinant convention of its prerequisite. Fix: one clause in the statement or in step 7.
3. [minor] example, step 3: "$(\nabla\times\mathbf{A})\cdot(u\times v)$ at $p$ — the circulation density of $\mathbf{A}$ through the parallelogram spanned by $u$ and $v$". A density would be per unit area, and $u\times v$ already carries the area; the quantity is the flux of $\nabla\times\mathbf{A}$ through that parallelogram, equivalently the leading term of the circulation round its boundary. Fix: "the flux of $\nabla\times\mathbf{A}$ through the parallelogram".
