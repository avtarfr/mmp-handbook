The unit states the sharp hypothesis (closed ball in $U$) and says in its own voice that this is how it reads Evans, which is the right handling. Recorded in `verification/discrepancies.md`. The bridging step — $\overline{B(x,r)}\subseteq U$ open gives an $R>r$ with $B(x,R)\subseteq U$, by compactness of $\overline{B(x,r)}$ — is never said. Fix: one clause in Step 1.
