## Findings

1. [blocking] proof, "The Bessel form" (lines 148 and 153): the matching of Hassani's
   general equation is done with the wrong sign of alpha, and the conclusion then
   swaps his two unknowns.

   The block states Hassani's scheme correctly: "the substitutions z = beta t^gamma,
   w = t^alpha u turn Bessel's equation into
   t^2 u'' + (2 alpha + 1) t u' + (beta^2 gamma^2 t^{2 gamma} + alpha^2 - nu^2 gamma^2) u = 0",
   so `u` is the new unknown and `w` is the Bessel function, i.e. u = t^{-alpha} w.
   Line 148 then reads

       2\alpha+1 = 0 \Rightarrow \alpha=\tfrac12

   but 2 alpha + 1 = 0 gives **alpha = -1/2**. With alpha = -1/2 the solution of the
   matched equation is u = t^{+1/2} J_nu(beta t^{3/2}), which is right; with the
   printed alpha = +1/2 it would be u = t^{-1/2} J_nu, which does not solve Airy's
   equation, and in any case 2 alpha + 1 = 2 != 0 so the matching condition the line
   is enforcing is violated by its own answer.

   Line 153 then writes "So **w** = t^{1/2} J_{+-1/3}(2 i t^{3/2}/3) solves Airy's
   equation", calling the Airy solution `w` — the letter the same block has just
   assigned to the Bessel function. The parenthesis compounds it: "the factor t^{1/2}
   that his own substitution w = t^alpha u introduces; with alpha = 1/2 that factor is
   sqrt(t)". Under the stated convention alpha = 1/2 gives the factor t^{-1/2}, not
   sqrt(t).

   Verified numerically at t = 1.3: f(t) = t^{1/2} J_{1/3}(2 i t^{3/2}/3) gives
   f'' = 1.3556217597 + 0.7826685879i and t f = 1.3556217597 + 0.7826685879i (equal to
   20 digits), whereas g(t) = t^{-1/2} J_{1/3}(2 i t^{3/2}/3) gives
   g'' = 0.5582716328 + 0.3223182775i against t g = 1.0427859690 + 0.6020527599i.

   Fix: print `alpha = -1/2`; call the Airy solution `u` (= t^{-alpha} w = t^{1/2} w)
   throughout the paragraph and in the parenthesis; and in the parenthesis say that
   the factor is t^{-alpha} = sqrt(t). The conclusions of the block — the sqrt(t), the
   order 1/3, the forms in statement part 3 — are all correct and need no change; only
   the two lines of bookkeeping do.
