## Findings

[blocking] remark "Where the hypotheses are used", second bullet (line 269): "Without it one must subtract $v_1(x)u_1(\xi)$ instead" is wrong — with that source the modified problem is *still* inconsistent, which is the opposite of the point being made. Consistency of $L_xg_M(x,\xi)=\delta(x-\xi)-T(x,\xi)$ requires the source to be orthogonal to the adjoint null solution $v_1$ for each fixed $\xi$, i.e. $\int_a^b T(x,\xi)v_1(x)\,dx = v_1(\xi)$. For $T=v_1(x)u_1(\xi)$ with $\int v_1^2=1$ the left side is $u_1(\xi)$, not $v_1(\xi)$. The correct subtraction has $v_1$ in the *source* slot: $T=u_1(x)v_1(\xi)$ with the biorthogonal normalisation $\int_a^b u_1v_1\,dx = 1$, or $T=v_1(x)v_1(\xi)$ with $\int v_1^2=1$.
