## Findings

1. [source] remark[Consequences], "The averaging construction": Hassani (§24.3, p. 734) introduces the same operator as "let $X$ be any operator in $L(W^{(\alpha)}, W^{(\beta)})$" and then writes $A = \sum_x T^{(\alpha)}_x X T^{(\beta)}_{x^{-1}}$. With his own convention (Lemma 24.3.1, p. 732: $A \in L(V,V')$ means $A \colon V \to V'$) that composition does not typecheck; it needs $X \colon W^{(\beta)} \to W^{(\alpha)}$, so the two carrier spaces are interchanged in the book's sentence. Our unit states the correct typing ($X \colon V_\beta \to V_\alpha$) and is right; recorded with `tools/discrepancy.mjs` against hassani-2013, §24.3, p. 734. No change needed here.

2. [minor] front matter, `prereqs` (line 7): the "Consequences" remark uses the centre $Z(G)$ and the "What the lemma does not say" remark uses the multiplicity notation $m_\lambda$; group.conjugacy-classes is `:ref`-ed for the former but is not listed in `prereqs`. Not wrong, but the list is slightly narrower than the unit's dependencies.

3. [minor] remark[Where the hypotheses are used], "Both representations must be irreducible in part 1": the sentence claims the proof "needs both", but only one direction is illustrated (the inclusion $W \hookrightarrow \mathbb{C}^3$, with the *target* reducible). The symmetric case is just as easy (the orthogonal projection $\mathbb{C}^3 \to W$ is a non-zero, non-invertible intertwiner with reducible source). Fix: add the projection in half a line, or soften to "needs the one it is applied to".
