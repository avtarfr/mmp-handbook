## Findings
1. [minor] :::idea (line 129): "The list is found by writing down the general solution of $X''=-\lambda X$ in each of the three cases $\lambda<0$, $\lambda=0$, $\lambda>0$" does not quite describe the proof that follows, which has four cases: Step 5 disposes of non-real $\lambda$, and the unit makes a point of that elsewhere ("only Strauss's route also rules out complex $\lambda$, which is what Step 5 above does", line 191). Fix: add "and the complex case, which the three real cases do not cover" to the idea.
2. [minor] :::definition (line 33): "Let $L>0$ and $c = \sqrt{\tau/\rho}>0$" introduces $\tau$ and $\rho$ without naming them. They are used again in the frequency formula (line 85), the scaling remark and the example, and Strauss writes the tension as $T$, so the reader cannot match the symbols without going to `pde.dalembert`. Fix: "$c = \sqrt{\tau/\rho}$ with $\tau$ the tension and $\rho$ the line density, as in :ref[d'Alembert's formula]".
3. [minor] :::statement (line 88): "the fact, as Strauss notes, that Euler discovered in 1749 and that creates a sensation still" puts a present-tense flourish inside an attribution. Strauss (p. 87) writes that the discovery "created a sensation"; the claim that it still does is ours. Fix: close the attribution after "1749".
