## Findings
1. [minor] definition block (line 32) against the summary (line 6): the summary says the unit "shows that a linear time-invariant operation on a signal is a convolution", but the convolution form $y = \Phi*x$ (and the discrete $y_n=\sum_k a_kx_{n-k}$) is adopted in the definition, not derived from linearity and time-invariance; what is actually shown, in part 2, is that the frequency-domain multiplication is equivalent to it, which is Arfken's route from the linear-response assumption. The representation theorem itself needs a continuity hypothesis and is not available here. Fix: soften the summary to "takes a linear time-invariant operation to be a convolution and shows the equivalent frequency-domain form", or add one sentence to the definition saying the representation is assumed.
2. [minor] statement, part 3 (line 70): "a causal filter is never zero-phase" needs the same exception as the clause just before it — the identity, and any positive multiple of it, is causal with constant real $A$. Fix: "and, apart from that same case, a causal filter is never zero-phase".
3. [minor] statement, part 4 (line 77): $g_X$ is introduced by the series $(2\pi)^{-1}\sum_n c_X(n)\mathrm{e}^{-in\lambda}$ without a hypothesis guaranteeing it converges to a density; the proof then uses $\int\lvert g_X\rvert = c_X(0)$ and the uniqueness of the spectral distribution. Fix: add $\sum_n\lvert c_X(n)\rvert<\infty$ (or "assume $X$ has a spectral density $g_X$") to the hypotheses, as the companion unit stats.periodogram does.
