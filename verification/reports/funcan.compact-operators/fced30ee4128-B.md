## Findings
1. [minor] idea (line 73): "since finite-rank operators are obviously compact (bounded sets in a finite-dimensional space are relatively compact)". `docs/STYLE_GUIDE.md` bans "obviously". The parenthesis already supplies the reason. Fix: "since finite-rank operators are compact (bounded sets in a finite-dimensional space are relatively compact)".
2. [minor] proof Parts 9 and 10 (line 130): "choose $n$ with $\sum_{i,j>n}\lvert k_{ij}\rvert^{2}<\varepsilon^{2}$". Read strictly, $i>n$ *and* $j>n$; but $K-\sum_{i,j\le n}k_{ij}e_i\bar e_j$ has coefficients over the pairs with $i>n$ *or* $j>n$, which is the larger tail, and that is the sum the bound $\lVert A-S_n\rVert\le\varepsilon$ needs. The same loose notation is in Stakgold and Holst (§6.1, p. 357, $\sum_{i,j=n+1}^{\infty}$), so this is inherited, and the choice of $n$ is still possible either way. Fix: write the tail as $\sum_{(i,j)\notin[1,n]^{2}}$ or "$\max(i,j)>n$".
3. [minor] proof Parts 5–8 (line 95): the orthonormal case of part 7 is closed by invoking the Riesz representation theorem (:ref to funcan.riesz-representation), which is not listed in `prereqs`. Fix: add it to `prereqs`, or close the step with continuity of the inner product as Stakgold and Holst do (p. 337), which the sentence already mentions.
4. [minor] remark (line 134): "Hassani makes the point in a footnote." In Hassani (2013) p. 524 the point ("linear transformations of finite-dimensional vector spaces are compact") is a margin note beside Proposition 17.5.5, not a footnote. Fix: "in a margin note beside Proposition 17.5.5".
