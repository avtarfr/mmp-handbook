## Findings

1. [minor] remark, fifth bullet (line ~150): "We follow Ahlfors, whose version is the stronger one". True of the hypothesis on the region — Ahlfors asks only for a region, Arfken and Hassani for a simply connected one — but Hassani's Theorem 10.5.8 (p. 319) also asks only that $\oint_C f = 0$ for every **simple** closed contour, which is a weaker hypothesis than our "every closed contour", so his version is not uniformly weaker than ours. The following sentence, "On a simply connected region the three statements coincide", is fine (given analyticity each hypothesis implies the others). Fix: say "stronger in its hypothesis on $\Omega$", and note that Hassani needs only simple contours.

2. [minor] remark, second bullet: "On the contour the integral is not even absolutely convergent as written." True whenever $f(z_0)\ne0$ (the arc-length integral behaves like $\int ds/s$), but false if $f$ vanishes at $z_0$ to first order. Fix: "not in general absolutely convergent".

3. [minor] `statement` consequence 4 (Morera) is stated for "a region $\Omega$", and the Morera `proof` uses part 4 of `complex.contour-integrals`, whose (a)$\Rightarrow$(c) step is proved component by component. Consistent, but since consequence 4 says "region" while the cited part 4 is stated for an open set, a half-sentence linking the two would spare the reader a cross-check.
