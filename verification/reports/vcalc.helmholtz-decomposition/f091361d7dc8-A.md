## Findings
1. [minor] proof "Helmholtz's theorem", Step 3 ("so it dies as $R \to \infty$ only if
   $\mathbf{c} = o(1/r)$") and remark, third bullet ("So it tends to zero if and only if
   $\mathbf{c} = o(1/r)$" and "The correct condition for Step 3 in general is therefore
   $\mathbf{c} = o(1/r)$"): the *bound* $8\pi R\max_{r_2=R}|\mathbf c|$ tends to zero exactly when
   $\mathbf c = o(1/r)$, but the surface term itself can vanish without that. Take $\mathbf c$
   azimuthal, so $\mathbf c\cdot d\mathbf S_2 \equiv 0$ on every sphere, with magnitude $\sim 1/r$ on
   a support whose solid angle shrinks like $r^{-1-\varepsilon}$ (which keeps $\mathbf A$
   convergent): the term is identically zero while $\max_{r_2=R}|\mathbf c| \sim 1/R$. So only
   sufficiency is established, and only sufficiency is needed. In Step 3 the sentence even
   distinguishes "it" from "the bound" within one sentence, which makes the necessity claim explicit.
   Fix: attach the "only if" to the bound -- "the bound dies only if $\mathbf c = o(1/r)$, so
   $\mathbf c = o(1/r)$ is the condition under which this route to Step 3 is available" -- and change
   "if and only if" to "if" in the remark.
2. [minor] statement "Scalar and vector potentials" and proof "Helmholtz's theorem", Step 2: the two
   vanishing identities and the curl-curl identity $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf A) =
   \nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf A) - \nabla^2\mathbf A$ are cited only to RHB Eqs. (10.37)-(10.38) and
   (10.41). The handbook already holds all three, with the $C^2$ hypothesis and the same Cartesian
   caveat, in vcalc.vector-identities, which is neither `:ref`-ed nor in `prereqs`. They are standard
   undergraduate vector calculus and are correctly sourced, so this is not a prerequisite gap in the
   blocking sense, but every other result the proofs lean on is pointed at its unit. Fix: add
   vcalc.vector-identities to `prereqs` and `:ref` it at Step 2 and in the opening display.
