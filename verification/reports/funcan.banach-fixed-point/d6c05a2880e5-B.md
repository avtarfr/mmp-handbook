## Findings
1. [minor] :::proof[Parts 3, 4 and 5], Part 3 (line ~173): "Put $n=m$ and $m=0$ in the estimate $(\ast)$". The substitution as written is self-cancelling and does not produce the display that follows, which is the $m=0$ case of the *pre-$(\ast)$* estimate $d(x_m,x_n)\le\alpha^m\frac{1-\alpha^{n-m}}{1-\alpha}d(x_0,x_1)$ of step 3, with the index $n$ left as $n$. (The display itself is right: $m=0$ gives $d(x_0,x_n)\le\frac{1-\alpha^n}{1-\alpha}d(x_0,x_1)$.) The wording is a leftover from Kreyszig's "put $m=0$ in (4), change $n$ to $m$", which fits his indexing and not ours. Fix: "Put $m=0$ in the estimate of step 3".
2. [minor] :::proof[Parts 3, 4 and 5], Part 3 (line ~175): "$T|_Y : Y \to Y$ — which does map $Y$ into $Y$, by what we have just shown applied to any starting point of $Y$". The induction just given uses the hypothesis $d(x_0,Tx_0)<(1-\alpha)r$ at the specific point $x_0$, which is not assumed at an arbitrary $y\in Y$, so it does not transfer. The conclusion $T(Y)\subseteq Y$ is nevertheless true, by a one-line estimate the proof does not give: for $x\in Y$, $d(Tx,x_0)\le d(Tx,Tx_0)+d(Tx_0,x_0)<\alpha r+(1-\alpha)r=r$. Fix: replace the parenthetical with that estimate.
3. [minor] :::application, "Nonlinear partial differential equations" (line ~235): "which is also why the method gives short-time existence and not global existence". Evans' Theorem 2 (p. 503) gives a unique weak solution on the whole of an arbitrary $[0,T]$: his $T_1$ depends only on the Lipschitz constant of $\mathbf f$, so the restart the unit has just described runs to the end of any fixed interval (p. 504, step 3). The clause therefore contradicts both Evans' theorem and the unit's own preceding sentence. It is true of contraction arguments in general, where the nonlinearity is only locally Lipschitz. Fix: "which is why the method is a short-time argument in general, and gives all of $[0,T]$ here only because $\mathbf f$ is globally Lipschitz, as Evans notes on p. 505".
