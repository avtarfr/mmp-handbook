## Findings

1. [minor] application, "Nonlinear partial differential equations" (line 246): "with the Lipschitz constant of $\mathbf{f}$ times $T$ playing the role of $\alpha$". Evans' contraction constant is $\gamma=(CT)^{1/2}$ (p. 505, eq. (14)) — a square root, with $C$ absorbing the Lipschitz constant through a Gronwall-type estimate, not a product $\mathrm{Lip}(\mathbf{f})\cdot T$. The sentence's point (small $T$ is what buys the contraction, and the Lipschitz constant of $\mathbf{f}$ is what sets the scale) is right, and the two statements the paragraph attributes to Evans by name — that $T_1$ depends only on the Lipschitz constant of $\mathbf{f}$, and the blow-up interpretation — are both on p. 505 verbatim in substance. Fix: "with $(CT)^{1/2}$, $C$ set by the Lipschitz constant of $\mathbf{f}$, playing the role of $\alpha$".

2. [minor] statement parts 4 and 5 (lines 76, 78) and the proof of part 4 (line 148) use "Banach space" with no definition, no `:ref`, and no prerequisite. `funcan.normed-banach-spaces` comes *after* this unit in content/map.yaml, and the map gives this unit the single prerequisite `funcan.metric-spaces`, so the term is genuinely undischarged for a reader following the order. The mathematics is unaffected — parts 4 and 5 only ever use that a closed subset of a complete normed space is a complete metric space. Fix: one clause ("a complete normed space; see :ref[normed and Banach spaces]{to=funcan.normed-banach-spaces}"), or state parts 4 and 5 for a closed subset of a complete metric space, which is all the proof needs.

3. [minor] application, "Integral equations" (line 244): "the iterates are precisely the partial sums of the Neumann series" holds for the particular starting function $y_0=f$ — which is how :ref[green.neumann-series] proves it (its step 4, "Take $y_0=f$") — whereas the theorem above allows any start. Fix: "started from $y_0=g$, the iterates are precisely the partial sums".
