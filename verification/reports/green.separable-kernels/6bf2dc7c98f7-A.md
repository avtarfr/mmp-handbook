## Findings
1. [minor] remark, "Each of the two families independent" (line 167): "Parts 1 and 3 are not disturbed by a padded representation" is loose. With a padded representation the *bijectivity* clause of part 1 fails outright — $c\mapsto f+\lambda\sum_jc_j\phi_j$ is no longer injective — and that clause contains no $n$, so it is not covered by the following "every statement in which the number $n$ appears". The sentence is rescued only by its own "one simply thins it first". Fix: say that with a padded representation the correspondence of part 1 is still onto the solution set but no longer one-to-one, and that thinning restores it.
2. Fix: either drop the "only for" clause or mark it as an aside ("constant for a disc or a ball, and in general not").
3. [minor] front matter, `kreyszig-1978` role (line 29): the role reads "the form of the second-kind Fredholm equation on C[a, b] used here", but the unit poses $(\ast)$ on $L^{2}(a,b)$, not on $C[a,b]$; only the algebraic shape $x-\mu\int k x=v$ of Kreyszig's eq.
