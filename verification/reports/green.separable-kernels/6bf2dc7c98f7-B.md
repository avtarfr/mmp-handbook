## Findings
1. [minor] remark, "Each of the two families independent" (line ~170): "every statement in which the number $n$ appears is about the thinned representation and would be false for a padded one" is too strong. With a padded representation of size $n'>n$ the statements do not become false, only weaker — "at most $n'$ characteristic values" still holds, and so does "the rank is at most $n'$". What genuinely fails for a padded representation is the *exactness*: rank $=n$, and codimension $=n$ for $\ker\mathcal{K}$. Fix: say "would no longer be sharp" and name the two exact clauses.

  - The Birman–Schwinger paragraph, worked through independently. For $V\le0$: $(H_0-E)\psi=|V|\psi$, $\varphi=|V|^{1/2}\psi$ gives $\varphi=|V|^{1/2}(H_0-E)^{-1}|V|^{1/2}\varphi$, rank $\le n$, monotonicity in $E$ caps the count at $n$. For general sign: $V=U|V|$, $U=\operatorname{sgn}V$, and $H\psi=E\psi$ rearranges to $\varphi=-|V|^{1/2}(H_0-E)^{-1}U|V|^{1/2}\varphi$ — the unit's display, and $U=-\mathrm I$ on $\operatorname{ran}|V|$ exactly when $V\le0$, which recovers the standard operator with the right sign. The unit's own caveat is also right: the rank bound alone caps multiplicity at a fixed energy, not the total, because the general-sign operator is not self-adjoint and not monotone in $E$. The repair given is valid: $V=V_++V_-$, $\operatorname{rank}V_-\le n$, $V\ge V_-$ as forms, min–max gives $N(H_0+V)\le N(H_0+V_-)\le n$. No error found here.
