## Findings
1. [blocking] application, second paragraph (line 188): "The three two-fold axes through the edge midpoints of a square-planar molecule are one class; the two through the ligands are another." A square-planar $\mathrm{ML}_4$ has point group $D_{4h}$ of order $16$, whose in-plane two-fold axes are **two** $C_2'$ along the M–L bonds and **two** $C_2''$ through the edge midpoints — the classes are $2C_2'$ and $2C_2''$. There is no class of three two-fold axes, and a square has only four in-plane two-fold axes in all, not five. The error also contradicts this unit's own part 2: a class of size $3$ is impossible in a group of order $16$, since $3 \nmid 16$. Fix: write "The two two-fold axes through the edge midpoints ... are one class; the two through the ligands are another", and optionally name the group $D_{4h}$ and its classes $2C_2''$ and $2C_2'$ so the reader can check the count against $|D_{4h}| = 16$.
