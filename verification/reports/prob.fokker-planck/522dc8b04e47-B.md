[minor] method[Finding a stationary density], step 2 (line 235): the argument that the flux constant $c$ must be zero is given "for bounded $a$ and $b$", but the method's own check in step 5 is the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process, whose drift $a(y) = -\beta y$ is unbounded, so the stated justification does not cover the case the recipe immediately applies it to. (The conclusion $c = 0$ is still correct there: the second solution of $\tfrac12\sigma^2 p' = -\beta y p - c$ behaves like $\sigma^2/(2\beta y)$ at infinity, which is not integrable.) Fix: state the hypothesis as "$ap \to 0$ and $bp \to 0$ at $\pm\infty$", which is what the argument actually needs and which holds for the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process.

The relabelling is sensible, since $a$ and $b$ are already in use, but it is silent. Fix: add half a clause saying the source's letters have been changed to avoid the clash.
3. [minor] statement (line 62) and the automated warning: `check` reports that the cited Grimmett & Stirzaker pages never mention "Fokker" or "Planck". That is correct and is exactly what the statement block already explains, attributing the name to the physics and PDE literature and to Evans p. 4. No change needed; recorded here only so the warning is seen to have been judged.
