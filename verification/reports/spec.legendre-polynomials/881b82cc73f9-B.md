## Findings
1. [blocking] remark[Where the hypotheses are used], "Regularity at $x=\pm1$" (line 247): "the second solution $Q_{0}=\tfrac12\ln\frac{1+x}{1-x}$ is not even square integrable on $(-1,1)$" is false. $Q_0=\operatorname{artanh}x$ has only a logarithmic singularity at $x=\pm1$, and $\int_{-1}^{1}Q_0(x)^2\,\mathrm{d}x=\pi^2/6=1.6449340668\ldots$ (computed to 20 digits with mpmath; the closed form is standard). Every Legendre function of the second kind is in $L^2(-1,1)$ for the same reason. This is an `original` block, so it is our own material and it is wrong. The surrounding claim — that orthogonality fails without regularity — is right, but for a different reason: $(1-x^2)Q_0'(x)\equiv1$, so the boundary term $[(1-x^2)P_kQ_0']_{-1}^{1}=P_k(1)-P_k(-1)$ survives, and indeed $\int_{-1}^{1}P_1Q_0\,\mathrm{d}x=1\neq0$ (verified numerically) while $\int_{-1}^{1}P_0Q_0=\int_{-1}^{1}P_2Q_0=0$. Fix: replace the square-integrability claim with the surviving-boundary-term reason and the concrete failure $\int_{-1}^{1}P_1Q_0=1$; $Q_0$ is square integrable but not in the domain on which the Sturm-Liouville operator is symmetric.
2. [minor] proof[Generating function and recurrences], Step 4 (line 165): "Adding (b) to (a) removes $Q_{l-1}'$" does not do what it says. Written as displayed, (a) is $Q_l=Q_{l+1}'-2xQ_l'+Q_{l-1}'$ and (b) is $xQ_l'-Q_{l-1}'=lQ_l$; adding the two equations leaves $Q_{l-1}'$ with coefficient $-2$. What produces (c) is substituting $Q_{l-1}'=xQ_l'-lQ_l$ from (b) into (a) (equivalently, subtracting (b) from (a) after moving both to the form $\cdots=0$). The displayed result (c) is correct and is Riley-Hobson-Bence (18.20). Fix: "Substituting (b) into (a) removes $Q_{l-1}'$".
3. [minor] method, step 1 (line 261): "the recurrence is stable upward for $\lvert x\rvert\le1$ because all three coefficients are positive and bounded" is not a correct reason. The $P_{l-1}$ term enters with a minus sign, and $(2l+1)x/(l+1)$ is negative for $x<0$. (The conclusion — upward recursion is the right way to evaluate $P_l$ — is correct; it is the stated justification that fails.) Fix: give the real reason, that $P_l$ is the dominant rather than the minimal solution of the recurrence, or drop the parenthetical.
4. [minor] frontmatter, rhb-2006 `loc` (line 11): "the recurrence relations (18.18)–(18.27), pp. 586". Eqs. (18.18)–(18.21) are on printed p. 585; only (18.23)–(18.27) are on p. 586. The `pages` range 577-586 is right, so nothing is unreachable. Fix: "pp. 585–586".
5. [minor] statement, part 2 (line 72): the generating function is asserted for $\lvert x\rvert\le1$, and the block's `src` lists strauss-2008 eq. (8), which states $\lvert z\rvert<1$. The wider range is correct (and Step 6 needs $x=1$), and Riley-Hobson-Bence's §18.1.2 uses $x=1$ too, so this is not an error; but the closed endpoint is the unit's, not Strauss's.
