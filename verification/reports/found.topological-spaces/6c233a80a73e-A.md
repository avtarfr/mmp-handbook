## Findings
1. [blocking] application (line 282): "Both bounds are attained at the antipodal pair, where $d_a = \pi = 3.14159\ldots$ and $d_c = 2$" is false for the upper bound. The two bounds are $\frac{2}{\pi}d_a \le d_c \le d_a$. At the antipodal pair $d_c = 2\sin(\pi/2) = 2$ while $d_a = \pi = 3.14159\ldots$, so $d_c \le d_a$ is *strict* there - the numbers the sentence itself quotes contradict it. Only the lower bound is attained at the antipodal pair, which is exactly what the "ratio being $2/\pi$" clause that follows demonstrates; the upper bound is sharp only in the limit $d_a \to 0$, where $d_c/d_a = 2\sin(\theta/2)/\theta \to 1$ and is never reached for $\theta > 0$. This is an `original` block, so the claim is ours and must be right. Fix: "The lower bound is attained at the antipodal pair, where $d_a = \pi = 3.14159\ldots$ and $d_c = 2$, the ratio being $2/\pi = 0.63662\ldots$; the upper bound is sharp only as $d_a \to 0$, where the ratio tends to $1$."
