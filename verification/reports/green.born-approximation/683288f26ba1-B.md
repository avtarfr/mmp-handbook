## Findings

1. [blocking] note "Conventions" (lines 24-45), statement (line 112), method step 3-4 (lines 196-201), application (lines 348-364): the unit writes $\widetilde{U}(\mathbf q) = \int e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}U(\mathbf r)\,\mathrm{d}^3r$, the **unnormalised** Fourier transform, using the tilde symbol that this handbook reserves for the **symmetric** transform $\tilde f(\mathbf k) = (2\pi)^{-n/2}\int f e^{-i\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf r}\mathrm{d}^n r$ of its own listed prerequisite `fourier.fourier-transform-rn` (that unit's definition block, "this unit uses throughout the symmetric convention"; `pde.radon-transform` line 170 states the reservation explicitly: "we avoid the tilde for the Radon transform because this handbook already uses it for the Fourier transform on $\mathbb{R}^n$"). The two differ by $(2\pi)^{3/2} = 15.749$. The Conventions note fixes three conventions and declares that "mixing them silently is the one thing that must not be done", but does not fix this one, and STYLE_GUIDE.md ("Notation") requires the Fourier convention to be stated whenever it differs. The clash is then made load-bearing twice by internal citation: method step 3 (line 200) attributes $\widetilde U(q) = (4\pi/q)\int_0^\infty rU(r)\sin(qr)\,\mathrm{d}r$ to `fourier.fourier-transform-rn`, whose radial reduction is $\tilde f(k) = (2\pi)^{-3/2}(4\pi/k)\int_0^\infty rf\sin(kr)\,\mathrm{d}r$; and the application (line 364) attributes the Yukawa result to the same unit, which quotes it as $4\pi(2\pi)^{-3/2}/(k^2+\alpha^2)$. A reader who follows either reference and feeds the result into step 4, $f_{\mathrm B} = -\widetilde U(q)/4\pi$, gets an amplitude too small by $15.749$ and a cross-section too small by $248.0$. The unit's own numbers are internally consistent and correct; the defect is the undeclared renormalisation of a prerequisite's symbol. Fix: add a fourth paragraph to the Conventions note saying that $\widetilde U$ here is the unnormalised transform $\int e^{-i\mathbf q\cdot\mathbf r}U\,\mathrm{d}^3 r = (2\pi)^{3/2}\tilde U$ in the convention of `fourier.fourier-transform-rn`, and insert that factor where the two refs to that unit are made (lines 200, 364) — or rename the object, e.g. $\mathcal{F}U$ or $\widehat U$.
