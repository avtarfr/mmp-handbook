1. [minor] :::remark[Where the hypotheses are used] (line 200): the quotation attributed to
   Reed and Simon, `which "in general is false"`, is not verbatim. Page 156 reads "Suppose,
   again for simplicity, that $A(s,0)\to0$ at infinity. In general, this is false and one must
   modify the argument below by what is known as making subtractions." The substance is
   reported correctly; only the quoted fragment has been contracted.
   Fix: quote "In general, this is false", or drop the quotation marks.

2. [minor] :::statement[Causality gives analyticity in the upper half-plane], claim 4 (line 60),
   with the remark at line 198. Claim 4's stated corollary is "hence Lipschitz on compact
   subsets of the real axis", but Step 3 of the dispersion-relation proof needs $f(z)=f(x_0)
   +O(|z-x_0|)$ for $z$ on a small semicircle *in the upper half-plane*, i.e. Lipschitz on a
   two-dimensional neighbourhood. The needed fact is there -- the first half of claim 4 gives
   $|\tilde\chi'|\le\int_0^\infty\tau|\chi|$ uniformly on $\overline H$, hence a global
   Lipschitz bound on $\overline H$ -- but the corollary the remark points at is the weaker
   one-dimensional statement, so the chain reads as if it had a gap it does not have.
   Fix: in claim 4 write "hence Lipschitz on $\overline H$" (or "on compact subsets of
   $\overline H$").

3. [minor] :::example (line 239): "Work with $\omega_0 = 1$ and $\gamma = 2/5$, in units where
   $\omega_0=1$" states the same normalisation twice.
   Fix: "Work in units with $\omega_0=1$, and take $\gamma=2/5$."
