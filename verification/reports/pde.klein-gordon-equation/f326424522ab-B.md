[source] remark, "The square-root equation" (line ~327): the displayed identity $\bigl(c^{-1}\gamma^0\partial_t+\gamma^1\partial_x+\gamma^2\partial_y+\gamma^3\partial_z+iM\bigr)^2 = c^{-2}\partial_t^2-\Delta+M^2$ is false as a statement about the *square*: writing $A$ for the first-order part, Strauss's own properties (4)–(6) give $A^2=c^{-2}\partial_t^2-\Delta$, so $(A+iM)^2=A^2+2iMA-M^2$ — the cross term does not vanish and the mass term has the wrong sign. But this is exactly what Strauss displays on p. 374 (and sets as his Exercise 2), and the unit attributes it to him, so by `docs/contributing/source-authority.md` it does not fail the unit. Recorded: `verification/discrepancies.md` (pde.klein-gordon-equation / strauss-2008 / p. 374). Suggested (non-blocking) improvement: display the product form and add one clause saying Strauss writes it as a square.
