## Findings
1. [minor] statement "Plane waves and the dispersion relation", item 1 (line 71): "At $k = 0$ the phase velocity is undefined, since $\omega/k$ diverges" conflates two things — at $k = 0$ exactly, $\omega/k = \mu/0$ is undefined because the denominator is zero; what diverges is the limit as $k \downarrow 0$. Fix: "is undefined, and $\omega/k \to \infty$ as $k \downarrow 0$". Also, the displayed inequalities are stated for $k > 0$ but tacitly for the positive root $\omega = +\sqrt{c^2k^2+\mu^2}$ (as the proof's step 2 says explicitly); the display does not say so, although item 3 immediately afterwards insists both signs occur. Fix: write "for the positive root and $k = \lvert\mathbf{k}\rvert > 0$" in the lead-in.

2. [minor] statement, item 1, last sentence (line 71): "For $\mu = 0$ both equal $c$" steps outside the standing hypothesis of the definition block, where $\mu$ is a positive constant (the strict inequalities just displayed are false at $\mu = 0$). It reads as a limiting aside and is correct as such, but the switch is not signalled. Fix: "In the massless limit $\mu \to 0$ both tend to $c$".

3. [minor] second idea block (line 155): "the Bessel profile is a function of $t^2-x^2/c^2$, which vanishes on the cone to exactly the order that kills the $\delta$ term" can be read as saying the profile vanishes on the cone, which it does not — $g = \tfrac12 J_0(0) = \tfrac12$ there. What vanishes to first order is $\phi_t g_t - \phi_x g_x = 2\phi G'$, as step 4 shows. Fix: "the Bessel profile is a function of $t^2 - x^2/c^2 = \phi\,(t+\lvert x\rvert)$, so its derivative along the cone's normal vanishes there to exactly the order that kills the $\delta$ term".

4. [minor] second idea block (line 155) and proof step 5 (line 248): "those follow from the shrinking support alone" and "Everything needed is the shrinking support ... and the bound $\lvert S\rvert \le \tfrac12$" overstate it — step 5 also computes $\int_{-t}^{t} S \,dx = \sin(\mu t)/\mu$ term by term from the $J_0$ series and the beta integral, which is more than support and a bound. Fix: say "the shrinking support, the bound $\lvert S\rvert \le \tfrac12$, and the exact mass $\int S\,dx = \sin(\mu t)/\mu$".

5. [minor] proof of the source function, step 5 (line 262): the test function is called $\varphi$, which is already the symbol for the initial datum $u(x,0) = \varphi(x)$ in the statement block two blocks above. Fix: use $\chi$ or $\eta$ for the test function.

6. [minor] frontmatter, `prereqs` (line 7): `var.noether-theorem`, `pde.wave-three-dimensions` and `pde.laplace-fundamental-solution` are `:ref`-ed in the remark and application blocks but not listed. No proof depends on them (they are signposts, not steps), so this is presentation only. Separately, `var.several-independent-variables` sits after this unit in the map — `npm run check` warns about it; it is a pre-existing map-ordering warning, not an error in the text.

7. [source] remark, "The square-root equation" (lines 386–394), against `strauss-2008` p. 374: Strauss displays the identity as a square, $(c^{-1}\gamma^0\partial_t + \gamma^j\partial_j + im)^2 = c^{-2}\partial_t^2 - \Delta + m^2$. Read as a square it is false — it leaves the cross term $2im\mathcal{D}$ and gives $-m^2$; the identity holds for the product $(\mathcal{D}+im)(\mathcal{D}-im)$. Our unit prints the product form, states that Strauss displays it as a square, and says why the square cannot be meant, so it reports the source faithfully. Already on record in `verification/discrepancies.md`; not re-added.

8. [source] remark, first bullet (line 382), against `strauss-2008` §13.5, Exercise 9, p. 379: the exercise prints "show that $u_{tt} - c^2u_{xx} - \tfrac14 u = 0$" after substituting $u = e^{-t/2}v$; the equation obtained is the one for $v$, as the exercise's own next sentence (Klein-Gordon with imaginary mass $m = i/2$) requires. Our unit states it in $v$ and flags the misprint in a parenthesis. Already on record; not re-added.

9. [source] remark, "The square-root equation" (line 390), against `strauss-2008` Eq. (1) p. 373 vs. p. 374: Strauss uses the same symbol $m$ for two constants that differ by a factor $c$ (inverse time in Eq. (1), inverse length in the Dirac identity). Our unit names the difference explicitly. Already on record; not re-added.
